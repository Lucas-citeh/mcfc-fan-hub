10-Man City Stun United 1-0: Haaland Seals Controversial Derby Win

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 13 September 2026

Erling Haaland's 60th-minute finish — controversially awarded by VAR after Enzo Fernández was flagged offside but adjudged not to be interfering with play — gave 10-man Manchester City a 1-0 victory over Manchester United at Old Trafford on Sunday. Phil Foden's red card after just 23 minutes, for kicking out at United captain Bruno Fernandes, had made City's task look impossible. Gianluigi Donnarumma and the defensive unit had other ideas.

Foden's Red Card: The Match Is Turned Upside Down

The 199th Manchester derby began at a furious pace, and it was Phil Foden who forced its decisive early twist. In the 23rd minute, after an altercation with Bruno Fernandes, Foden outstretched his legs and caught the United captain in the lower abdomen with his studs. Referee Michael Oliver produced a straight red card; VAR confirmed the decision. Manchester City would need to defend for over an hour with ten men against a raucous Old Trafford crowd.

Enzo Maresca reorganised quickly. Elliot Anderson, who had started alongside Enzo Fernández in a double pivot, was tasked with doing the work of two men as City dropped into a defensive 4-4-1. It was Anderson who would be named Premier League Player of the Match, recording 76 touches — more than any other player on the pitch — as his energy and intelligence off the ball proved crucial in keeping United at arm's length.

Haaland — and Controversy

United had the lion's share of possession and created the clearer first-half chances with City reduced. Marcus Rashford rattled the woodwork before the break; Kobbie Mainoo did the same in the second half. But City — organised, combative, and dangerous on the counter — kept their shape.

Then came the goal that settled the 199th Manchester derby. In the 60th minute, a City move broke quickly down the right. The ball was played into the area and Haaland met it to score — but referee Oliver had raised his flag for offside on Enzo Fernández, who had been in an advanced position as the cross came in. After a VAR review lasting over two minutes, the decision came back: Fernández was judged not to be actively interfering with play. The goal stood. Old Trafford erupted in anger; the City end erupted in ecstasy.

Donnarumma Keeps the Door Shut

The final half hour tested City's defensive resolve to the limit. Gianluigi Donnarumma was required to produce a stunning late save to deny Bryan Mbeumo, who had been introduced to add United's attacking threat. The Italian — one of the best goalkeepers in the world — was immovable when it mattered most. United also hit the woodwork a second time through Mainoo, but the goal would not come.

When Michael Oliver blew the final whistle, City had done what seemed unthinkable after 23 minutes: won the Manchester derby with ten men in front of a full house at Old Trafford.

Implications: Perfect Start, and a Foden Cloud

Manchester City go to the top of the Premier League on 12 points — four wins from four, a goal difference of +6, and the best possible start to the Enzo Maresca era. Haaland now has four Premier League goals and six in all competitions. Donnarumma has kept back-to-back Premier League clean sheets.

The shadow over the result is Phil Foden's three-match suspension for violent conduct. The England midfielder will miss the Carabao Cup third-round tie at home to Norwich City on Thursday 17 September, the Premier League visit of Sunderland on Sunday 20 September, and — most painfully — the trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on Sunday 11 October after the international break. Maresca will need to find solutions without one of his most creative players across what is shaping up as the most important week of the season so far.

Next up: Norwich City at home in the Carabao Cup on Thursday, then Sunderland at the Etihad on Sunday. The Foden-less City will need to show their squad depth.
