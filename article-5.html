Doku Confirmed Out for Coventry; Bouaddi Pushing for a Start

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 3 September 2026

Manchester City will be without Jeremy Doku when they face newly promoted Coventry City at the Etihad on Saturday 5 September (15:00 BST). The Belgian winger has been confirmed absent for a third successive match, while Ayyoub Bouaddi is pushing for his first Etihad start.

Doku's Calf Injury: The Full Picture

Jeremy Doku sustained a calf injury during the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal at the Principality Stadium on 16 August, forcing him off at half-time. Enzo Maresca initially described the concern as a "two to three week" issue, but the 24-year-old Belgian has not featured in either of City's Premier League victories since — the 2-1 comeback against Bournemouth on Matchday 1 or the commanding 4-1 win at Crystal Palace on Matchday 2.

Maresca confirmed in his pre-match briefing that Doku will not be available for Saturday's fixture. The manager has not placed a firm return date on the forward's comeback, with the first Manchester derby at Old Trafford on 12 September now the most realistic target for the winger's return to the matchday squad.

What Doku's Absence Means for the XI

In Doku's absence, Maresca has shown tactical flexibility on the left flank. Phil Foden operated there in the first two league games, with Antoine Semenyo on the right. The Belgian's direct, one-versus-one style is difficult to replicate, but City's depth means alternatives exist — Semenyo can switch flanks, Grealish is an option, and Jeremy Monga provides a young wildcard on either wing.

The Coventry game also represents an opportunity to manage minutes across a squad that has several players accumulating games quickly after their World Cup returns. Rayan Cherki has started both league matches following his summer break; rotating resources against a limited Coventry side makes tactical sense ahead of the derby.

Bouaddi: Ready for His Etihad Moment?

Ayyoub Bouaddi made his Manchester City debut as a substitute in the 82nd minute of the Crystal Palace win at Selhurst Park, replacing Elliot Anderson as City sought to close out a comfortable victory. The 18-year-old Moroccan, signed from Lille for €100m on 25 August, had less than ten minutes to make an impression — but enough for City supporters to see why the club spent a Premier League record on a teenager.

A home fixture against a newly promoted side with zero points from their opening two games is an ideal environment for Bouaddi to get a longer audition. Maresca has spoken warmly of his composure in training, and the temptation to hand him a first start at the Etihad — where the crowd, rather than a hostile away end, will be on his side — is clear.

Coventry: How Much of a Test?

Frank Lampard's Coventry City are back in the top flight for the first time in 25 years and have found the step up predictably steep. Defeats in their first two matches have left them bottom of the early table, without a goal scored and with a goal difference of -4. Injuries to Haji Wright, Kaine Kesler-Hayden and Luke Woolfenden further restrict their options.

On paper, this is as close to a banker as the Premier League offers. City's concern will be complacency after the intensity of the Palace performance, and ensuring the squad stays sharp heading into the derby week that follows.

City's Perfect Start

Six points from six — top of the Premier League table after two Matchdays. Erling Haaland has two goals, Rayan Cherki has two goals (including his first City brace at Selhurst Park), Marc Guehi has one and Josko Gvardiol has one. Maresca's City have conceded twice — Tavernier for Bournemouth and an own goal at Palace — and have looked increasingly fluent with each game.

Saturday offers the chance to make it nine points from nine, and to build momentum before the biggest early-season test: the Manchester derby at Old Trafford on 12 September.
