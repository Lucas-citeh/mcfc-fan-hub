Derby Preview: City's Perfect Start Meets the Ultimate Test at Old Trafford

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 11 September 2026

With 48 hours to go until the 199th Manchester derby, Enzo Maresca has given his clearest injury update yet on Jeremy Doku and Nico O'Reilly — while Manchester City prepare to carry a perfect Premier League record and a Champions League win at Porto into the cauldron of Old Trafford.

Maresca's Latest Injury Verdict

Speaking at his pre-match press conference, Maresca was cautiously optimistic about the availability of two of City's key absentees for Sunday's fixture. On Nico O'Reilly, who dropped out of the matchday squad for the Coventry win on 5 September after a warmup issue and subsequently missed the 2-0 UCL victory at Porto, the City head coach gave his most positive assessment yet: "Nico probably yes — but we need to wait." The 20-year-old has been a key component of Maresca's 4-1-4-1 structure when fit.

On Jeremy Doku, Maresca was more measured: "Jeremy, I'm not sure yet. We need to wait." The Belgian winger has been sidelined since the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal on 16 August with a calf complaint, missing all four competitive matches since. His return would add a different dimension to City's wide play, and his one-versus-one ability would be a significant asset against United's full-backs in the biggest fixture of the season so far. Per Cityxtra / Yahoo Sports (10 September 2026).

City's Form: Five Games, Four Wins

Since the Community Shield, City have won four of five competitive fixtures — the exception being only that 3-0 defeat to Arsenal in Cardiff that now looks increasingly like a blip on a machine in fine working order. Three consecutive Premier League wins have produced seven goals, two conceded, and nine points — enough for top spot in the table. Tuesday's 2-0 triumph at the Estádio do Dragão, with Erling Haaland scoring twice including a stoppage-time cutback finish from Rayan Ait-Nouri, showed that Maresca's City are as capable in Europe as they are in the league.

Haaland now has five goals in all competitions in 2026/27. Rayan Cherki has two. The spine of the team — Donnarumma, Dias, Guehi, Gvardiol, Anderson, Fernández — is beginning to look formidably settled for a side only 12 weeks into a new managerial era. Bouaddi continues to grow in confidence from the bench.

United's Position

Manchester United host this derby with four points from three Premier League matches — a record of one win, one draw and one defeat that leaves them in the lower half of the table. Old Trafford remains a genuinely difficult venue, however, and the atmosphere of a Manchester derby has historically been capable of elevating United beyond what their league form might suggest. City will need to be at their best.

A City win would take Maresca's side to 12 points from 12 — the perfect foundation for a title challenge and a definitive early statement. Anything less and United will take the bragging rights into the international break.

The Bigger Picture

The summer has reshaped this fixture in ways that will take time to fully appreciate. Gone from City are Rodri, Bernardo Silva, John Stones, Savinho, and Reijnders. In their place: Anderson, Fernández, Bouaddi, Ndiaye, Allan Elias. The new midfield axis of Anderson, Fernández, Cherki and O'Reilly (when fit) is already functioning with a pace and directness that some feared would be lost in the reconstruction. The first derby is as much about establishing the identity of this new City as it is about three points.

For Enzo Maresca, who served here as a player and an assistant, Sunday is a milestone — his first Manchester derby as head coach. He has handled every test so far with composure and tactical clarity. Old Trafford, at 16:30 BST with 74,000 watching, is the biggest one yet.
