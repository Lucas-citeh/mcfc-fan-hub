Anderson Saves City a Point as Fulham End 20-Match Losing Streak Against Blues

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 21 November 2026

Manchester City 1-1 Fulham — Premier League Matchday 11, Etihad Stadium, 21 November 2026. Rodrigo Muniz put Fulham ahead in the 16th minute before Elliot Anderson's 57th-minute volley rescued a point for City, whose 20-match winning streak against Fulham came to an end. City are now winless in four Premier League games, and boos rang out at the Etihad at full-time.

Muniz Breaks the Deadlock

Fulham arrived at the Etihad having already confounded expectations this season, and they served early notice of their intentions when Rodrigo Muniz gave them the lead in the 16th minute. The Brazilian striker, one of the Premier League's most underrated forwards, found space to convert and silence the home crowd.

City's response was hampered almost immediately by a remarkable decision. Erling Haaland appeared to have equalised shortly after the opener, but referee intervention — confirmed by VAR — ruled the goal out after Haaland was adjudged to have fouled Fulham goalkeeper Bernd Leno. The Norwegian was left furious; Haaland has 14 goals in all competitions this season and has rarely had a decision go against him in that fashion. The Etihad's frustration was palpable.

Anderson Breaks His Premier League Duck

City finally found a way through in the 57th minute — and it came from an unlikely source. Elliot Anderson, City's £116m summer signing from Nottingham Forest, opened his Premier League account for the club with a well-struck volley. The goal came from a Phil Foden corner that Fulham's Sander Berge headed out only as far as Anderson, who met it cleanly and fired into the net.

It was a goal that briefly lifted the Etihad and threatened to give City the platform for a winner. Anderson has been one of the more consistent performers in a difficult autumn period — his work rate, pressing and progressive carrying have drawn praise even when results have not followed — and his first PL goal for the club was richly deserved.

Four Games Without a Win

City ultimately could not find a winner, and the draw means they have now gone four Premier League games without a victory — the worst run of Enzo Maresca's tenure. The sequence reads: 2-1 L at Villa (24 Oct), 0-1 L vs Brighton (31 Oct), 1-3 L at Nottingham Forest (7 Nov), 1-1 D vs Fulham (21 Nov). Eleven points dropped in a month.

The result also confirmed a historic milestone: Fulham's first point at the Etihad in what had been a 20-match losing streak against City. It was the kind of result that summed up City's difficulties — struggling to win games they were expected to win, and doing just enough to avoid defeat.

Boos greeted the full-time whistle, an increasingly uncomfortable sign at the Etihad. With Napoli visiting in the Champions League on Tuesday (24 November) and a top-of-the-table trip to Arsenal on Saturday (28 November), Maresca must find solutions fast.
