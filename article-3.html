Foden Commits to City: Boyhood Blue Signs New Deal Until 2030

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 5 May 2026

Phil Foden has agreed to a new four-year contract with Manchester City, committing his future to his boyhood club until the summer of 2030. Sky Sports and ESPN reported the deal is done in principle, drawing a line under a summer of speculation that had threatened to overshadow the end of the season.

Foden, 25, was entering the final year of his existing deal — a situation that had drawn interest from clubs across Europe as senior figures Bernardo Silva and John Stones prepared to leave. The contract extension was described by sources close to the club as a non-negotiable priority for manager Pep Guardiola and sporting director Hugo Viana.

The Next Era Starts Here

With Bernardo confirmed to leave as a free agent this summer after nine seasons and 451 appearances, and Stones also departing after a decade of service, Guardiola needed a totemic figure to build the next chapter around. Foden — Stockport-born, City Academy-raised — is that player.

"Phil is not leaving. He is part of what we want to build. He is part of the future," Guardiola said earlier in the year, as negotiations quietly moved forward behind the scenes. The manager's public confidence reflected a private certainty that Foden would sign.

City's hierarchy reportedly moved swiftly to ensure no ambiguity crept into Foden's mind as the season entered its final stretch. The new deal, which runs until 2030 with an optional additional year, is understood to come with a significant uplift to his wages, reflecting his importance to the project.

A Season of Transition, Not Decline

The 2025/26 campaign has been a quieter one statistically for Foden — seven Premier League goals and nine assists across 31 appearances — but those who watch him closely at the club point to the way the team's structure has evolved rather than any dip in quality. His backheel assist for Antoine Semenyo in the 3-0 win over Crystal Palace earlier this month underlined that the instinct is still very much there.

He has played through a period of significant transition: the adaptation to new signings in Tijjani Reijnders and Rayan Cherki, the mid-season arrivals of Semenyo and Marc Guehi, and the gradual reintegration of Rodri after his injury. In all of that flux, Foden remained a constant. He played a full part in both legs of the FA Cup run and delivered a composed performance in the semi-final.

What It Means

The renewal keeps Foden at the Etihad for the prime years of his career. He will be 29 when the deal expires. City's rebuild this summer will be framed around him — new arrivals will be shaped to complement his qualities, not vice versa.

For supporters who feared the worst when early-season reports suggested European clubs were tracking his situation, the resolution is a relief and a statement of intent. Foden is not leaving. The boy from Stockport stays home.

Sources: Sky Sports, ESPN (5 May 2026)
