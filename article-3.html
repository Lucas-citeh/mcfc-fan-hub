Arsenal Go Top After Historic Liverpool Win: City Drop to Second

1 November 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Arsenal displaced Manchester City from the top of the Premier League on Saturday evening after a 1-0 victory at Anfield — their first win at Liverpool's ground in 14 years — leaving the Gunners on 24 points from nine games, three ahead of City in second.

How it happened

The result capped a difficult week for Enzo Maresca's side. City had already slipped to back-to-back Premier League defeats — first 2-1 at Aston Villa on 24 October and then 0-1 at home to Brighton on 31 October — before Arsenal applied further pressure by claiming all three points at Anfield. Arsenal's first Premier League win at Anfield since 2012 was enough to move the Gunners three points clear of City at the summit.

The bigger picture

City remain second, having picked up 21 points from nine matches (W7 D0 L2, GD +10). The gap is small and the season is long, but Maresca will be keenly aware that two consecutive league defeats heading into November are a warning sign.

A return to winning ways at Nottingham Forest on Saturday 7 November (15:00 GMT, City Ground) is now even more pressing. City's squad faces doubts over Matheus Nunes, Nico O'Reilly and Mateo Kovacic, while Antoine Semenyo remains sidelined until December. Elliot Anderson will face the club he signed from in the summer in what promises to be a charged atmosphere.
