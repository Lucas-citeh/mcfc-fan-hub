City Lift FA Youth Cup with Win Over United

By MCFC Fan Hub 14 May 2026

Manchester City's youth team claimed the FA Youth Cup 2025-26 with a 2-1 victory over local rivals Manchester United in an enthralling final.

Goals from Floyd Samba and Reigan Heskey secured City's fifth FA Youth Cup title in a match that showcased the strength of the club's academy system.

The victory continues City's proud tradition of developing young talent, with the club's academy consistently producing players capable of making the step up to first-team football.

Match Report

Floyd Samba opened the scoring in the first half with a composed finish before Reigan Heskey doubled the lead with a well-taken goal that sent the City fans wild.

United pulled one back to set up a tense finale, but City's young stars held firm to secure the trophy and bragging rights in this Manchester derby.

"These young players have shown incredible character throughout the competition. This is what the academy is all about - developing winners." - City Academy Director

Academy Success

The victory comes in the same week that Nico O'Reilly was awarded the Premier League Academy Graduate of the Year for his outstanding performances in the first team this season.

O'Reilly has made 47 appearances this campaign, proving that the pathway from City's academy to the senior squad remains as strong as ever under Pep Guardiola's management.

Future Stars

Several players from this FA Youth Cup-winning squad are expected to be involved in first-team training next season, continuing the conveyor belt of talent that has seen players like Phil Foden and Rico Lewis break through in recent years.
