O'Reilly Called Up by England — Fit for Anfield

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 7 October 2026

Nico O'Reilly has been named in the England squad for October's international fixtures, confirming his full recovery from the back injury that forced him to miss four matches in September. The Manchester City academy graduate is now available to Enzo Maresca for the Premier League trip to Anfield on Sunday 11 October.

The Injury Background

O'Reilly suffered a back injury during the warm-up ahead of City's Premier League fixture against Coventry City on 5 September 2026. Maresca confirmed at the time that the knock was not expected to be serious, but the 20-year-old was ruled out for the following four matches: the UCL opener at Porto (8 Sep), the 199th Manchester derby at Old Trafford (13 Sep), the Carabao Cup Round 3 win over Norwich (17 Sep), and the 5-3 Premier League thriller against Sunderland (20 Sep).

He had made three appearances this season before the injury — starting the Community Shield against Arsenal at Cardiff's Principality Stadium, and in both of City's opening Premier League wins over Bournemouth and Crystal Palace.

England Call-Up Confirms Fitness

O'Reilly's selection in the England squad for October's international fixtures is the clearest confirmation yet that he has come through his rehabilitation unscathed. The midfielder worked through his recovery during the international break, and his national team call-up signals he is back to full fitness and available for club action.

The timing could scarcely be better for Manchester City. With Phil Foden ruled out of Sunday's trip to Anfield — absent both through suspension (the third and final match of his ban for violent conduct against Bruno Fernandes on 13 September) and recovery from appendicitis surgery — Maresca will need all his available midfielders to step up at one of English football's most demanding venues.

A Boost for Maresca

O'Reilly's return gives Maresca additional options in the midfield-to-forward zone where City have been excellent in Foden's absence. Elliot Anderson, Ayyoub Bouaddi, Enzo Fernández and Rayan Cherki have all impressed in the early weeks of the season, but O'Reilly's press-resistance and ability to play at left-back as well as centrally adds a different dimension.

City head to Anfield as Premier League leaders on 15 points from five games. Opta's supercomputer makes Liverpool narrow favourites (43% vs City's 30.3%, Draw 26.7%), though the Blues have lost just twice in their last six Premier League visits to Anfield. Kick-off is 16:30 BST on Sky Sports.
