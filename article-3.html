2026/27 Fixture Reveal This Friday: Season Start Date, Promoted Clubs & What City Fans Need to Know

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 17 June 2026

The wait is almost over. The Premier League has confirmed that the full 2026/27 fixture list will be published on Friday 19 June at 10:00 BST — just two days away. For Manchester City fans, it will be the moment that makes Enzo Maresca's new era feel truly real: the first look at who City play, when, and in what order across a campaign that kicks off in late August.

When and Where to See the Fixtures

The Premier League will release all 380 matches simultaneously at 10:00 BST on Friday 19 June via the official Premier League website (premierleague.com) and its app. Manchester City will publish their own fixture card on mancity.com and across the club's social media channels at the same time. Blue Moonies Fan Hub will update the #matches section with City's key fixtures as soon as they are available.

The Season Starts a Week Later Than Usual

The 2026/27 Premier League campaign kicks off on Saturday 22 August 2026 — one week later than the traditional early-August start. The delay is a direct consequence of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, which runs into mid-July, with the Premier League building in additional recovery time for the international players returning from tournament duty.

For City, this matters enormously. Erling Haaland (Norway), Phil Foden (England), Josko Gvardiol (Croatia), Rayan Ait-Nouri (Algeria), Rodri and Nico Gonzalez (Spain), Omar Marmoush (Egypt), Jeremy Doku (Belgium), Antoine Semenyo (Ghana), Savinho (Brazil), and Gianluigi Donnarumma (Italy) — all are at the World Cup in some capacity. Maresca and his staff will receive players back in waves, making the squad management challenge in the opening weeks comparable to the post-tournament starts City have navigated in previous seasons.

Before the Season: The Community Shield

Before a ball is kicked in the Premier League, Manchester City begin 2026/27 with the FA Community Shield. As FA Cup holders — Semenyo's 72nd-minute winner against Chelsea at Wembley in May sealed a 1-0 win and City's domestic double — the Blues face Premier League champions Arsenal at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August. The fixture was moved from Wembley due to concert commitments on the national stadium's schedule on 15-16 August. Cardiff hosts the curtain-raiser for the second time in the competition's modern format.

This makes 16 August City's competitive season opener — a week before the first Premier League round. Maresca's side will have a competitive match under their belt before facing their first league opponent.

Three New Clubs Join the Premier League

The 2026/27 campaign will feature three clubs returning to the top flight after promotion from the Championship:

  • Coventry City — Championship title winners. Their first Premier League season in many years brings a new fixture to the calendar for City fans and the prospect of matches at the Coventry Building Society Arena.
  • Ipswich Town — Runners-up in the Championship, securing an immediate return to the Premier League after spending last season at the top level.
  • Hull City — Play-off final winners after beating Middlesbrough in the Championship play-off final at Wembley. The Tigers complete the 20-club lineup for 2026/27.

All three newcomers will face Manchester City in their opening fixtures, giving Maresca's side three opponents with points to prove — the exact sort of game a new manager tends to handle carefully in an opening weeks period where squad depth and freshness from international duty will vary.

A New Era Under Maresca: What City Fans Are Looking For

Friday's fixture release lands at the start of a summer of transformation at the Etihad. Pep Guardiola has gone after 10 years, Bernardo Silva and John Stones have departed, and Enzo Maresca arrives with a clear attacking philosophy honed at Leicester and Chelsea. Fans will be scanning the fixture list for the same indicators they always do: when is the first Manchester derby? Who does City face in the opening weeks before World Cup players return to full fitness? Are there any gruelling early-season runs or a kind start?

The Champions League qualifying format has also changed again for 2026/27, and City's European pathway will be clarified once the fixture list is confirmed — with the new league phase schedule overlapping with the Premier League calendar in ways that will shape Maresca's rotation decisions across the autumn.

Friday 10:00 BST — Mark It in Your Calendar

Two years ago, the Premier League fixture release was the summer moment that set the agenda for everything that followed: which runs to worry about, which weekends to book, which early games would define the mood of the campaign. This summer, with a new coach, a rebuilt squad, and the backdrop of the Elliot Anderson pursuit still unresolved, the fixture list feels more significant than usual. It marks the moment that uncertainty becomes a schedule — and the point at which Enzo Maresca's Manchester City becomes something you can plan for rather than just anticipate.
