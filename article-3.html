Dias Breaks Silence on Real Madrid Rumours as Sam Lee Report Puts City Captain in Summer Frame

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 23 June 2026

Ruben Dias is the latest Manchester City player to find himself in the Real Madrid orbit. Sam Lee of The Athletic — one of the most authoritative City correspondents in the business — has reported that Jose Mourinho's Real Madrid have identified the Portuguese centre-back as a potential summer target. Dias, speaking at a Portugal World Cup press conference when asked about the speculation, gave the understated, noncommittal response his media training has perfected. Manchester City say he is not for sale.

Sam Lee Reports Real Madrid Interest

The Athletic's Sam Lee reported in mid-June 2026 that Real Madrid, under newly appointed head coach Jose Mourinho, have an interest in signing Ruben Dias. The report places Dias firmly in Mourinho's plans for rebuilding the Bernabéu defence — a pursuit that carries added weight given the manager's shared Portuguese identity with the City captain.

The timing is not coincidental. Mourinho's first major success in this transfer window was persuading Bernardo Silva to choose the Bernabéu over Barcelona, PSG, and Juventus — a move that owed as much to personal relationship and national connection as it did to footballing vision. With that template established, Real Madrid turning their attention to another Portuguese cornerstone of the Etihad is a logical next step in Mourinho's reconstruction.

Dias Speaks at Portugal's World Cup Camp

When asked about the transfer speculation at a Portugal World Cup press conference, Dias delivered the response that has defined his media appearances throughout his career: measured, composed, and entirely unhelpful to anyone looking for a storyline. "They ask you, you answer 'Portugal' and that's it," the defender said, declining to engage further with questions about his club future while on international duty.

It is a masterclass in media management — neither a denial nor an endorsement, giving absolutely nothing away. Dias has navigated years of transfer speculation with the same psychological steadiness that makes him such an effective central defender. The non-answer tells you he is aware of the situation; it tells you nothing about his intentions.

Manchester City's Position: Not for Sale

Manchester City's stance is unambiguous. Dias is not for sale. The club regard him as a central pillar of Enzo Maresca's defensive rebuild, and there is no financial or contractual pressure that would compel them to open the door to offers. He has a contract in place that extends beyond the coming season, giving the club the leverage to rebuff approaches at whatever valuation they choose.

Maresca's defensive options — Ruben Dias, Marc Guehi, Abdukodir Khusanov, Nathan Aké, Josko Gvardiol, and Rayan Ait-Nouri — give him quality and depth across the back line. But Dias remains the first name on the teamsheet, the organising voice, and the one player whose departure would require the most urgent replacement. City know this; so does Real Madrid.

Why the Speculation Has Taken Root

The summer of 2026 has seen a remarkable concentration of high-profile exits from the Etihad. John Stones' contract expired and was not renewed. Bernardo Silva left on a free transfer to join Mourinho at Real Madrid. Pep Guardiola departed after ten years. Manuel Akanji left permanently when Inter Milan triggered their €15m purchase option after winning Serie A. Against that backdrop of change and transition, questions about which senior player might be next are inevitable — and the Mourinho–Portugal connection gives them particular oxygen when Dias' name is mentioned.

For City fans, the key distinction is this: the summer's confirmed departures were largely contractual ends or pre-agreed clauses. Dias is contracted and wanted. That is a fundamentally different situation from the players who have already left.

What Happens Next

Dias will remain focused on Portugal's World Cup campaign for the foreseeable future. Any formal Real Madrid approach — if Mourinho decides to push for one — would most likely materialise in the post-tournament window, once players have returned from international duty and pre-season schedules begin to take shape. For now, City's position is clear and firm. And Ruben Dias, characteristically, is thinking only about Portugal.
