Foden Undergoes Appendicitis Surgery: Liverpool Out, PSG in Doubt

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 1 October 2026

Manchester City have confirmed that Phil Foden underwent surgery in London for acute appendicitis during the international break. The England midfielder will miss the Premier League trip to Anfield on 11 October and his availability for the Champions League home clash with Paris Saint-Germain on 14 October remains unclear.

What Happened

Foden complained of feeling unwell and was taken to hospital, where he underwent surgery to remove his appendix. Manchester City confirmed the operation was a success but stated that it was unclear at this stage how long the 26-year-old would be sidelined.

The timing compounds what was already a difficult fortnight for the Stockport-born midfielder. Foden had been serving the third match of a three-game domestic ban — picked up after being sent off for violent conduct against Bruno Fernandes in the 199th Manchester derby on 13 September — meaning he was already set to miss Liverpool regardless of the injury.

Liverpool Absence Confirmed; PSG a Doubt

Manchester City travel to Anfield on Sunday 11 October for the Premier League Matchday 6 clash with Liverpool (16:30 BST). Foden will not be involved — a combination of suspension and recovery from surgery rules him out.

Three days later, on Wednesday 14 October, City host Paris Saint-Germain at the Etihad in UCL League Phase Matchday 2 (20:00 BST). Foden's domestic ban did not extend to European competition, and his return for PSG had been widely anticipated. That now hinges on his recovery progress. The UK National Health Service recommends a minimum two-week recovery period following an appendectomy, placing the 14 October date in serious doubt.

Manchester City have declined to give a firm return date at this stage.

Maresca's Options

The injury adds to a challenging team-news picture for Enzo Maresca. Nico O'Reilly (back) has been working through his rehabilitation during the international break and is targeting a return for Liverpool, though he has not played since 28 August. Jeremy Doku made his first appearance since the Community Shield off the bench against Sunderland on 20 September, but 90 minutes of Anfield — or a European night at the Etihad — may come too soon for him.

Rayan Cherki, Elliot Anderson, Ayyoub Bouaddi and Enzo Fernández have all impressed in the central-attacking positions this season. Maresca has rotational options in behind Haaland, but the absence of Foden at his sharpest will be felt, particularly against PSG, where City will need their most creative players at full capacity.

Haaland Unaffected — City Still Flying

The broader picture remains extremely positive. City are top of the Premier League on 15 points from five games, three clear of their nearest challengers, with Erling Haaland already on seven goals across all competitions. The squad depth assembled in the summer — Anderson, Fernández, Bouaddi, Ndiaye, Elias — gives Maresca cover, but Foden is irreplaceable when at his best.

Updates on his recovery are expected ahead of the Liverpool trip. City fans will be hoping for positive news in the days ahead.
