Samba Lights Up the Etihad: City Destroy Norwich 5-0 in Carabao Cup

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 17 September 2026

Manchester City 5-0 Norwich City — Carabao Cup Third Round, Etihad Stadium, 17 September 2026. Seventeen-year-old academy graduate Floyd Samba scored twice on his senior debut as defending holders City made light work of newly promoted Norwich to advance to the fourth round in style.

Samba Opens His City Account

With Erling Haaland rested and Phil Foden serving the first game of his three-match domestic suspension (for the red card against Manchester United on 13 September), Enzo Maresca handed a maiden senior start to Floyd Samba — the 17-year-old son of former Premier League centre-back Christopher Samba. The teenager repaid the faith immediately, heading home from Allan Elias's cross in the 29th minute to open the scoring.

Rayan Cherki doubled the advantage three minutes later (32'), finishing crisply from a Ayyoub Bouaddi assist, before Brazilian winger Allan Elias — making his first City start after signing from Palmeiras on 31 August — drove a low shot into the bottom corner in the 48th minute to make it 3-0.

Samba's Second Seals a Memorable Night

The evening's star turn was not finished. Samba curled in a brilliant second goal in the 54th minute to leave the Etihad buzzing. "It was mind-blowing," the teenager told mancity.com after the final whistle. He is the son of Republic of Congo international Christopher Samba — a long-serving Premier League centre-back — and the younger brother of Tyrone Samba. Academy colleagues and regular watchers of the City EDS had long tipped him as an exceptional talent; Thursday confirmed it for a wider audience.

McAidoo Adds Gloss

Ryan McAidoo completed the rout in the 90th minute, converting an assist from Nico O'Reilly to make it five and seal City's place in the Carabao Cup fourth round. The result extended City's unbeaten run across all competitions to six games since the start of the 2026/27 season.

Rotation Vindicated

Maresca deployed a heavily rotated XI: Gerónimo Rulli started in goal, with Vitor Reis deputising for Ruben Dias at centre-back alongside Khusanov, and Rayan Aït-Nouri and Rico Lewis in the full-back positions. Kovacic, Bouaddi, O'Reilly and Cherki formed an energetic midfield. The manager will have been delighted by the returns of fringe players and academy talent, with next Sunday's Premier League home game against Sunderland now the focus.
