Carabao Cup R4: Rulli Heroics Send City Into Quarter-Finals After Brighton Thriller

28 October 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester City 2-2 Brighton & Hove Albion (City win 4-3 on penalties) — Etihad Stadium, Wednesday 28 October 2026.

Manchester City are into the Carabao Cup quarter-finals after a dramatic night at the Etihad that required a penalty shootout to separate the sides. Enzo Maresca's holders came from behind twice and were ultimately indebted to goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli, who saved two penalties — from Yasin Ayari and Lewis Dunk — while Rayan Cherki stepped up to seal a 4-3 shootout victory with the final kick of the night.

Match summary

Brighton made the brighter start and were rewarded around the 30th minute when Malick Yalcouyé gave the visitors the lead. City hit back before half-time through Mateo Kovacic, who struck from inside the area to level at 1-1. Early in the second half, Georginio Rutter restored Brighton's advantage, leaving Maresca's side with work to do.

The equaliser came with roughly 20 minutes remaining, and it brought a milestone with it. Vitor Reis — flicking home from Cherki's low free-kick delivery — scored his first goal for Manchester City, turning home at the near post to send the Etihad into celebration and take the tie to a shootout.

Shootout drama

Rulli, who had replaced Stefan Ortega as City's cup goalkeeper, was the hero of the shootout. The Argentine — signed from Marseille in August on a two-year deal — produced two outstanding saves, first denying Ayari and then diving low to his right to keep out Dunk's effort. City held their nerve from 12 yards and, when Cherki stepped forward for the fourth kick, he dispatched it calmly into the corner to seal a 4-3 win.

What it means

City face Bradford City in the quarter-final in December — a rematch of sorts with a lower-league side having beaten Norwich 5-0 in the third round. Winning the Carabao Cup last season, City are well placed to defend their trophy under Maresca. The result also provides a timely lift ahead of Saturday's Premier League meeting with Brighton at the Etihad — the second of a double-header in the space of four days — as City look to respond to Friday's first league defeat of the season at Aston Villa.

Vitor Reis, the 20-year-old Brazilian centre-back who returned from a successful loan at Girona in the summer, will particularly remember the night after his first senior goal for the club.
