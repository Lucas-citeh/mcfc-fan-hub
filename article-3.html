Farewell Ake: Nathan Aké Confirmed to Fenerbahce in £7m Deal After Six Trophy-Laden Years

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 5 July 2026

Manchester City have officially confirmed Nathan Aké's departure to Fenerbahce in a permanent deal worth £7 million, potentially rising to £8.5m with add-ons. The Dutch international, 31, leaves the Etihad after six years and 12 trophies, with sporting director Hugo Viana paying tribute to "an outstanding, model professional."

The Deal

Fenerbahce announced the agreement first — with Manchester City initially declining to comment — but the Premier League club formally confirmed the transfer following the finalisation of documentation on 4 July 2026. The base fee of £7m is a straightforward permanent deal, with performance-related add-ons potentially taking the total to £8.5m. Aké, who joined City from Bournemouth in 2020 for around £41m, departs significantly below his original price tag but with his legacy cemented in silverware rather than transfer fees.

Aké's Farewell

The Dutchman issued a farewell message on the club's official channels. "It's been a privilege to represent Manchester City over the past six years," Aké said. "I will always cherish the memories, particularly of winning the treble." The sentiment was echoed by City supporters across social media, where Aké's reliability and professionalism — far more consistent than many initially expected for a £41m purchase — earned genuine warmth and respect.

Hugo Viana's Tribute

Manchester City's new sporting director Hugo Viana offered a warm farewell to the departing defender: "There's no doubt that Nathan has been a key part of our success over the past few years. Whenever he stepped onto the pitch, he always gave everything as well as being hugely dependable for the team. He's also shown versatile qualities — arriving as a centre-back but excelling at left-back including in our Treble-winning season of 2022/23. At the training ground, he was an outstanding, model professional who set high standards for everyone around him."

Viana's emphasis on Aké's adaptability is well-founded. Originally purchased as a centre-back, Aké evolved into one of the best attacking left-backs in the Premier League under Pep Guardiola — a transformation that became central to how City pressed and built from the back during their most dominant years.

What Aké Won at City

In 177 appearances for Manchester City, Nathan Aké was part of a squad that accumulated 12 trophies: four Premier League titles (2020/21, 2021/22, 2022/23, 2023/24), one UEFA Champions League (2022/23), one FIFA Club World Cup (2023), two FA Cups (2022/23, 2024/25), two Carabao Cups, one UEFA Super Cup, and one FA Community Shield. His performance level in the 2022/23 treble campaign — where he operated predominantly at left-back alongside Kyle Walker, Rúben Dias and Manel Akanji — was arguably the peak of his City career.

What Happens Now: Maresca's Left-Side Problem

Aké's departure presents Enzo Maresca with a challenge as he builds his squad for 2026/27. The left side of City's defence has historically been an area of strength; Aké's exit creates a vacancy that currently leaves Rayan Aït-Nouri as the primary option at left-back, with Joško Gvardiol able to cover across the back line. Rico Lewis' ability to invert from the right into midfield could also see tactical reconfigurations.

Whether Maresca dips into the market for a specialist left-back this summer remains to be seen. Aït-Nouri — signed from Wolverhampton Wanderers last summer — is an exciting attacking option but has not yet cemented first-choice status at the top level for a full season. The pre-season Asia tour and Community Shield will provide early tests of how the new manager intends to solve the equation.

As for Aké himself: Fenerbahce, managed by José Mourinho, have been active in the market this summer and represent a genuinely attractive destination. Champions League football could be within reach if they navigate qualifying. Aké arrives in Istanbul as an experienced, decorated international with plenty still left in the tank.
