Rodri Surgery Casts Cloud Over City Future as Real Madrid Circle

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 24 July 2026

Five days after winning the World Cup with Spain, Rodrigo Hernández faces an uncertain summer. Back surgery with no confirmed return timeline has complicated both Manchester City's pre-season plans and their contract negotiations with one of the greatest players of his generation — and Real Madrid, according to multiple reports, are growing in confidence that they can bring him to the Spanish capital before the window closes.

What We Know About the Surgery

Multiple outlets, including CityXtra and Read Man City, reported on 23 July 2026 that Rodri is set to undergo surgery on a back complaint suffered during the 2026 World Cup. The procedure is described by Spanish sources as relatively minor in nature — not a major structural operation — but it carries an unclear recovery timeline that no outlet has been able to pin down precisely as of the date of publication.

What is clear is that Rodri will not be present for City's Asia Tour pre-season, which opens against Inter Milan in Hong Kong on 1 August under new head coach Enzo Maresca. Whether he is available for the Community Shield against Arsenal in Cardiff on 16 August is now considered unlikely by most reports. Earlier speculation pointed to the Manchester derby in mid-September as a potential return target, though that framing predates the surgery news and should be treated with caution until there is a specific medical update from the club.

Real Madrid's Growing Confidence

Manchester City have always maintained publicly that Rodri is not for sale. But the combination of factors now in play has reportedly altered the dynamic significantly.

CityXtra reported on 23 July that Real Madrid are growing in confidence over a potential deal, with the Spain international understood to have opened the door to leaving the Etihad Stadium this summer. Yahoo Sports and Read Man City carried similar reporting, noting that Rodri has indicated a desire to return to Spain — a long-held ambition that intensified after the World Cup triumph. Multiple outlets have reported that Manchester City are holding out for a fee in the region of €100m should they decide to sanction a sale.

It is important to note what this does and does not represent. No official bid has been made by Real Madrid. No transfer request has been submitted by Rodri. Manchester City have not publicly indicated that the player is available. The reporting reflects sources indicating that Rodri would be open to a move and that Real Madrid are monitoring the situation closely — the distinction between interest and an imminent deal matters.

Why This Moment Is Different

The Real Madrid–Rodri story is not new. Interest from Madrid has been reported in previous summers, and Fabrizio Romano confirmed earlier in June 2026 that Real Madrid would not pursue Rodri in the summer window. What appears to have changed is the context: a player with reportedly one year remaining on his contract, heading into surgery with an unknown recovery period, represents a fundamentally different negotiating challenge for City than he did before the World Cup ended.

The timing also complicates Manchester City's management of Enzo Maresca's first season. City spent £116m on Elliot Anderson from Nottingham Forest in part because Maresca needs elite central midfield cover — and that need is more acute if Rodri's start to the season is delayed by weeks rather than days. But selling their best midfielder into that situation would be a significant gamble. That tension sits at the heart of City's current dilemma.

Maresca's Position

The new head coach has not commented publicly on the Rodri situation. His focus will be on building cohesion during the Asia Tour and integrating new arrivals including Anderson and Jeremy Monga, both of whom are expected to be involved in pre-season. Nico Gonzalez and Mateo Kovacic offer depth in central midfield, but neither is a like-for-like replacement for what Rodri provides at the highest level.

City supporters will hope for clarity from the club in the coming weeks — both on the surgery and recovery timeline, and on the transfer situation. For now, the picture remains uncertain in a way it rarely has been around one of football's most dependable players.
