Maresca: 'We Need Balance' — City To Use International Break To Fix Three-Game Losing Run

8 November 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca vowed to use the November international break to identify and address the problems that led to a third consecutive Premier League defeat — a 3-1 loss at Nottingham Forest on Saturday — leaving City six points behind Premier League leaders Arsenal.

Post-match reaction

Maresca was forthright in his assessment after the final whistle at the City Ground. Speaking to reporters, he said the first half had not been good enough: "It's quite clear — in the first half, we were not good enough." He pointed to City's inability to deal with Forest on the counter-attack as the key problem, describing the second and third goals as essentially the same in structure — "copy and paste" — with his side losing the key duels in transition.

On the swing from seven consecutive wins to three straight defeats, the City boss called for perspective: "We won seven in a row. Now we have lost three in a row, so we need balance on that."

International break plan

With the November international window now under way, City will be without several first-team players on international duty. Maresca said the break would be used constructively: "We're going to use this international break to try to clarify what we can do better."

It is the first time City have lost three successive Premier League matches since a run of four in November–December 2024.

Looking ahead to Fulham

City's next Premier League fixture is at home to Fulham on Saturday 21 November (15:00 GMT, Etihad Stadium). Antoine Semenyo remains sidelined with the knee injury he sustained against Ipswich in October, with his return still expected around December. The fitness of Matheus Nunes, Nico O'Reilly and Mateo Kovacic — all absent for the Forest defeat — will be monitored during the break.

Title race snapshot (after GW10)

1. Arsenal — 27 pts (W9 D0 L1)
2. Manchester City — 21 pts (W7 D0 L3, GD +8)

Arsenal lead by six points with 28 games remaining. The Gunners visit the Etihad for a top-of-the-table clash on Saturday 28 November.
