City Begin Winger Hunt as Savinho Exit Edges Closer to Tottenham

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 15 July 2026

Manchester City have begun exploring the market for a new wide-attacking player, with Fabrizio Romano confirming the club are actively assessing options as Savinho's expected departure to Tottenham Hotspur continues to inch forward. Sam Lee of The Athletic has reported on the profile sporting director Hugo Viana is seeking: a direct winger capable of dribbling and creating chances through passing.

Romano: City Exploring New Winger Options

Romano — whose track record on Manchester City transfer intelligence is strong — confirmed that the club are "exploring the possibility to go for a new winger" this summer. The urgency of the search is linked directly to the Savinho situation: the Brazilian has agreed personal terms with Tottenham on a five-year deal until 2031, and Romano described the move as a "strong possibility" as recently as 13 July, with Spurs "not stopping" in their pursuit of a player they have coveted for over a year.

City's asking price has consistently been reported in the £55–60m range. Once a club-to-club agreement is finalised, a significant attacking departure is expected to be rubber-stamped quickly — at which point City's search for cover on the right flank will become a formal pursuit rather than a contingency plan.

Viana's Profile: Dribbling and Chance Creation

Sam Lee, whose reporting on City's internal workings has proven reliable across recent transfer windows, specified that Viana and the City recruitment team want a wide player who can both "dribble and create chances with passes" — a profile that rules out certain types of wider midfielder in favour of someone genuinely direct and progressive in their ball-carrying.

The description points toward a winger who combines one-on-one threat with playmaking output: the kind of profile that would allow Enzo Maresca to retain the directness Savinho brought to the right flank while also adding an additional creative dimension through the wide areas. Maresca's Chelsea side — where he deployed a high-tempo, positionally fluid system with active wide players — gives some indication of the type of profile he and Viana will prioritise.

Context: The Savinho Situation

Savinho arrived at Manchester City in summer 2023 from Troyes and has developed into one of the Premier League's most exciting wide players over the past two seasons. With Enzo Maresca's arrival and a squad rebuild underway, City took the commercial and sporting decision to sanction his exit — giving Tottenham the green light to negotiate in late June 2026.

Spurs have since been relentless in their pursuit. In addition to Savinho, Tottenham have already completed the £100m signing of Sandro Tonali from Newcastle United in early July, signalling the scale of their summer investment under new head coach Roberto De Zerbi. Savinho would represent Spurs' second major acquisition of the window.

For City, the sale — should it be completed at or around the reported £55–60m mark — would generate funds that can be immediately recycled into bringing in their preferred wide-attacking profile before the start of the 2026/27 Premier League season. City's PL opener against Bournemouth at the Etihad is scheduled for 23 August, giving the recruitment team roughly five to six weeks to complete a deal once Savinho's transfer is formalised.

The Window in Wider Context

City's summer has already seen significant activity. The club-record £116m signing of Elliot Anderson from Nottingham Forest represents a major investment in the central-midfield area. Jeremy Monga (£10m from Leicester City) adds youthful attacking energy. Incoming manager Enzo Maresca and Viana have also overseen the departures of Bernardo Silva, John Stones, Nathan Aké, Manuel Akanji, and Pep Guardiola — a significant change of personnel across the squad and the dugout simultaneously.

A winger signing would complete what appears to be Maresca's planned first-window rebuild: a new goalkeeper (Donnarumma, signed last summer), a new central midfielder (Anderson, this summer), and now a new wide forward to replace Savinho's output on the right. City fans will be watching the next few weeks closely as Hugo Viana targets the final piece of Maresca's new-era starting XI.
