Two Games, Two Points: City's Title Charge Enters Its Final Act

By MCFC Fan Hub 15 May 2026

Manchester City go into the final fortnight of the 2025/26 Premier League season two points behind Arsenal, needing maximum points from their remaining two fixtures — away at Bournemouth on Tuesday 19 May and at home to Aston Villa on Sunday 24 May — while hoping results elsewhere go their way.

Tuesday's commanding 3-0 victory over Crystal Palace at the Etihad cut the gap to Arsenal to just two points, shifting goal difference in City's favour. It was the kind of statement performance that keeps the title race mathematically alive heading into the most consequential week of the campaign.

The Maths

City's maximum return from their remaining two games is 83 points. Arsenal, who lead with 79 points, can accumulate a maximum of 85, meaning two Arsenal wins in their final fixtures would seal the title regardless of what City do. For Guardiola's side to lift the trophy, they need to win both their remaining matches and hope that Arsenal drop points in at least one of their own remaining games.

A goal-difference advantage currently sits with City, meaning a level-points finish on 83 apiece — should Arsenal take just three points from their last two — could yet see the trophy swing to the Etihad on the final day.

The Bournemouth Test

First up is a trip to the Vitality Stadium on Tuesday 19 May to face Bournemouth. The Cherries have been one of the stories of the season and will provide a stern examination, though City's away record — which includes the 3-0 win at Stamford Bridge in April — underlines the confidence running through Guardiola's squad.

Villa Day

Should City win at Bournemouth, the final-day home fixture against Aston Villa on 24 May becomes a potential title-decider. The Etihad will be rocking if City need a win to leapfrog Arsenal at the summit. Villa, who finished fourth last season and are chasing European football, will come to Manchester with their own agenda — making it a true match-day spectacle whatever the title situation.

Cup Before League

Before any of that, City face Chelsea in the FA Cup Final at Wembley on Saturday 16 May. Winning at Wembley would complete a domestic cup double — City already won the EFL Cup earlier this season — and could galvanise the squad ahead of those crucial league dates. The mental and physical demands of a Wembley final a week before back-to-back decisive league fixtures is a genuine factor, and Guardiola will be managing his squad carefully across all three games.

After Tuesday's win over Crystal Palace, Rodri — who had been sidelined with a groin issue — is reported to be in a better condition and in contention to feature at Wembley and beyond. His return, should it materialise, would represent a significant boost as City enter the most critical stretch of their season.

History Beckons

If City win their final two Premier League games and claim the FA Cup at Wembley, they would stand as one of English football's great sides — a domestic treble candidate entering the final weekend of the season. The fate of the title is not in their hands alone, but the squad, the manager, and the Etihad faithful will give everything to ensure that when it counts most, City are ready.
