Bouaddi Transfer 'Imminent' as City and Lille Poised to Finalise €100m Deal

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 11 August 2026

Manchester City's most significant piece of summer business is entering its final phase. Goal.com has declared the Ayyoub Bouaddi deal "imminent," with Yahoo Sports and Get French Football News both reporting that City and Lille are "poised to finalise" an agreement for the 18-year-old Moroccan midfielder — described across multiple outlets as the natural long-term successor to Rodri, who is expected to sign for Barcelona on Wednesday 12 August.

'One Foot in Manchester City'

Goal.com's headline on Tuesday 11 August was unambiguous: "Imminent deal: Bouaddi puts one foot in Manchester City." Yahoo Sports and French specialist outlet Get French Football News echoed the same language — "Lille and Manchester City poised to finalize agreement" — with the completion of the deal described as "imminent" by parties involved in the negotiations.

Fabrizio Romano, via Manchester City fan outlet mancitynews.com, reported that City are "on the verge" of sealing their fifth summer signing. Romano had described the negotiations as "accelerating" from as early as 29–30 July, and has been a consistent presence in the Bouaddi reporting chain throughout the summer. This is the clearest language yet from the journalist with the strongest track record in this saga.

Personal terms have been agreed between Bouaddi and Manchester City for some time. Reports have varied between a five-year contract to June 2031 (per Yahoo Sports) and a six-year deal (Morocco World News), but the direction of travel has been consistent throughout. Critically, sources close to the Moroccan midfielder indicate he would prefer to join City immediately this summer rather than return to Lille on loan for another season — which had been one of the central stumbling blocks in negotiations. That preference from the player's side gives City additional leverage to push Lille towards a final agreement.

Fee Structure: Bridging the Gap

The remaining obstacle between the clubs is financial. Lille's valuation sits at approximately €100 million for an outright purchase, while Manchester City's position has been reported at around €90 million plus performance-related bonuses. Both CaughtOffside and Get French Football News believe the gap is narrow enough to bridge, and Goal.com's "imminent" characterisation suggests the clubs are closer than they have been at any point in this saga.

A fee in the region of €90–100 million would make Bouaddi one of Manchester City's most significant transfer commitments in recent history. The profile justifies the outlay: Bouaddi is 18 years old, rated among the best central midfielders in European football regardless of age group, and was a standout performer for Morocco at the 2026 World Cup — his composure, pressing intensity, and passing range catching the eye of managers across the continent.

The Rodri Connection

Bouaddi's potential arrival has always been inseparable from Rodri's departure. The Ballon d'Or winner is expected to travel to Barcelona on Wednesday 12 August to sign a four-year deal with FC Barcelona, following the club-to-club fee agreement of €50 million reached on 9 August. Rodri's back surgery — carried out in Madrid on 28 July — means he would have been unavailable until mid-September in any case, but the transfer makes his absence from the Etihad permanent.

Bouaddi's profile — a ball-winning midfielder with elite positional intelligence and the range of passing to operate as a genuine deep-lying playmaker — positions him as the long-term answer to the void Rodri leaves. Whether he joins this summer or on a delayed basis if any loan-back arrangement is still agreed, his trajectory points clearly to becoming a central figure in the Maresca era. City's ability to plan for the future while the present moves forward is one of the defining challenges of the summer window.

Community Shield: Five Days Away

While the transfer machinery keeps turning, City's attention is also shifting to the first competitive fixture of the Maresca era: the FA Community Shield against Arsenal at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August. Kick-off is at 15:00 BST; the match is live on TNT Sports and HBO Max in the UK.

Arsenal arrive in Cardiff as Premier League champions, having pipped City to the title by three points on the final day of the 2025/26 season. City arrive as FA Cup holders — Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute winner at Wembley in May sealed a 1-0 victory over Chelsea. For Maresca, the Community Shield will be his first competitive test, and the first chance for supporters to see how he sets up without Rodri as the fulcrum of the midfield.

Haaland, Elliot Anderson, Marc Guehi and Nico O'Reilly are all expected to be available having returned from post-World Cup breaks. The squad flew back from Seoul after the Asia Tour and have resumed training at the CFA. On the pre-season evidence — three results that mixed encouragement with defeat — Maresca's City are still finding their shape. Cardiff is where that process becomes competitive.
