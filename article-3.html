Carabao Cup R4: City Look to Extend Trophy Defence vs Brighton

25 October 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester City return to the Etihad on Wednesday 28 October (19:30 BST) looking to defend the Carabao Cup they won last season, when Enzo Maresca's squad face Brighton & Hove Albion in the fourth round.

It will be the first of two meetings with Brighton inside four days — the clubs also meet in the Premier League on Saturday 31 October at the same venue, making this the most concentrated fixture sequence of City's season so far.

How they got here

City cruised through the third round with a commanding 5-0 victory over Norwich City at the Etihad on 17 September. Floyd Samba — the 17-year-old academy forward and son of former Blackburn centre-back Christopher Samba — scored twice on his senior debut that night, with Rayan Cherki, Allan Elias and substitute McAidoo also on the scoresheet.

Brighton's route to the fourth round was considerably tougher: they beat Manchester United 3-2 at Old Trafford, eliminating last season's runners-up on their own ground.

Squad news

Antoine Semenyo remains sidelined with the knee injury he suffered in the 3-2 win over Ipswich Town on 17 October, with manager Enzo Maresca confirming on 19 October that the Ghanaian winger faces up to eight weeks out, pointing to a return around December 2026.

Phil Foden is expected to be available after coming off the bench at Villa Park on Saturday. Erling Haaland, who headed home City's consolation in the 2-1 defeat at Aston Villa on 24 October, is also expected to feature, with Maresca likely to rotate given the Premier League fixture follows just three days later.

Context

City sit first in the Premier League on 21 points (W7 D0 L1) after Saturday's first loss of the season at Villa Park — three points clear of second-placed Arsenal. The Carabao Cup offers Maresca's side an opportunity to put that setback behind them before the league rematch with Brighton on Saturday.

City are the current holders, having beaten Chelsea in the final last season. A fourth-round exit at the hands of a fellow Premier League side would mark a rare early Cup departure for the club.
