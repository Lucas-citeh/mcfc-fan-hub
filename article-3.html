Doku Doubt, O'Reilly Boost: Maresca's Derby Injury Update

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 10 September 2026

Manchester City head coach Enzo Maresca has provided optimistic injury updates on both Nico O'Reilly and Jeremy Doku ahead of Sunday's Manchester derby at Old Trafford (16:30 BST, 13 September 2026).

O'Reilly In Contention to Return

Nico O'Reilly, who was absent from City's 22-man squad for the Champions League trip to FC Porto on Tuesday, is in contention to return for the derby. The 20-year-old had been managing a back complaint that ruled him out of the Estádio do Dragão fixture, but Maresca gave optimistic signals ahead of the showdown at Old Trafford.

O'Reilly has been a key part of Maresca's system so far this season, operating as an energetic left-sided midfielder in City's 4-1-4-1. His return would strengthen a midfield that saw Matheus Nunes deputise at Porto. Per readmancity.com and 101greatgoals.com (10 September 2026).

Doku Remains a Doubt

Jeremy Doku continues to be assessed with the calf injury he sustained during the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal on 16 August. The Belgian winger has missed all three Premier League matches and the opening UCL fixture. Maresca has not ruled him out for Sunday, and the winger's return — if it comes — would add a different dimension to City's wide play for the derby.

Doku's directness and one-versus-one ability would be a significant asset against United's full-backs in a high-profile fixture that suits his fearless, dribbling style. A decision on his availability is expected to be made closer to matchday. Per mancitynews.com; Sports Mole (10 September 2026).

City Head to Old Trafford in Excellent Shape

Even with those absentees, City arrive at Old Trafford with maximum momentum. Tuesday's 2-0 Champions League victory at Porto — sealed by a Haaland brace — extended City's run to five wins from five competitive fixtures under Maresca (Community Shield defeat aside). Erling Haaland now has five goals in all competitions this season, including a pair of headers and a clinical right-foot finish in Porto.

The Premier League table reads: Manchester City 1st, 9 points, three wins, no defeats. A victory at Old Trafford would put Maresca's side on 12 points and further cement their title credentials.

United's Early-Season Picture

Manchester United kick off as a rejuvenated home side at Old Trafford — historically one of City's toughest venues even in dominant eras. The atmosphere of the derby will provide the sternest test yet of Maresca's squad depth and the new signings' ability to handle big-game pressure.

City are the clear favourites heading into Sunday, but derby football has a way of upending expectations. Maresca and his squad will be focusing on extending their unbeaten run in what is the biggest test of the new era so far.
