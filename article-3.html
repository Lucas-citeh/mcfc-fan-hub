Manchester City 2-4 PSG: Haaland Brace Not Enough as Defending Champions Stun Etihad

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 14 October 2026

Paris Saint-Germain delivered a statement performance at the Etihad Stadium on Wednesday, defeating Manchester City 4-2 in UEFA Champions League League Phase Matchday 2. The defending champions led 3-0 at half-time and held on despite Erling Haaland's second-half brace, with Maghnes Akliouche's 62nd-minute strike sealing the points. City suffer their first Champions League defeat of the season.

A Brutal First Half

PSG — managed by Pep Guardiola, making his return to the Etihad Stadium — were devastating in the opening 45 minutes, scoring three times to stun the home crowd. Désiré Doué opened the scoring in the 13th minute, and Ousmane Dembélé doubled the lead just two minutes later. Vitinha added a third before half-time in the 32nd minute, leaving City with an enormous mountain to climb.

The manner of the first half was a harsh lesson for Enzo Maresca's side: PSG pressed high, moved the ball with speed and punished every lapse in concentration.

Haaland Fights Back

City emerged with more purpose after the interval and Erling Haaland gave them hope. The Norwegian struck in the 53rd minute to make it 3-1, and PSG's Akliouche responded with a fourth goal at 62' to seemingly put the result beyond doubt. Haaland was not done — he converted again in the 75th minute to bring it back to 4-2 — but City could not find a third goal.

Haaland's brace takes his tally to 10 goals in all competitions this season — a remarkable return across just nine appearances.

Guardiola's Return

The occasion carried extra emotional weight: Pep Guardiola, City's manager for a decade (2016–2026), returned to the Etihad as PSG's head coach for the first time since his summer departure. The packed stadium gave him a reception before kick-off; he left with all three points.

Context and Outlook

The defeat comes less than three weeks after the independent commission delivered a guilty verdict on City's financial charges. The club remain focused: their six-point lead at the top of the Premier League is undiminished, and they return to league action at home to Ipswich Town on Saturday (15:00 BST).

City's UCL record now stands at P2 W1 D0 L1, with fixtures against Barcelona, Sporting CP, Napoli, RB Leipzig, AEK Athens and Lens still to come in the league phase. A run of wins will be needed to ensure comfortable qualification for the knockout rounds.
