Haaland and Cherki Braces Blow Away Crystal Palace in 4-1 Rout

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 28 August 2026

Manchester City swept aside Crystal Palace 4-1 at Selhurst Park on Friday night, with Erling Haaland and Rayan Cherki each hitting braces in a dominant display that puts Enzo Maresca's side on six points from two Premier League games.

The Match

City made a commanding start at Selhurst Park, taking the lead in the 17th minute when Phil Foden found Haaland with a precision delivery and the Norwegian powered a header into the net. Palace threatened intermittently but City controlled the pace of the game throughout.

The hosts levelled in unfortunate fashion — Yeremy Pino's free kick struck the crossbar, cannoned into the back of Gianluigi Donnarumma, and bounced over the line for an own goal. It proved a brief interruption. Rayan Cherki put City back in front nine minutes after the restart with an electrifying display of footwork before a composed finish, then added his second on 59 minutes with a clinical strike from outside the box with his weaker right foot — his first senior brace for the club.

Haaland completed the afternoon's work in the 84th minute, latching on to another Foden delivery to thump a left-footed strike into the near post. Phil Foden finished with two assists, continuing the creative form he showed against Bournemouth in the season opener.

City's XI

Maresca lined up with: Donnarumma; Khusanov, Dias (C), Guehi, Gvardiol; Anderson, O'Reilly; Foden, Cherki, Semenyo; Haaland. The back four retained from the Bournemouth win was intact, while Anderson and O'Reilly gave the midfield engine room industry and pressing intensity.

Cherki's Moment

The 20-year-old French playmaker has been developing steadily since his summer 2025 arrival from Lyon, showing flashes of his exceptional talent in training and pre-season. This was his first decisive contribution on a Premier League stage — two goals in five minutes that effectively killed the match. Maresca will be delighted to have this quality now available in a midfield that has been rebuilt from scratch this summer.

What This Means

Two Premier League games, two wins, six goals scored, two conceded. Maresca's new-look City — missing Rodri, Bernardo Silva, Reijnders, Savinho, Gonzalez and Marmoush, all sold or loaned this summer — are finding their feet quickly. The next test comes at the Etihad on 5 September when newly promoted Coventry City visit for Matchday 3.
