Triple Deadline Day: Fernandez, Ndiaye & Allan Complete City's Summer Revolution

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 4 September 2026

Manchester City made a stunning triple deadline-day swoop on 1 September 2026, completing the signings of Enzo Fernández from Chelsea, Iliman Ndiaye from Everton and Allan Elias from Palmeiras — taking their summer outlay well past £600m and completing one of the most extraordinary squad rebuilds in English football history.

Enzo Fernández: The Joint British Record

The headline move arrived in the final hours of the window: Enzo Fernández signed from Chelsea for a joint British record £125m fee, equalling Liverpool's outlay for Alexander Isak. The Argentine World Cup winner — a Champions League finalist and one of the most complete central midfielders in the Premier League — put pen to paper on a five-year contract taking him to June 2031.

Fernández wears No. 17 at the Etihad — the shirt made famous by Kevin De Bruyne during his extraordinary decade-long tenure. He had hoped to take the No. 8 he wore at Chelsea, but the shirt was unavailable. "He takes a huge burden with that number, but he's more than capable," noted one source close to the deal.

The signing fills the deep-lying midfield vacancy left by Rodri's departure to Barcelona and adds world-class quality alongside Elliot Anderson, Mateo Kovacic and Ayyoub Bouaddi in City's remade engine room. Sources: mancity.com ('City complete Enzo Fernández signing from Chelsea' — 1 September 2026); ESPN; Sky Sports; Al Jazeera; Yahoo Sports.

Iliman Ndiaye: The Flank Reinforcement

City's second deadline-day capture brought Senegal international Iliman Ndiaye from Everton for £65m. The 26-year-old, who wears No. 7, is equally effective on either flank and impressed significantly across two Premier League seasons at Goodison Park. City had to move quickly after Tottenham's interest collapsed when Richarlison refused to return to Everton as part of a swap arrangement.

"My heart was set on joining City," Ndiaye told mancity.com in his first interview. The winger signed a five-year deal. Sources: mancity.com ('Ndiaye signs for City'; 'Ndiaye: My heart was set on joining City' — 1 September 2026); ESPN; Football Transfers; Washington Times.

Allan Elias: The Young Brazilian

Completing an extraordinary day's business, City also confirmed the signing of Brazilian winger Allan Elias from Palmeiras for €40m (≈£32m) on a five-year contract to June 2031. The 22-year-old, who wears No. 37, is primarily a right winger and had made 96 first-team appearances for Palmeiras before his move to Manchester. Director of Football Hugo Viana said: "Allan is a really exciting player, and we think he will be a big addition to the squad." The signing was officially announced on 31 August 2026. Sources: mancity.com ('City seal signing of winger Allan Elias' — 31 August 2026); ESPN; Al Jazeera.

Grealish Returns to Everton on Loan

As part of a busy deadline day, Jack Grealish rejoined Everton on a season-long loan. The England forward impressed during a first spell at Goodison Park — scoring two goals and providing six assists before a January foot injury curtailed his campaign. The loan move is viewed as Grealish's opportunity to rediscover full match fitness ahead of a potential permanent decision in January 2027. Sources: ESPN; Yahoo Sports; OneFootball.

The Full Summer Picture

The late flurry means City's completed summer business is now as follows:

Ins: Enzo Maresca (head coach), Elliot Anderson (£116m, Nottingham Forest), Ayyoub Bouaddi (€100m/≈£86m, Lille), Enzo Fernández (£125m, Chelsea), Iliman Ndiaye (£65m, Everton), Allan Elias (£32m, Palmeiras), Jeremy Monga (£10m, Leicester City), Gerónimo Rulli (€3.8m, Marseille), Pierce Charles (£10m, Sheffield Wednesday), Mathys Detourbet (€25m, Troyes — loaned to Monaco).

Outs: Rodri (FC Barcelona, €76.5m), Savinho (Tottenham, £85m), Nico González (Newcastle, £52m), Tijjani Reijnders (Al Qadsiah, €61m), Omar Marmoush (Tottenham, loan/£60m obligation), Bernardo Silva (Real Madrid, free), John Stones (Inter Milan, free), James Trafford (Leeds United, £40m), Nathan Aké (Fenerbahce, £7m), Manuel Akanji (Inter Milan, €15m). Loans: Jack Grealish (Everton, season-long).

Malo Gusto was not signed despite extensive negotiations. The right-back position remains to be addressed in January, but with Matheus Nunes, Rico Lewis and Nico O'Reilly continuing to rotate through the role, Enzo Maresca has significant options. City sit top of the Premier League after two wins from two and face newly promoted Coventry City at the Etihad on Saturday 5 September.

Sources: mancity.com (multiple announcements — 31 August / 1 September 2026); ESPN; Sky Sports; Al Jazeera; Yahoo Sports; Washington Times ('Man City signs Enzo Fernandez and Iliman Ndiaye to take unprecedented summer outlay past $600M' — 1 September 2026).
