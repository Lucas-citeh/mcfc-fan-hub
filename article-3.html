Manchester City 3-2 Ipswich Town: Haaland Brace Seals Maresca's Record Seven-From-Seven

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 17 October 2026

Manchester City made history at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday, defeating Ipswich Town 3-2 to record a perfect seven wins from seven Premier League matches. Enzo Maresca became the first manager in the competition's history to win his opening seven games in charge of a club. Phil Foden marked his return from suspension with a goal, Erling Haaland struck twice, and City survived a late Ipswich fightback to extend their lead at the top of the table.

Foden Returns in Style

Enzo Maresca made six changes from the Champions League defeat to PSG, handing starts to Phil Foden — back after suspension — alongside Ayyoub Bouaddi, who made his first Premier League start for the club. The hosts took the lead in the 19th minute when Foden found space inside the area and slotted past Walton with composure. It was Foden's first goal of the season in the league, and his hunger throughout the first half was evident.

The lead lasted barely two minutes. Daizen Maeda, Ipswich's energetic Japanese forward, capitalised on a moment of uncertainty in the City backline to level in the 21st minute, briefly silencing the Etihad.

Haaland Takes Control

City did not dwell on the setback. In the 34th minute, Erling Haaland demonstrated why he remains the Premier League's most potent goalscorer, meeting a delivery into the box with a powerful header to restore City's advantage. It was a goal of simplicity and certainty — the hallmark of Haaland at his best.

The game remained at 2-1 at the break, with City firmly in the ascendancy but Ipswich refusing to concede territory cheaply.

VAR Drama and a Clincher

The second half produced controversy. Haaland thought he had sealed his hat-trick in the 85th minute, only for VAR to intervene and rule the effort out for offside in the build-up. The decision drew loud protests from the home supporters but the verdict stood.

It ultimately did not matter. Haaland had already wrapped up the points with his second of the afternoon in the 69th minute, sliding in expertly to finish off a move that showcased City's incisive counter-attacking football. Haaland now has eight Premier League goals in seven appearances this season.

Ipswich Fight Back Late

Ipswich pulled a goal back through Zian Flemming, who converted from the penalty spot in the 90th minute to set up a nervy finale. City held firm, however, to take all three points and move six clear at the summit.

Injury Concerns

The only shadow over the day came post-match, as both Haaland and substitute Antoine Semenyo left the pitch with apparent injuries. Maresca was cautiously optimistic after the game, suggesting neither was believed to be serious, but the coming days will determine their availability for City's trip to Leipzig in the Champions League.

Maresca's Place in History

Seven wins from seven in the Premier League is a record no manager had previously achieved. Maresca's meticulous preparation, and the squad depth assembled by sporting director Hugo Viana over the summer, have given City an early stranglehold on the title race. With six points separating City from the chasing pack, the early signs suggest a formidable campaign ahead.
