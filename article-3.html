Romano Confirms It: Elliot Anderson to Manchester City — Here We Go, £116m British Record Deal Agreed

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 26 June 2026

It is done. Fabrizio Romano issued his famous "here we go" at around 20:00 BST on 25 June 2026, confirming that Manchester City have agreed a £116m deal with Nottingham Forest for England midfielder Elliot Anderson. The 23-year-old, currently with the England World Cup squad in the United States, asked Forest to let him leave and has been pencilled in for a medical in New York. An official announcement is expected once that formality is completed.

Romano Drops the Bombshell

The transfer saga that had dominated Manchester City's summer finally reached its conclusion on Wednesday evening when Romano posted on X: "BREAKING: Elliot Anderson to Manchester City, here we go! Deal in place between the clubs, fee worth £116m. #MCFC sources confirm agreement now done after talks at final stages as reported last week. Anderson asked Forest today to go and leave the club. Medical in the US."

The announcement capped weeks of dramatic negotiations that included a British record opening bid, a rejected offer of £106m plus £15m add-ons, and speculation about Manchester United entering the race — before Fabrizio Romano confirmed they had formally withdrawn. Throughout, Manchester City maintained confidence and kept pressing, and Forest owner Evangelos Marinakis ultimately accepted the improved terms. The total package may rise to £130m with add-ons, though Manchester City sources insist the guaranteed flat fee is £116m.

ESPN, Sky Sports, and The Athletic's David Ornstein all corroborated the agreement within hours of Romano's post, making this one of the summer's most comprehensively confirmed transfers even before any official club statement.

Who Is Elliot Anderson?

Anderson is a 23-year-old central midfielder from Newcastle, who was developed through the Nottingham Forest academy and burst into the Premier League with a string of outstanding performances. A dynamic box-to-box presence with an engine to match his technical quality, he ranked among the Premier League's top midfielders for progressive carries, chances created, and pressing intensity across the 2025/26 season.

He has already earned senior England recognition and is currently in Thomas Tuchel's World Cup squad, with England facing Panama in their group stage finale. His availability for a medical in the United States has been cleared, and Anderson has already signalled his desire to make the move — asking Forest's hierarchy to facilitate the departure.

A Record Fee That Reflects New Ambitions

At £116m guaranteed (potentially £130m in full), the Anderson deal would be Manchester City's most expensive signing ever, surpassing the £100m paid for Jack Grealish in 2021 — itself a British record at the time. The fee also eclipses Declan Rice's £105m move from West Ham to Arsenal in 2023, making Anderson the most expensive British transfer in football history should the full package be realised.

The scale of investment reflects two things: the quality of the target, and the ambitions of Enzo Maresca's incoming regime. City have entered this window under no illusions about the rebuild required. Pep Guardiola has gone. Bernardo Silva has joined Real Madrid. John Stones and Manuel Akanji have departed. The midfield needs dynamism, energy, and a player capable of driving from deep — Anderson fits every part of that profile.

What Anderson Brings to Maresca's System

Enzo Maresca built his Chelsea side around technical midfielders who could sustain high tempo and press with purpose. Anderson is ideally suited to that system. His ability to win the ball high up the pitch, carry into the final third, and arrive late into scoring positions makes him something City's midfield has lacked for two seasons — a box-to-box contributor with elite athleticism.

Paired with Rodri — whose own renewal talks are expected to resume after Spain's World Cup campaign — Anderson would give Maresca a midfield axis capable of competing for honours immediately. The addition of Rayan Cherki, Tijjani Reijnders (assuming he stays), and the established presence of Mateo Kovacic offers significant depth behind him.

What Happens Next

Anderson's medical in New York is the next step. Once that is cleared, both Manchester City and Nottingham Forest are expected to make official announcements. The personal terms — understood to involve a long-term contract at the Etihad — are already agreed. Barring any unexpected complication, Elliot Anderson will be a Manchester City player before the end of June.

For City supporters who spent the first weeks of summer watching Bernardo Silva leave and Guardiola say farewell, this is the first major reason to look forward. A new era is arriving, and it is arriving with intent.
