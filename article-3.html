Ait-Nouri, Reijnders & Mubama Fire City to 3-1 Win Over K League All-Stars in Seoul

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 6 August 2026

Manchester City recorded their first win of the Enzo Maresca era on Wednesday evening, beating K League All-Stars 3-1 at Seoul World Cup Stadium in a thrilling second fixture of their Asia Tour. Rayan Ait-Nouri, Tijjani Reijnders and Divin Mubama all found the net in a breathtaking 23-minute spell, with Antoine Semenyo the creative force running the show throughout.

Three Goals in 23 Minutes

City's opening night in Seoul was a statement of attacking intent. The tone was set almost immediately, with Maresca's 4-2-3-1 generating chances at will against an All-Stars XI that struggled to live with the visitors' pace and movement in the first quarter of the game.

Rayan Ait-Nouri opened the scoring after a flowing team move down the right, with a cross from Tijjani Reijnders eventually finding its way to the Algerian left-back, who fired home at the back post. It was the kind of overlapping run that Maresca has been keen to encourage from his full-backs — and Ait-Nouri delivered on the promise, arriving late and converting with authority.

City doubled their lead through Tijjani Reijnders, and the goal owed much to the individual brilliance of Antoine Semenyo. The Ghanaian winger — outstanding across both of City's Asia Tour fixtures so far — delivered a trademark backheel assist that redirected the play beautifully into Reijnders' path, and the Dutch midfielder finished brilliantly. The crowd of tens of thousands at Seoul World Cup Stadium rose to applaud.

The K League All-Stars pulled one back through Kim Dae Won, offering a brief reminder that pre-season opponents are rarely compliant. But City were not rattled. Within minutes, Divin Mubama restored the two-goal cushion — the young centre-forward following up a parried effort to chip coolly over goalkeeper Song Bum Keun. Three-one, and it was not yet the 23rd minute.

Semenyo Again the Standout

If there was one name above all others that will have caught Maresca's attention across both Seoul fixtures, it is Antoine Semenyo. The Bournemouth signing from January 2026 — who joined City for a reported £65 million — has carried his end-of-season form seamlessly into pre-season. In Seoul, he was direct, creative and relentlessly positive in every phase of play. Sky Sports singled out both Semenyo and Tijjani Reijnders as City's standout performers.

Reijnders, whose future had briefly been the subject of speculation earlier in the summer (with The Hard Tackle reporting City had "rebuffed Newcastle United and priced them out"), looks every inch a key cog in Maresca's plans. The Dutchman's movement, press resistance and ability to arrive late into the box make him a natural fit for the system being built at the Etihad.

What This Tells Us About Maresca's City

Across two pre-season fixtures, a pattern is already emerging. Maresca's City press with energy, rotate the ball quickly, and consistently get numbers forward — a 4-2-3-1 that at times morphs into something closer to a 4-3-3 in the attacking phase, with the double pivot providing the platform. Against Inter Milan in Hong Kong last Saturday, the performance was encouraging if ultimately ended by a penalty shootout. Against the K League All-Stars in Seoul, the first win followed.

Gianluigi Donnarumma was composed in goal, making the routine stops required of him. Rico Lewis continued to show his tactical intelligence in the right-back role. Josko Gvardiol and Abdukodir Khusanov gave City a physical presence in central defence.

Divin Mubama — leading the line in the absence of Erling Haaland, who is expected to join the pre-season group on a staggered basis — has now scored in both Asia Tour fixtures. The youngster is not a long-term Haaland replacement, but his performances so far have been impressive enough to earn genuine praise within the camp.

What Comes Next

The Asia Tour concludes on Sunday 9 August with a third and final fixture — against Atlético Madrid at Seoul World Cup Stadium, also at 13:00 BST. It will be the sternest pre-season test yet for Maresca's squad, and the first opportunity to see how City cope against top-level European opposition in a more competitive environment.

After that, the squad returns to the UK ahead of what has now become a genuine season opener: the FA Community Shield against Arsenal on Sunday 16 August at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff. With the squad expected to be close to full strength by that point — Haaland, Elliot Anderson, Marc Guehi and Nico O'Reilly all returning from World Cup duty — it promises to be a genuine contest against Premier League champions Arsenal.

On this evidence from Seoul, the Maresca era is building nicely.
