Savinho Stays: Tottenham Transfer Collapses as City Winger Commits to 2031

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 21 July 2026

The summer's most drawn-out transfer saga has ended in a surprise resolution: Savinho is staying at Manchester City. After weeks of reports that a £60m deal with Tottenham Hotspur had been agreed in principle, the transfer has collapsed and the Brazilian winger has committed his future to the Etihad until 2031.

How It Unravelled

Reports on 16 July from Read Man City, TeamTalk and others indicated that City and Tottenham had reached a verbal agreement worth approximately £60m (rising to around £65m with add-ons), with Savinho having agreed personal terms with Spurs on a five-year deal. The move appeared to be nearing completion.

However, Manchester City's position contained a crucial condition: they would not sanction Savinho's departure until they had secured a replacement wide forward. As reported by CityXtra and esteemedkompany.com on 19 July, City were actively searching the market for a winger to fill the gap before allowing the deal to proceed. This is a pattern consistent with City's approach in previous windows — they showed a similar reluctance to sell Savinho in summer 2025, when interest from Tottenham collapsed after the club was unable to sign Rodrygo Goes from Real Madrid as his replacement.

With no suitable replacement secured in the required timeframe, the Tottenham deal fell through. Reports emerged that Savinho has now signed on until 2031 at Manchester City, with Goal.com and mancity.com confirming he is staying at the club. SI.com reported a "U-turn" by the player, while Soccerway described it as "a new six-year deal to put an end to the Tottenham saga."

What This Means for Maresca's Plans

Savinho's retention is a significant boost for Enzo Maresca heading into the first competitive fixture on 16 August. Questions had been asked about City's wide options following Savinho's expected departure — with only Jeremy Doku, Jeremy Monga and Antoine Semenyo available as natural wide forwards on the right or left flank. Savinho's continued presence gives Maresca four wide forwards in his squad, allowing genuine competition for places and rotation across multiple competitions.

Whether Savinho was actually given assurances about his role under Maresca, or whether the collapse was purely circumstantial — City needing a replacement that was not available at the right price — remains unclear from public reporting. What is clear is that he is now part of Maresca's plans for 2026/27 and beyond.

A Costly Window for Spurs

For Tottenham and Roberto De Zerbi, the collapse is a significant setback. Savinho was identified as a priority target and the deal was believed to be imminent. Spurs will now need to turn their attention elsewhere in the market to strengthen their wide options ahead of the new Premier League season.

City supporters, meanwhile, can welcome back a player whose directness, pace and ability to beat his man make him one of the most dangerous one-on-one attackers in the Premier League. At 21, Savinho has years of development ahead of him — and Maresca, who built a system at Chelsea that thrived on dynamic wing play, should be the ideal coach to get the best from him.
