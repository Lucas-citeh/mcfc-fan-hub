What's Next: Carabao Cup Home Tie vs Norwich Then Sunderland at the Etihad

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 13 September 2026

After today's 199th Manchester derby at Old Trafford, Enzo Maresca's side return to the Etihad for two home fixtures in quick succession: a Carabao Cup third-round tie against Norwich City on Thursday (19:30 BST) and a Premier League clash with Sunderland on the following Sunday (14:00 BST).

Carabao Cup Third Round — Manchester City vs Norwich City

Manchester City were drawn at home to Norwich City in the Carabao Cup third round, with the fixture confirmed for Thursday 17 September 2026, kick-off 19:30 BST at the Etihad Stadium. The match will be screened on Sky Sports+.

Maresca is expected to use the League Cup tie as an opportunity to rotate, giving minutes to fringe players and younger squad members who have not featured regularly in the opening weeks of the season. Players such as Jeremy Monga, Allan Elias, Ayyoub Bouaddi and Pierce Charles — still on loan at QPR — will be assessed. Nico O'Reilly, returned from a back complaint, may also get further minutes ahead of the league schedule. Sources: mancity.com ('City to host Norwich City in Carabao Cup third round'); mancity.com ('Date and kick-off time confirmed for Carabao Cup home clash with Norwich — 17 Sep 2026, 19:30 BST').

Norwich City, the Carabao Cup third-round opponents, were promoted back to the Premier League in May 2026 after winning the Championship. Maresca's City go into the tie as heavy favourites, though the League Cup provides an opportunity for squad depth to be tested and proven.

Premier League Matchday 5 — Manchester City vs Sunderland

City's next Premier League fixture is at home to newly promoted Sunderland, with kick-off on Sunday 20 September 2026 at 14:00 BST at the Etihad Stadium. The game was moved from its original Saturday 19 September slot to avoid a fixture clash with the Carabao Cup tie. It will be broadcast live on Sky Sports. Sources: mancity.com ('New date for Sunderland Premier League clash'); Premier League official announcement.

Sunderland make their return to top-flight football after years in the Championship. The Stadium of Light club, managed by Mike Dodds, secured promotion last season via the Championship play-offs and will be aiming to make their first visit to the Etihad a competitive one. City are expected to be at close to full strength for the home league fixture, with the Carabao Cup providing rotational cover in the days before.

The Bigger Picture

City currently lead the Premier League on nine points — three wins from three. A victory at Old Trafford today would take them to 12 points before the international break. The Sunderland match on 20 September falls after the international break, giving Maresca a chance to recharge squad members called up for national duty before resuming the domestic programme. The next Champions League matchday is in early October.

With a Carabao Cup quarter-final place and three consecutive Premier League wins as the targets for the next seven days, it promises to be a full and important week for the new City era.
