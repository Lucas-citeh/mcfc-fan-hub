Swiss Soldier Departs: Akanji's €15m Exit to Inter Confirmed as City's Summer Clear-Out Begins

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 2 June 2026

Manchester City's summer squad overhaul has its first confirmed departure: Manuel Akanji will leave the Etihad permanently after Inter Milan activated their mandatory €15 million purchase option, a clause triggered by the Nerazzurri's Serie A title win. It is the opening move in what promises to be a transformative pre-season for new head coach Enzo Maresca.

Fabrizio Romano confirmed the deal in late April, describing it as a matter of "only formalities" after Inter hit the sporting and appearance conditions embedded in the original loan agreement. Akanji, 30, joined Inter from City on a season-long loan in September 2025 for an initial €1 million fee, with Inter holding the right — and ultimately the obligation — to purchase him outright at €15 million if those conditions were met.

A Clause Written in Trophies

The purchase clause was contingent on two criteria: Akanji had to feature in at least 50 per cent of Inter's Serie A fixtures across the season, and Inter had to win the Italian top flight. Both conditions were satisfied with room to spare. The Swiss international made over 30 appearances across all competitions in 2025/26, becoming a regular in manager Cristian Chivu's three-man defensive system. Inter won the Scudetto, completing the obligation and sending City a fee of approximately £13 million.

For Manchester City, that represents a modest but welcome return on a player who had fallen behind in the pecking order at the Etihad. Akanji had been a capable if unspectacular contributor in previous City seasons, but the arrivals of Marc Guehi and Abdukodir Khusanov's development into a reliable starter had squeezed his pathway to regular minutes. The loan to Milan offered playing time; the buyout now completes a clean exit that benefits all three parties.

Akanji in Numbers at Inter

Akanji's time in Milan was, by most measures, a success. He scored once and recorded an assist in the league, and his ability to operate as the right-sided centre-back in Chivu's 3-4-2-1 structure made him a trusted rotation option. He arrived with Premier League experience and the ability to play out from the back — qualities that were well-suited to Inter's possession-orientated system. City supporters might feel a pang of nostalgia for a player who served the club competently, even if he rarely felt like a first-choice starter.

The Bigger Picture: Summer Clear-Out Under Way

Akanji's departure is the first in what looks set to be a significant summer reshuffle. Bernardo Silva and John Stones have already left as free agents following the end of their contracts, and Savinho appears increasingly likely to follow. Fabrizio Romano reported on 2 June that Tottenham Hotspur's new head coach Roberto De Zerbi has given his full approval for the club to sign the Brazilian winger, with City open to selling at approximately £60 million. Savinho himself is open to the move after limited starts in 2025/26.

That would represent three senior squad exits in a single window — a significant reduction in depth, particularly in wide areas. Enzo Maresca and sporting director Hugo Viana are aware of the gaps and are working to fill them. Romano has identified RB Leipzig winger Yan Diomande — Bundesliga Rookie of the Year in 2025/26 with 13 goals and 10 assists — as City's primary attacking wide target this summer.

What It Means for Next Season

City's defensive picture heading into 2026/27 is relatively clear, even without Akanji: Ruben Dias, Marc Guehi, Abdukodir Khusanov and Josko Gvardiol provide quality and depth at centre-back, while Rico Lewis and Rayan Ait-Nouri cover the flanks. The right-back position is a known area of focus for Maresca, with Pedro Porro (Tottenham) among those linked with a return to the club. City will likely operate with no more than three senior centre-backs once the squad is trimmed.

The summer transfer window for Premier League clubs opens on Monday 15 June. The fixture list for 2026/27 is due to be published on Friday 19 June. Between those two dates, and across the subsequent weeks of the World Cup, Maresca's new-era squad will begin to take shape. Akanji's departure, official and confirmed, is where it starts.
