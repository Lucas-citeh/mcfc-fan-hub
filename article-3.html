Blue Heaven in Cardiff: City to Face Arsenal in Community Shield at Principality Stadium

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 30 May 2026

Manchester City will face Premier League champions Arsenal in the 2026 FA Community Shield at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August — the first time the curtain-raiser has left Wembley in 20 years, and the first competitive match of the Enzo Maresca era.

The Football Association confirmed the details in March, revealing that Wembley Stadium is unavailable on both Saturday 15 and Sunday 16 August due to pre-planned concerts. The Principality Stadium — formerly the Millennium Stadium, where the Community Shield was staged six times between 2001 and 2006 while Wembley was being redeveloped — steps in as host for the first time in two decades.

Why Cardiff?

Wembley's calendar conflict centres on performances by The Weeknd, who is scheduled to play at the national stadium on both 15 and 16 August. With no viable rescheduling window, the FA moved swiftly to secure an alternative venue. Cardiff's 73,000-capacity, roof-enclosed Principality Stadium has a strong history with the Community Shield and is widely regarded as one of the best neutral venues in Britain.

How Both Clubs Qualified

Arsenal qualify as 2025/26 Premier League champions, finishing the campaign on 85 points — seven clear of second-placed City. Manchester City earned their berth as FA Cup winners, with Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute goal at Wembley on 16 May settling a tense 1-0 final against Chelsea to complete a domestic double under Pep Guardiola in his final season at the club.

It will be the first competitive fixture of the Enzo Maresca era at City. The new head coach takes charge of his first match on a big stage — away from the Etihad, at a neutral ground, against the reigning league champions. A daunting debut, and a mouth-watering tie for supporters planning the trip west to Wales.

Looking Ahead: The Full Season Schedule

The full 2026/27 Premier League fixture list will be published at 10:00 BST on Friday 19 June, giving supporters their first sight of the complete domestic schedule. The Premier League season itself kicks off on 22 August — one week later than usual because of the 2026 FIFA World Cup taking place this summer. The Community Shield on 16 August is the first confirmed date on the 2026/27 calendar.

Tickets and travel information for Cardiff are expected to be released by both clubs closer to the date.
