Derby Confirmed for Sunday 13 Sep: Doku Targets Return at Old Trafford

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 7 September 2026

Manchester City's first Manchester derby of 2026/27 has been confirmed for Sunday 13 September at 16:30 BST at Old Trafford — not Saturday as some listings had suggested — and there is a fitness boost ahead of the fixture with Jeremy Doku targeting a return from the calf injury that has kept him out since the Community Shield.

Derby Date Confirmed: Sunday 13 September

Sky Sports and Goal.com have confirmed that the Manchester United vs Manchester City Premier League fixture will take place on Sunday 13 September 2026, kicking off at 16:30 BST at Old Trafford. The match is live on Sky Sports. It is Matchday 4 of the 2026/27 Premier League season and the first league meeting between the two clubs this term.

Enzo Maresca's City arrive at Old Trafford with a perfect nine-point record from the first three Matchdays — wins over Bournemouth (2-1), Crystal Palace (4-1), and Coventry City (1-0) — and will be looking to stretch that start to 12 points from a possible 12.

Doku Targeting Derby Return

Jeremy Doku has not featured since sustaining a calf injury during the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal on 16 August. Enzo Maresca initially described the problem as a "two to three week" issue, and after three full weeks on the sideline the Belgian winger is reported to be back in individual training and targeting a return for the Old Trafford trip.

Doku was absent for all three Premier League matches in August and early September. His directness, acceleration, and one-versus-one ability would offer a different dimension to City's attack — particularly against a United side that will be expected to sit defensively compact in the second half if City take the lead. A final fitness assessment is expected closer to matchday. Sources: Sports Mole (injury report — 4 September 2026); Fantasy Football Scout (FPL Gameweek 4 fitness update — 4 September 2026); Read Man City (injury tracker — 2 September 2026).

Nunes Back in Training

Matheus Nunes, who had been described as "still not 100%" by Maresca ahead of the Coventry fixture, has also returned to full training during the international break. The Portuguese wing-back — who Maresca lists as a defender — is expected to be available for selection against United. Sources: Sports Mole (Crystal Palace injury report — 28 August 2026); Fantasy Football Scout (FPL Gameweek 3 update — 4 September 2026).

Full Fitness Picture

City's squad is in good health heading into the derby. Enzo Fernández and Iliman Ndiaye, who both made their competitive City debuts against Coventry on 5 September, are fully available. Ayyoub Bouaddi — who made his debut as a substitute in the 4-1 win at Palace and again came off the bench against Coventry — continues to build his match sharpness. The only confirmed absentee remains Doku, whose status is to be confirmed by the manager ahead of the weekend.

With a full 13-day gap between the Coventry win and the Old Trafford showdown, Maresca has had more preparation time than at any point in the season so far. City head into the international break in the form of their lives and with, potentially, their strongest available squad since the summer.
