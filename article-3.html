UCL Kicks Off Tonight: City Head to Porto Without O'Reilly or Doku

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 8 September 2026

Manchester City begin their UEFA Champions League 2026/27 campaign tonight, travelling to FC Porto's Estádio do Dragão for an 8 September kick-off at 20:00 BST. Enzo Maresca's side arrive in Portugal carrying a perfect Premier League record — nine points from nine — but without two key players: Nico O'Reilly (back) and Jeremy Doku (calf) have both been ruled out for tonight's match.

Fixture Details

FC Porto host Manchester City in the UEFA Champions League 2026/27 league phase at the Estádio do Dragão in Porto, Portugal. Kick-off is at 20:00 BST (19:00 UTC). This is City's first competitive European fixture under new head coach Enzo Maresca, who replaced Pep Guardiola in May 2026. Sources: mancity.com ('FC Porto v City: Kick-off time and TV information' — 8 Sep 2026); UEFA.com.

O'Reilly Did Not Travel

Nico O'Reilly suffered a lower back problem during the pre-match warm-up before Saturday's Premier League win over Coventry City (1-0), forcing a late change to the starting lineup — Enzo Fernández came in to replace him. The 21-year-old has not made the trip to Portugal for tonight's game. However, Enzo Maresca was quick to play down fears when asked about the injury ahead of the Champions League opener, saying the problem is "not serious" and expressing confidence that O'Reilly will be available for Sunday's Manchester derby at Old Trafford.

O'Reilly has been one of City's reliable options in the opening weeks of the season, having featured in the Community Shield and started the Premier League wins over Bournemouth and Crystal Palace. His expected return for Sunday will be a significant boost for a derby Maresca will want to approach at full strength. Sources: Sports Mole (injury report — 5 Sep 2026); Yahoo Sports ('Maresca reveals Man City duo could return for Manchester derby'); GiveMeSport ('Man City Handed Injury Scare Over Wonderful Star Ahead of Man Utd Derby Clash').

Doku Remains Absent

Jeremy Doku is also absent from City's travelling squad for Porto. The Belgian winger has not played since sustaining a calf injury during the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal on 16 August — now three weeks ago. Maresca was cautious when pressed on Doku's availability for the derby, saying: "Jeremy, I'm not sure yet. We need to wait." Doku did not train fully with the squad during the international break, though he has been working individually on his recovery. His electric pace and directness would provide a different dimension against a United side likely to sit deep.

Ndiaye: No Concern After Coventry

Iliman Ndiaye, who made his competitive City debut in the Coventry win and came off late in the second half, confirmed via City Chief on X that his substitution was precautionary: "Just a little cramp." The Senegal international is fully available for both tonight and the derby. Source: City Chief (@City_Chief) on X — 5 Sep 2026.

Predicted Lineup

With O'Reilly absent and Maresca expected to rotate ahead of Sunday's derby, the predicted starting XI is a 4-2-3-1: Donnarumma; Nunes, Khusanov, Dias (C), Gvardiol; Anderson, Bouaddi; Foden, Cherki, Semenyo; Haaland. This would hand Ayyoub Bouaddi a first Champions League start for the club at just 18. Marc Guehi — who has started all three Premier League games — is expected to be rested.

City's Perfect Start Under Maresca

Enzo Maresca's men go to Portugal in outstanding domestic form: three wins, nine points, seven goals scored and only two conceded in the Premier League. Erling Haaland has three league goals to his name, Rayan Cherki has two stunning finishes, and new signings Enzo Fernández and Iliman Ndiaye both impressed on debut against Coventry. The squad has depth — and tonight offers a first chance to demonstrate European ambitions under a new era.

Ahead of the Porto match and the derby double-header, Maresca said: "We know it is a tough schedule — Porto and then United in five days. We need to manage minutes carefully, but I want to win every game." City have beaten Porto in Europe on three previous occasions in recent years.
