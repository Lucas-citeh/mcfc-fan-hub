Anderson Medical Complete — Last-Minute Kansas Switch Clears Final Hurdle in Record £116m Manchester City Deal

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 29 June 2026

Elliot Anderson has completed his Manchester City medical in Kansas, clearing the final pre-signing hurdle in the club's record £116m deal with Nottingham Forest. David Ornstein (The Athletic) reported a last-minute change to the arrangements — the check-up was originally planned for New York but was moved to Kansas while Anderson continued his England World Cup duties. Official announcements from both Manchester City and Nottingham Forest are expected imminently, with all personal terms already agreed.

The Last-Minute Location Change

Elliot Anderson's medical had originally been pencilled in for New York — convenient given the England squad's base during their World Cup group stage. However, David Ornstein reported that a last-minute logistical change shifted the venue to Kansas, where the medical was ultimately completed. The change did not affect the substance of the deal or the timeline for an announcement, with the check-up proceeding smoothly once the revised location was confirmed.

Anderson was cleared to undergo the medical by England manager Thomas Tuchel despite being in the middle of international duty. The arrangement underscores just how far advanced the deal was: both clubs and the player were sufficiently confident in the outcome to organise a medical mid-tournament.

The Deal in Numbers

When the official announcement lands, the headline figure will be £116 million — a fixed fee with no add-ons, per Manchester City sources. That makes this the most expensive British transfer in football history, eclipsing Declan Rice's £105m move from West Ham to Arsenal in 2023 and Grealish's £100m switch to City from Aston Villa in 2021. Anderson, 23, will sign a five-year contract worth in the region of £300,000 per week once all performance bonuses are included, with an option for a further 12 months.

The signing was first confirmed by Fabrizio Romano on 25 June 2026 with his signature "here we go," with ESPN, Sky Sports, and David Ornstein all corroborating the agreement that same evening. Nottingham Forest had initially rejected an opening bid before accepting City's improved proposal. Anderson himself drove the move forward, asking Forest to facilitate his departure — a significant factor in accelerating the final stages of negotiations.

What Anderson Brings to Maresca's System

Enzo Maresca's first major statement as Manchester City head coach is a big one. Anderson is a box-to-box central midfielder of the highest calibre — ranked among the Premier League's top midfielders in the 2025/26 season for progressive carries, chances created, and pressing intensity. At 23, he is in the prime developmental window for a midfielder of his type.

Maresca built his Chelsea side around technically confident midfielders who could sustain high tempo and press with purpose. Anderson fits that profile precisely: able to win the ball high, carry into the final third, and arrive late into scoring positions. With Rodri's renewal talks expected to resume after Spain's World Cup campaign, City could build their midfield engine room around Anderson and the Spanish Ballon d'Or winner for years to come.

What Happens Next

With the medical done, all that remains is the administrative finalisation and signature of contracts. Both Manchester City and Nottingham Forest are expected to release official statements once the paperwork is complete. Given the scale of the deal and Anderson's profile as a current England international, both clubs are likely to stage a formal announcement rather than a quiet midweek confirm.

Anderson's arrival will be the headline move of Manchester City's summer rebuild — a signal that despite the departures of Pep Guardiola, Bernardo Silva, John Stones, and Manuel Akanji, the ambition at the Etihad has not diminished. A new era is taking shape, and its most expensive building block is now one signature away from being official.
