Anderson Officially Signed — Manchester City Confirm Club-Record £116m Deal from Nottingham Forest

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 2 July 2026

Manchester City have officially confirmed the signing of Elliot Anderson from Nottingham Forest for a club-record fee of £116 million — the most expensive British transfer in football history. The 23-year-old central midfielder has signed a five-year contract at the Etihad Stadium and will join Enzo Maresca's squad following England's elimination from the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

The Deal Confirmed

The transfer was first broken by Fabrizio Romano on 25 June 2026 with his signature "here we go," with ESPN, Sky Sports, and David Ornstein (The Athletic) all immediately corroborating the agreement. The fee is a fixed £116 million with no add-ons, per Manchester City sources — eclipsing Declan Rice's £105 million move from West Ham to Arsenal in 2023 as the largest fee ever paid for a British player.

Anderson completed his medical at England's World Cup training base in Kansas following a last-minute change of venue from the original New York location. Premier League administrative paperwork was lodged and processed, and Anderson took to the field for England's World Cup Round of 16 victory over DR Congo (2-1, 1 July 2026) as an official Manchester City player. Both clubs formally confirmed the transfer following England's match.

The Deal in Numbers

Anderson, 23, has penned a five-year contract at the Etihad — with an option for a further 12 months — worth in the region of £300,000 per week once all performance bonuses are factored in. The £116 million fixed fee makes this Manchester City's largest ever transfer, beating the £100 million paid for Jack Grealish from Aston Villa in 2021, and marks the most expensive deal ever completed for a British player in football history.

Nottingham Forest had initially resisted enquiries but ultimately accepted City's improved proposal. Anderson himself played a decisive role, asking Forest's hierarchy to facilitate his departure once personal terms with City were agreed — a significant factor in accelerating the final stages of negotiations.

What Anderson Brings to Maresca's System

Enzo Maresca's first major signing as Manchester City head coach is a statement of intent. Anderson is a box-to-box central midfielder of the highest calibre — ranked among the Premier League's top midfielders across the 2025/26 season for progressive carries, chances created, and pressing intensity. His ability to win the ball high, carry into the final third, and arrive late into scoring positions fits the template Maresca honed at Leicester and Chelsea.

The England international turned 23 in November 2025 and is in the prime developmental window for a midfielder of his type. With Rodri's renewal talks expected to resume after Spain's World Cup campaign, City's ambition is to build their midfield engine room around Anderson and the Spanish Ballon d'Or winner for years to come.

What Happens Next

Anderson remains in the United States on England World Cup duty. England face Mexico in the Round of 16, and Anderson is expected to be part of Tuchel's plans. Should England progress, his return to Manchester for pre-season preparations will be delayed — but City will manage the workload of their record signing with care.

Pre-season training under Maresca is due to begin in mid-July. The Community Shield against Arsenal is on 16 August in Cardiff; the Premier League opener against Bournemouth follows on 23 August. Anderson's squad number at Manchester City is yet to be officially allocated.
