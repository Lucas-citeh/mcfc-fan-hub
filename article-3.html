Villa Park Awaits: Can Maresca's Unbeaten City Extend Their Perfect League Start?

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 22 October 2026

Manchester City travel to Villa Park on Saturday (kick-off 11:30 BST) in high spirits, unbeaten in the Premier League with seven wins from seven. Enzo Maresca's side sit six points clear at the top of the table and will be looking to become the first City squad in history to win their opening eight league games.

The Task at Hand

Aston Villa finished in the top half last season and their manager has consistently set them up to press high and play an attacking brand of football. Villa Park is traditionally one of the noisier atmospheres in English football, and the home side will be eager to end what has been an exceptional run for Maresca's team.

City arrive fresh from a 5-1 demolition of AEK Athens in the Champions League on Tuesday — Phil Foden's hat-trick (34', 65', 76') and goals from Elliot Anderson and Rayan Cherki provided a decisive response to the PSG setback. The mood at the club is buoyant heading into the weekend.

Record-Breaking Form

Seven consecutive wins to open a Premier League season is already extraordinary — no other City side has managed it. The sequence has included a Manchester derby win at Old Trafford with ten men, a 3-0 dismantling of Liverpool at Anfield, and a nervy but ultimately convincing 3-2 victory over Ipswich the previous Saturday. Maresca already holds the Premier League record for the best start by a manager in their first season.

Erling Haaland leads the scorers with eight Premier League goals in seven games, on pace to threaten his own records. Rayan Cherki has been electric in attack with four league goals, while the collective energy of Anderson, Fernández, and Bouaddi in midfield has given City a formidable engine.

The Fixture in Context

City face a busy spell: after Villa, they host Brighton in the Carabao Cup on Wednesday, then Brighton again in the Premier League on Saturday (31 October), before a UCL home tie against Napoli on 24 November and the highly-anticipated trip to Arsenal on 28 November. Getting to the international break in November on the back of a strong run would put City in an enviable position.

What to Watch

Maresca is likely to rotate given the fixture congestion. The return of Jérémy Doku from a calf injury in September adds a further weapon to City's attacking options. Whether Haaland is rested, with Carabao Cup duties two days later, will be a key pre-match talking point.
