Dias Signs New Four-Year Deal to 2030 as O'Reilly Set to Follow

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 27 July 2026

Ruben Dias has signed a new four-year contract at Manchester City, keeping the Portuguese defender at the Etihad Stadium until 2030. The deal was confirmed by ESPN and removes any lingering uncertainty over the skipper's long-term future under new head coach Enzo Maresca. Academy product Nico O'Reilly has also reached an agreement in principle on a new deal, per BBC Sport, with a formal announcement expected shortly.

Dias Commits to the Maresca Era

Ruben Dias has been at the centre of Manchester City's defensive identity since joining from Benfica in September 2020 for £62m. Named Premier League Player of the Season in his debut campaign, the Portuguese international won four Premier League titles, the Champions League, the FA Cup, and the EFL Cup during Pep Guardiola's final years — making him one of the most decorated defenders in the club's history.

The new four-year deal, which runs until June 2030, is a clear statement from sporting director Hugo Viana and head coach Enzo Maresca that Dias is central to City's rebuild. At 26, he enters the new deal at the peak of his powers — Portugal's World Cup campaign this summer only added to his standing as one of the best centre-backs in world football. The contract extension had been a priority for the club during the summer window, and its confirmation brings significant stability to a defence that has undergone considerable change, with Manuel Akanji and John Stones both departing.

Dias is expected to captain the side under Maresca, as he did in the latter stages of the Guardiola era. His partnership with Marc Guehi — the January 2026 signing from Crystal Palace — gives City a starting pair capable of competing at the highest level. The addition of Abdukodir Khusanov as a third option in central defence provides real depth, though Khusanov is unconfirmed in the squad at this stage and will compete for a place.

O'Reilly: An Academy Reward

Nico O'Reilly's contract situation is further advanced than many had anticipated. BBC Sport reported on 27 July 2026 that the midfielder has reached an agreement in principle on a new deal with Manchester City. The announcement had not yet been made official as of the date of publication, but the broad shape of the contract has been settled.

O'Reilly, 20, emerged as one of the most exciting young players in the country during the 2025/26 season. The left-footed midfielder became a fan favourite through his performances in the FA Cup run — including a two-goal semi-final display — and has demonstrated the versatility to play in central midfield or at left-back. His continuation at the club on improved terms reflects both his development and City's policy of extending homegrown talent before the final year of their contracts.

The exact length and financial terms of O'Reilly's deal have not yet been disclosed. The formal announcement is expected imminently.

A Pattern of Stability

The Dias and O'Reilly deals follow similar renewals earlier in the summer for Phil Foden (four-year deal to 2030, confirmed May 2026) and a reported verbal agreement with Jeremy Doku on a deal to 2031, per Fabrizio Romano, though that one has not yet been formally announced.

Together, the renewals signal that while City have undergone significant change at the top — Pep Guardiola leaving after a decade, Bernardo Silva and John Stones departing, Enzo Maresca appointed in May — the club's core is committed to the new project. Dias signing until 2030 means he will be part of whatever Maresca builds across the first four years of his tenure. That is about as strong a statement of intent as a centre-back can make.

City begin their pre-season Asia Tour on 1 August in Hong Kong, where they face Inter Milan in Maresca's first game in charge. The squad is expected to include several of the players who have committed long-term this summer as the new head coach begins the process of building his own identity at the Etihad.
