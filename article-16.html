Cherki Late Strike Earns City a 2-2 UCL Draw Against Napoli

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 24 November 2026

Manchester City 2-2 Napoli — UEFA Champions League Matchday 5, Etihad Stadium, 24 November 2026. Napoli led twice at the Etihad before Rayan Cherki's 86th-minute equaliser earned City a share of the spoils. Ayyoub Bouaddi notched his first City goal earlier in the second half, but the draw leaves Maresca's side sixth in the UCL league phase with 10 points from five games.

Napoli Take the Lead

Enzo Maresca named a rotated side for the visit of Napoli, with an eye on Saturday's crunch Premier League trip to Arsenal. The Italian champions had arrived in Manchester in good form and demonstrated why in the 55th minute, breaking the Etihad deadlock with a well-worked move that cut through City's defence.

The goal was a setback City had been unable to prevent in the first half despite having the better of the early exchanges. Napoli, organised and disciplined, looked comfortable sitting on their lead and threatened on the counter as the second half got under way.

Bouaddi Levels — His First City Goal

City's response came in the 67th minute — and it came from the most exciting source in their squad. Ayyoub Bouaddi, the €100m teenager from Lille, found space in the Napoli penalty area and finished with composure to score his first goal for Manchester City. The Etihad erupted. At 18, Bouaddi has already shown why City spent such a significant fee on him, and his first goal was a measure of his growing confidence in blue.

The equaliser seemed to settle City and they began to dominate possession, probing for the goal that would give them a lead. Erling Haaland was his typically threatening self but found Napoli's goalkeeper in outstanding form.

Napoli Strike Again

The momentum City had built was quickly undone in the 76th minute when Napoli struck again, restoring their lead with a clinical finish that silenced a crowd that had just found its voice. It was a sucker punch — the kind of goal that has tested City's resilience throughout their recent difficult patch in the Premier League.

Maresca's side had just over 10 minutes to find another equaliser or face a second Champions League defeat of the season. The pressure was on.

Cherki Rescues a Point

Four minutes from time, Rayan Cherki reminded the Etihad why City signed him from Lyon in the summer of 2025. The French playmaker found space on the edge of the box and struck a precise, curling effort past the Napoli goalkeeper to make it 2-2 in the 86th minute. It was his 8th goal of the 2026/27 season in all competitions — and arguably the most important.

The Etihad roared its appreciation. Cherki has been City's most consistent creative force during a turbulent autumn and his late leveller, following his brace at Crystal Palace and goals against Sunderland, AEK Athens, Nottingham Forest and Fulham, confirmed his status as one of Maresca's most important players.

UCL Picture — and What Comes Next

The draw leaves City in sixth place in the Champions League league phase with 10 points from five matches — still on track for the knockout rounds but requiring improvement if they are to secure a top-eight automatic berth. Their record now reads P5 W3 D1 L1.

The more pressing concern is Saturday's Premier League visit to Arsenal at the Emirates Stadium. City are five points behind the Gunners in the Premier League and have not won in four league games. A victory at the Emirates would close the gap to two points; defeat would open up an eight-point chasm. The form from this Champions League draw — two spirited equalisers, a Bouaddi breakthrough, Cherki's late magic — may at least offer some psychological momentum ahead of the biggest game of City's domestic season so far.
