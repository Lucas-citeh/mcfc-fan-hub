Anfield Injury Boost? Isak and Gakpo Both Doubts for Liverpool vs City

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 8 October 2026

Manchester City will head to Anfield on Sunday with a potential advantage in the opposition attack. Alexander Isak withdrew from Sweden's October camp with a thigh problem, while Cody Gakpo was forced off during the Netherlands' win over Serbia with an ankle injury. Both are doubts for Liverpool's home clash with the Premier League leaders on 11 October.

Isak: Thigh Problem, Described as Minor

Alexander Isak pulled out of Sweden's squad during the international break after reporting discomfort in his thigh. Sweden head coach Graham Potter described the issue as minor, telling reporters: "He's got a small problem with his thigh. It's not a big one but because of the amount of games we've got in a small space of time, he won't be able to make those." However, a second report quoted Potter giving a slightly more cautious message after Isak had further assessment, saying the problem was "a little bit more than what you think during the match."

Isak's injury history — he has been prone to muscle issues at both Newcastle and Liverpool — adds an extra layer of concern. Liverpool and Andoni Iraola will be hoping he has come through the break without aggravating the problem ahead of their biggest home fixture of the season to date. No official fitness update has yet been given by Liverpool FC.

Gakpo: Ankle Concern After Serbia Clash

Cody Gakpo's situation is potentially more serious. The Netherlands winger was substituted during the international fixture against Serbia after Sasa Lukic caught Gakpo's left ankle in a scissor challenge. The Netherlands confirmed he would not play again during the break. Netherlands boss Xavi said: "It's his ankle, that's all I know. Tomorrow we will know but hopefully it's not too important."

Sports Mole's assessment placed the likely absence at one to two weeks for a straightforward injury, but noted that a more severe ankle problem could keep Gakpo out upwards of three months. Liverpool are expected to provide a fuller update in Andoni Iraola's pre-match press conference, due later this week ahead of Sunday's 16:30 BST kick-off.

Liverpool's Wider Injury List

Even before the international break, Liverpool were already without several key players. Conor Bradley (right-back), Giovanni Leoni, Federico Chiesa and Vitezslav Jaros were already confirmed absentees. Hugo Ekitike also missed the end of Liverpool's last Premier League fixture. The potential absence of both Isak and Gakpo would represent a significant blow to Liverpool's attacking options against a City side that has kept clean sheets in three of their last four league games.

City's Own Team News

Phil Foden is definitely absent — he is serving the third and final match of his three-game domestic ban (red card vs Manchester United, 13 September) and is also recovering from appendicitis surgery confirmed during the international break. No return date has been set.

Nico O'Reilly is fit and available. The academy midfielder was named in the England squad for October internationals, confirming his full recovery from the back injury that had kept him out since 5 September. O'Reilly is in contention to start at Anfield.

Jeremy Doku appears to be nearing full fitness after returning as a substitute in the 5-3 Sunderland win on 20 September — his first appearance since picking up a calf knock in the Community Shield. The international break will have given him further time to build match sharpness.

The Context

City arrive at Anfield as Premier League leaders with five wins from five games and a goal difference of +8, six points clear of Liverpool in the table. Opta's supercomputer makes Liverpool narrow favourites for Sunday's match (Liverpool 43%, Draw 26.7%, City 30.3%), reflecting home advantage and the hostile atmosphere expected in the wake of City's guilty verdict on Premier League financial charges.

Enzo Maresca is due to hold his pre-match press conference on Friday 9 October at the City Football Academy. Official team news and confirmed line-ups are expected on matchday.
