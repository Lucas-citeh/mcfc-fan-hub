Ait-Nouri Linked with Shock January Move to Manchester United

3 November 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester United are reportedly keen on signing Manchester City left-back Rayan Ait-Nouri in the January transfer window, and the Algerian international is said to be open to crossing the Manchester divide, according to Sky Sports' paper-talk column published on 3 November 2026.

What We Know

Sky Sports' paper-talk reported on Monday 3 November that United have identified Ait-Nouri as a January target and that the player himself would be open to the move. No fee has been mentioned, no formal bid has been lodged, and mancity.com has made no statement. This is a paper-talk item, not a confirmed or imminent deal.

Ait-Nouri, 24, joined Manchester City from Wolverhampton Wanderers in the summer of 2025. The Algerian international is quick, attack-minded, and a consistent presence down City's left flank. He has started in the majority of City's Premier League fixtures so far in 2026/27 and is a key part of Enzo Maresca's system.

Why Would City Sell?

Under normal circumstances, selling a first-choice left-back to a direct rival mid-season would be unthinkable. City have already lost several senior players this summer — Rodri, Bernardo Silva, John Stones, Nathan Aké, Reijnders, Savinho and Omar Marmoush all departed — and Ait-Nouri has been one of the more consistent performers during the current campaign.

That said, City's new sporting director Hugo Viana has shown a willingness to sell at the right price. If United were to make an offer significantly above market value — and if Ait-Nouri were to push for the move — the situation could become complicated. For now, nothing suggests City have any intention of selling.

Manchester United's Situation

United have struggled with left-back cover throughout the 2026/27 season and have reportedly identified the position as a priority in January. Ait-Nouri's Premier League experience, pace and ability to play as a wing-back would fit with how United's new manager has set up the team this season.

What Happens Next

The January transfer window opens on 1 January 2027. With Sky Sports' paper-talk representing the first credible report linking Ait-Nouri to Old Trafford, City fans will be watching closely to see whether United formalise their interest or whether the story fades, as many paper-talk items do. Blue Moonies Fan Hub will update the Transfer Hub as and when confirmed developments emerge.
