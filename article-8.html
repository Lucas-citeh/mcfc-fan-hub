Liverpool 0-3 Manchester City: Haaland, Doku & Cherki Stun Anfield

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 11 October 2026

Manchester City delivered a statement performance in their first match since the Premier League guilty verdict, routing Liverpool 3-0 at Anfield. Three goals in 13 second-half minutes — from Haaland, Doku and Cherki — after Ryan Gravenberch's red card sent Enzo Maresca's side six points clear at the top of the table.

The Turning Point: Gravenberch Sees Red

The match turned on a pivotal moment just after the hour mark when Liverpool's Ryan Gravenberch received his second yellow card, reducing the hosts to ten men. Until that point the game had been a tight, cagey Premier League encounter with City controlling possession without cutting through a compact Liverpool shape. The dismissal, which came from a clumsy challenge, changed everything.

Liverpool, already without Conor Bradley, Giovanni Leoni, Federico Chiesa and Vitezslav Jaros from the starting lineup, and with doubts surrounding Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo heading into the match, suddenly found themselves scrambling with a man disadvantage against a City side full of quality in the final third.

Haaland Opens — 65'

Erling Haaland needed just three minutes after the red card to make his mark, converting to open the scoring in the 65th minute. The Norwegian, who had already created chances in normal play, showed his clinical instinct to find the net with Liverpool stretched and disorganised. It was his sixth Premier League goal of the 2026/27 season, and his eighth in all competitions — further evidence that Haaland's relationship with scoring is as natural as breathing.

Doku Makes It Two — 68'

Three minutes later and the match was done. Jérémy Doku, introduced as a substitute and still building back to full fitness after the calf injury that had kept him out since the Community Shield, struck two minutes after Haaland's opener. The Belgian winger latched onto a pass from Elliot Anderson to finish clinically in the 68th minute. It was Doku's first goal of the 2026/27 campaign and a reminder of his devastating impact when cutting inside from the left flank.

The goal underscored the quality City now possess from their bench — Anderson, who set up Doku's goal, has been one of the Premier League's standout performers since his club-record £116m arrival from Nottingham Forest this summer.

Cherki Seals It — 78'

Rayan Cherki put the seal on a remarkable afternoon with a superb finish in the 78th minute, converting from Joško Gvardiol's incisive pass. The French playmaker's goal was his fourth of the Premier League season and fifth in all competitions, cementing his status as one of the most exciting attacking midfielders in the English top flight. A year after joining from Lyon, Cherki is now influencing the biggest matches on the biggest stages.

A Defiant Statement

The significance of the result extended well beyond three points. City's win came in their first Premier League fixture since an independent commission delivered a guilty verdict on all charges relating to serious breaches of financial rules on 29 September. The club lodged an appeal on 1 October, and with sanctions yet to be determined, the case remains live. But on the pitch, Maresca's players produced a performance that silenced the sceptics.

Phil Foden was absent — serving the third and final match of his domestic ban from the red card against Manchester United on 13 September, and also continuing his recovery from appendicitis surgery carried out during the international break. Nico O'Reilly, confirmed fit after his England international call-up during the break, was available to Maresca.

The Standings

The result moves City to 18 points from six Premier League games — W6 D0 L0, goal difference +11 — and extends their lead at the top of the table to six points. Liverpool suffer their first defeat of the 2026/27 season, dropping to seventh on nine points. With PSG visiting the Etihad in the Champions League on Wednesday and Ipswich Town in the Premier League on Saturday, City's momentum is building at precisely the right time.
