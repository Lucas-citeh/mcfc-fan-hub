Haaland Named in 2026 FIFPro World XI for a Fifth Time

9 November 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Erling Haaland has been voted into the 2026 FIFPro Men's World XI for the fifth consecutive year, cementing his status as one of the most reliable goal-scorers in world football. The Norwegian striker received 28.4% of votes among forwards — narrowly edging out Harry Kane (27.6%) — in a list that recognised players for their performances between August 4, 2025 and July 19, 2026.

The Award

FIFPro's annual World XI is determined by votes from professional players worldwide and requires a minimum of 30 official appearances across the assessed period. Haaland met that threshold comfortably, with his prolific Premier League form in 2025/26 — in which City finished second — and a strong 2026 World Cup campaign in Norway's colours helping him secure the fifth selection of his career.

The 2026 FIFPro World XI forward line featured Haaland alongside Kylian Mbappé (Real Madrid), Lionel Messi (Inter Miami) and Lamine Yamal (Barcelona). Harry Kane was notably omitted despite a strong individual season at Bayern Munich, with Haaland edging him out in the vote by less than a percentage point.

What It Means for City

The recognition comes at a timely moment for Haaland and Manchester City. Despite the club's three-match Premier League losing run in October and November, Haaland has continued to deliver — his 14 goals in all competitions this season place him among the leading scorers in Europe. Nine of those goals have come in the Premier League, keeping City in second place and within reach of leaders Arsenal.

Haaland's FIFPro inclusions now span five years: 2021 (Borussia Dortmund), 2022, 2023, 2024 and now 2026 — an extraordinary sequence of consistent elite-level performance. He also added the FIFPro Special Young Player of the Year award in 2021, early in his rise to the summit of world football.

Looking Ahead

City return from the November international break with a home Premier League fixture against Fulham on Saturday 21 November (15:00 GMT), followed by a critical Champions League game against Napoli on 24 November and a top-of-the-table trip to Arsenal on 28 November. Haaland will be expected to spearhead the club's response to their recent domestic struggles as they look to close the gap on Arsenal at the top of the table.
