Five-Star City Beat Sunderland 5-3 in Eight-Goal Thriller

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 20 September 2026

Manchester City made it five Premier League wins from five with an exhilarating 5-3 victory over Sunderland at the Etihad Stadium on Sunday 20 September, heading into the international break three points clear at the top of the table.

A Game of Two Halves — and Three Brobbey Goals

It was never going to be straightforward. Sunderland arrived at the Etihad with a newly promoted side full of confidence following a 1-0 win over AZ Alkmaar in the Europa League, and their striker Brian Brobbey ensured City were made to work every minute. The Dutch centre-forward, signed from Ajax in the summer, produced one of the great Premier League hat-tricks in defeat — scoring in the 12th, 33rd and 59th minutes to repeatedly haul Sunderland level.

But City had answers every time. Enzo Fernández opened the scoring in the 9th minute with a well-struck effort from the edge of the area, before Brobbey levelled almost immediately. Rayan Cherki then restored City's lead in the 29th minute with a precise left-footed curler — his third Premier League goal of the season — only for Brobbey to equalise again before half-time.

Semenyo Brace Breaks Black Cats

The second half began with City's foot firmly on the accelerator. Antoine Semenyo, deployed on the right flank with Phil Foden absent through suspension, scored twice in quick succession — in the 43rd and 57th minutes — to put City 4-2 ahead after Brobbey completed his hat-trick in the 59th minute had reduced the arrears to one.

For a brief moment, the Etihad held its breath. At 4-3, Sunderland sensed an unlikely comeback was possible. But Erling Haaland, inevitably, had the final word. The Norwegian — who has now scored against all 25 Premier League teams he has faced — lashed home in the 81st minute to seal the three points and send the home crowd into raptures. The goal came after Jeremy Doku, making his first appearance since the Community Shield after a calf injury, came on at the 75th minute and burst forward to create the chance for Gvardiol, whose assist picked out Haaland at the back post.

Maresca's Men Head to International Break on a High

The result means City have 15 points from 15, with a goal difference of +8 — the best record in the Premier League through five games. The 4-2-3-1 deployed by Enzo Maresca (Donnarumma; Nunes, Dias, Guehi, Gvardiol; Anderson, Fernández; Semenyo, Cherki, Ndiaye; Haaland) is functioning with increasing cohesion, with the key summer signings growing into their roles week by week.

Fernández's goal was his first for the club in the Premier League, and his composure in the base of midfield alongside Elliot Anderson has already drawn widespread praise. Iliman Ndiaye, lively on the left flank, continues to justify the £65m outlay, linking effectively with Cherki in the attacking third.

What Next

Phil Foden completes the third and final game of his domestic ban during this international break period — his suspension running from the Manchester United red card on 13 September covered Norwich (EFL Cup, 17 Sep), Sunderland (PL, 20 Sep) and Liverpool at Anfield (PL, 11 Oct). City fans will have to wait a little longer still to see Foden back in a sky-blue shirt in the league. The next Premier League fixture, and the sternest test of City's title credentials so far, comes at Anfield on Sunday 11 October (16:30 BST).
