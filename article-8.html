Title Race

City Top by Three Goals: Arsenal Level on Points After Matchday 8

26 October 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester City remain top of the Premier League, but only on goal difference. After Saturday's 2-1 defeat at Aston Villa ended Enzo Maresca's side's perfect start, Arsenal's 2-0 win over Everton on the same matchday pulled the Gunners level on 21 points — leaving three goals as the only thing separating the sides at the summit.

The Numbers

Both City and Arsenal have played eight games, won seven, lost one, and drawn none. City's goal difference stands at +11; Arsenal's at +8. That three-goal advantage is all that keeps City at the top of the table heading into the final week of October. Brighton sit third on 16 points — five behind — and Liverpool are fourth on 15.

Elsewhere in Matchday 8, Tottenham Hotspur claimed a statement 2-0 win at Chelsea, continuing their strong form under their new regime; Manchester United were hammered 3-0 at home by AFC Bournemouth, their third defeat in eight games. Sunderland, newly promoted, drew 0-0 with Leeds United to pick up a point but remain in the relegation zone.

A Familiar Rival

Arsenal and City have contested the Premier League title in four of the last five seasons. The Gunners finished as champions last season with 85 points, seven more than City's 78. This year, both sides have come out flying — but Saturday's results mean the first two months of the season have produced a mirror image: identical records, separated only by the margin of goals scored and conceded.

Arsenal's loss this season came at home to Brighton in Matchday 4 — a 3-1 defeat that came before their recent nine-point run. City's loss, at Villa, was their first of the campaign. The fixture between the two sides is scheduled for 28 November at the Emirates Stadium — a game that could define the early title picture.

City's Immediate Challenge

Before they can look that far ahead, Maresca's squad faces the toughest rotation test of the season: Brighton at the Etihad in the Carabao Cup on Wednesday (19:30 BST), then Brighton again at the Etihad in the Premier League on Saturday (15:00 GMT). Antoine Semenyo remains absent with a knee injury (est. return December), and Wednesday's Carabao Cup tie offers an opportunity to rotate while maintaining momentum as holders.

City won the Carabao Cup last season and enter this year's competition with genuine ambition. Brighton — under their new manager — eliminated Manchester United in the third round and will arrive full of confidence. Maresca is expected to rotate, giving squad players like Floyd Samba, Allan Elias, Gerónimo Rulli and Ayyoub Bouaddi minutes, before a stronger side faces Brighton again in the league on Saturday.

The Wider Picture

City's goal difference advantage of +11 versus Arsenal's +8 may seem slim, but with 30 games still to play, the early-season table rarely predicts the final standings. What it does confirm is that two familiar rivals are once again operating at the highest level. The question of which side can sustain form over a full campaign — particularly through a congested winter schedule — may ultimately decide the title.
