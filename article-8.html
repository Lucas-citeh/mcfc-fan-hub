Nottingham Forest 3-1 Manchester City: Delap Brace Extends City's Winless Run to Three

7 November 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester City suffered a third successive Premier League defeat on Saturday as Liam Delap's brace and Jair Cunha's early opener gave Nottingham Forest a deserved 3-1 victory at the City Ground. Rayan Cherki's 52nd-minute strike was scant consolation as Arsenal extended their lead at the top to six points.

How It Happened

Forest came out of the blocks quickly. Jair Cunha opened the scoring inside six minutes, taking advantage of hesitant City defending to tuck home at close range. Liam Delap then doubled the advantage on 18 minutes, using his strength and pace to hold off Dias before sliding the ball past Donnarumma.

Cherki pulled one back for City in the 52nd minute with a sharp finish to briefly raise hopes at the Etihad, but Delap put the game beyond doubt in the 73rd minute, completing his brace after a driving Forest counter-attack that exposed City's high defensive line.

Key Absences Hit City Hard

Enzo Maresca was without four players for the City Ground trip. Antoine Semenyo (knee, est. return December 2026) remains sidelined, while Matheus Nunes, Nico O'Reilly and Mateo Kovacic were all absent after being rated doubtful in the build-up. The result forced Maresca into a 4-2-3-1 shape with Ndiaye and Doku providing width behind a lone Haaland, with Anderson and Fernández holding in midfield.

Elliot Anderson, returning to the City Ground for the first time since his club-record £116m departure in July 2026, was unable to inspire a turnaround in front of his former supporters.

Title Race Implications

Three successive Premier League defeats — Aston Villa (Oct 24), Brighton (Oct 31) and now Forest — represent City's worst run of form since the 2023/24 season. Arsenal, who beat Liverpool 1-0 at Anfield on 1 November, remain on 27 points from 10 games and are now six points clear at the top. City sit second on 21 points (W7 D0 L3, GD +8).

Maresca's side must bounce back when Fulham visit the Etihad on Saturday 21 November (15:00 GMT), before a Champions League home game against Napoli on 24 November and a top-of-the-table trip to Arsenal on 28 November.
