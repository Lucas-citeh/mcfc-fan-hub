Semenyo Out for Up to Eight Weeks: Who Fills the Gap at Villa Park?

23 October 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Antoine Semenyo faces up to eight weeks on the sidelines after suffering a knee injury in last Saturday's 3-2 Premier League win over Ipswich Town. The blow means the Ghanaian winger is set to miss a significant stretch of City's packed fixture schedule — including Saturday's crucial trip to Villa Park.

Manager Enzo Maresca confirmed the news at his Monday press conference on 19 October, though he was keen to stress the scan results were better than feared. "The damage is collateral around the knee — it is not a major ligament rupture," Maresca said. "But we expect up to eight weeks." Reports from PA Media and Reuters suggest Semenyo's return window is December 2026, ruling him out of the Carabao Cup tie against Brighton (28 Oct), both Premier League fixtures against Brighton (31 Oct) and at Nottingham Forest (7 Nov), as well as the UCL home tie with Napoli (24 Nov) and the Arsenal trip (28 Nov).

Erling Haaland's Comeback

There was better news on Haaland, who took a knock to his foot and ankle late in the Ipswich win. The Norwegian came through Tuesday's 5-1 Champions League rout of AEK Athens from the bench, with Maresca managing his minutes carefully. Floyd Samba and Allan Elias were handed their first UCL starts — both impressed, with Samba causing havoc with his movement and Elias showing composure in tight areas. Phil Foden's hat-trick (34', 65', 76') stole the headlines, but the depth Maresca has at his disposal was plain to see.

The Villa Test

City travel to Villa Park on Saturday (11:30 BST, kick-off) as they seek to extend their perfect Premier League start to eight wins from eight. Unai Emery's Aston Villa are among the league's toughest hosts, and without Semenyo's direct pace and energy on the right flank, Maresca will need to find an alternative solution. Iliman Ndiaye, Jérémy Doku, and the 22-year-old Allan Elias are all options in wider areas. Rayan Cherki and Phil Foden remain central to everything City do going forward, while Haaland's fitness for the full ninety minutes at Villa Park is expected to be tested in training on Thursday and Friday.

Semenyo's Season So Far

Semenyo had made six appearances in all competitions before the injury, contributing two Premier League goals — both in the 5-3 win over Sunderland on 20 September, when he took his tally to two for the season. He was part of a fluid forward line alongside Haaland, Cherki, and Foden, and his direct running had established him as a key outlet in Maresca's system.

His absence for up to two months is a significant test of City's squad depth — though with Doku, Ndiaye, Elias, and Monga all available, Maresca has options. Whether one of them can replicate Semenyo's impact remains to be seen.
