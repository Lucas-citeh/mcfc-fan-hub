City Go Top: Maresca's Men Lead Premier League After Perfect Start

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 30 August 2026

A summer of upheaval. A new manager. A rebuilt squad. And six points from six. Manchester City sit at the top of the Premier League on 30 August 2026, ahead of every other club in England after the opening two matchdays — precisely the statement Enzo Maresca needed to make as he begins the daunting task of following Pep Guardiola into the Etihad dugout.

From Panic to Promise: How City's Summer Led Here

In the weeks after Guardiola's farewell, the mood around the Etihad was uncertain. Rodri departed for Barcelona. Bernardo Silva joined Real Madrid on a free. Tijjani Reijnders was sold to Saudi Arabia. Nico González went to Newcastle. Savinho followed just days after signing a new contract. City had lost the backbone of a squad that won six Premier League titles, a Champions League, and the Treble.

The counter-movement has been substantial. Elliot Anderson — the most expensive signing in Premier League history — arrived from Nottingham Forest for £116 million as the engine of a new midfield. Ayyoub Bouaddi, 18, was brought in from Lille for €100 million as the long-term successor to Rodri. Rayan Cherki had already been signed the previous summer, and the January additions of Marc Guehi and Antoine Semenyo had already proved their worth in the FA Cup run. The structure is different; the ambition is identical.

Game One: Etihad Comeback Against Bournemouth

Maresca's opening Premier League fixture on 23 August could not have been scripted more dramatically. Bournemouth, a side always difficult to break down under their own approach, took the lead through Marcus Tavernier after 26 minutes and led at half-time. City had struggled to impose themselves in the first half, with Foden and O'Reilly unable to find pockets of space in a deep Bournemouth block.

The decisive move came at the hour mark: Kovacic for Anderson and Cherki for O'Reilly. Cherki, in particular, transformed the game. It was his corner that Marc Guehi met at the back post in the 84th minute to make it 1-1, and moments later the Frenchman threaded the pass that sent Joško Gvardiol racing through to poke the ball home in stoppage time. VAR confirmed the goal was onside. City 2, Bournemouth 1. A debut PL win for Maresca under the Etihad lights.

Game Two: Selhurst Park Demolished

If the Bournemouth win was gritty, the trip to Selhurst Park five days later was a masterclass. Crystal Palace were overwhelmed 4-1 in a display that had City fans openly discussing the prospect of a genuine title challenge.

Erling Haaland headed Phil Foden's delivery home in the 17th minute, and the game was effectively over by the hour mark. Rayan Cherki's first City brace — a dazzling piece of skill and a clinical right-footed finish from outside the box — arrived either side of Crystal Palace's fortunate own goal, before Haaland drove into the near post in the 84th to complete a scoreline that flattered the hosts. Foden's two assists were a reminder of precisely why City signed their new contract extension to keep him at the club through 2030.

City have now scored six and conceded two in their opening two Premier League games, sitting atop the table with a goal difference of +4 — ahead of every other side in the division after two matchdays.

Who Is Scoring?

The early goal tallies are encouraging. Erling Haaland and Rayan Cherki are each on two Premier League goals, with Haaland also converting against Bournemouth. Marc Guehi and Joško Gvardiol — both centre-backs — are on the scoresheet too, a throwback to the Guardiola-era habit of defenders arriving for crucial moments. Phil Foden has not yet scored but leads the side with two assists from the Palace game.

The early-season absentee is Jeremy Doku, who has been sidelined since the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal and has featured in neither Premier League fixture. The Belgian winger's exact return timeline has not been announced.

What Comes Next

The transfer window closes at 23:00 BST on 1 September. Malo Gusto, Chelsea's French right-back, remains the outstanding target for Maresca's side, with personal terms reported as agreed but the clubs still apart on fee. City are reluctant to meet Chelsea's €87 million valuation. Whether a deal gets done before the window shuts is the main storyline heading into the final 48 hours.

On the pitch, newly promoted Coventry City visit the Etihad on Saturday 5 September for Matchday 3, before the first Manchester derby of the season at Old Trafford on 12 September. Both are winnable on current form.

The summer was chaotic. The start to the season has been anything but.
