International Break: O'Reilly Resting, Semenyo On Track — City Regroup Before Fulham

11 November 2026 · Blue Moonies Fan Hub

Manchester City have entered the November international break carrying the wounds of three consecutive Premier League defeats — their worst run under Enzo Maresca — but the fortnight off brings an opportunity to restore injured players and reset ahead of a pivotal eight-day spell.

O'Reilly resting back problem

Nico O'Reilly, who has been managing a back issue throughout much of the season, will rest and recuperate during the international window rather than join up with the England squad. The academy product has been playing through discomfort since suffering the problem in the warm-up ahead of the Coventry City Premier League fixture on 5 September; he missed four games in that initial recovery period before returning and has since been used sparingly. The November break offers the first sustained break from match duties since the problem first surfaced, and City hope a full rest will allow the 20-year-old to return fully fit when the Premier League resumes.

Semenyo on track for December return

Antoine Semenyo, who was ruled out for approximately eight weeks after sustaining a knee injury in the 3-2 win over Ipswich Town on 17 October, is understood to be progressing well in his rehabilitation. Manager Enzo Maresca confirmed the injury prognosis has not changed, with the Ghanaian forward's return still projected for mid-to-late December 2026. Semenyo has not travelled internationally during the break and is continuing work with City's medical team at the Etihad Campus.

Nunes and Kovacic assessed

Matheus Nunes and Mateo Kovacic were both absent for the defeat at Nottingham Forest on 7 November — Nunes through injury, Kovacic having missed the previous three weeks. City expect both to be available for the Fulham fixture on 21 November, though no official confirmation has been given. Their returns would significantly bolster Maresca's options in midfield and at right-back, areas where City have been stretched.

Maresca's reset plan

Speaking after the Forest defeat, the City boss was candid about the work ahead: "We're going to use this international break to try to clarify what we can do better." He acknowledged the swing from seven consecutive Premier League wins to three straight defeats required honest reflection, adding: "We won seven in a row. Now we have lost three in a row, so we need balance on that."

Maresca noted the November window would be the final pause in City's fixture schedule until March 2027, making it all the more important that players and staff use the time productively. City's coaching staff will focus on defensive transition — the area where Forest exposed them twice within the same structure, something Maresca described pointedly as "copy and paste" between the second and third Forest goals.

What's coming up

City face a crucial eight-day run upon the Premier League's resumption:

  • Sat 21 Nov — Fulham (H), Etihad Stadium, 15:00 GMT
  • Tue 24 Nov — Napoli (H), UEFA Champions League MD5, 20:00 GMT
  • Sat 28 Nov — Arsenal (A), Emirates Stadium, 15:00 GMT

The Arsenal fixture — a top-of-the-table clash with the team six points clear of City — is now being described by pundits as the most important game of Maresca's tenure so far. Arsenal beat City 3-0 in the Community Shield in August; closing that gap requires results to change immediately when the break ends.
