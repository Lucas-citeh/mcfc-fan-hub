City vs Brighton Today: Third Meeting in Seven Days as Maresca Eyes Title Cushion

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 31 October 2026

Manchester City host Brighton & Hove Albion at the Etihad Stadium this afternoon (15:00 GMT) in Premier League Matchweek 9 — the sides' third meeting in just seven days. It is a chance for City to pull clear of Arsenal at the top of the table; only goal difference currently separates the pair.

The Title Picture

Heading into today, City sit first in the Premier League on 21 points from eight games — a record of seven wins and one defeat. Arsenal, who beat Everton 2-0 on 26 October, are level on 21 points but trail City on goal difference (+8 vs City's +11). Three points this afternoon would give City a three-point cushion over their nearest rivals and send a statement to the rest of the division.

City's only dropped points came at Villa Park on 24 October, where Nicolas Jackson's brace gave Aston Villa a 2-1 victory. Before that, Enzo Maresca became the first manager in Premier League history to win his opening seven top-flight games. The City boss will be determined to rediscover that winning habit immediately.

Fresh From the Cup Thriller

Wednesday's Carabao Cup Round 4 tie against Brighton finished 2-2 at the Etihad after 90 minutes, with City advancing 4-3 on penalties. Gerónimo Rulli was the hero, saving spot-kicks from Yasin Ayari and Lewis Dunk before Rayan Cherki converted the winning penalty. Vitor Reis headed his first City goal to make it 2-2 late on. Both City and Brighton will be well-acquainted with each other's shape and personnel after three competitive fixtures in a week.

Rotation and the Squad Depth Test

The quick turnaround from Wednesday's cup tie means Maresca must manage his squad carefully. Key absentee Antoine Semenyo (knee, expected return December 2026) misses out again, and some players who featured heavily on Wednesday may be rested or rotated. Erling Haaland, who has 13 goals in all competitions this season, is expected to lead the attack. Rayan Cherki — scorer of the winning Carabao Cup penalty — will hope to add Premier League goals to his cup contributions.

Jeremy Doku, Phil Foden, Iliman Ndiaye and Allan Elias all provide Maresca with wide attacking options to cover for Semenyo's absence. Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernández are likely to anchor the midfield after the more rotated cup lineup.

Upcoming Schedule

After today, City travel to the City Ground to face Nottingham Forest on 7 November — a return for Elliot Anderson to the club he left for a record £116m in July 2026. November then brings Fulham at home (21 Nov), Napoli in the Champions League (24 Nov), and the top-of-the-table clash away to Arsenal (28 Nov). The Carabao Cup quarter-final against Bradford City falls in December.
