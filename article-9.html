International Break: Fitness Tracker & Liverpool Preview

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 23 September 2026

Manchester City enter the September/October international break as Premier League leaders, five wins from five and three points clear at the top. The next fixture is a trip to Anfield on Sunday 11 October — and manager Enzo Maresca will be hoping to have key absentees back to full fitness by then.

The Injury & Suspension Picture

Phil Foden (suspended): The Stockport-born midfielder is serving the third and final game of his three-match domestic suspension following his straight red card against Manchester United on 13 September. The ban covers Norwich City (Carabao Cup, 17 Sep), Sunderland (PL, 20 Sep) and Liverpool at Anfield (PL, 11 Oct). Foden will be unavailable for the Anfield showdown itself, meaning Maresca must navigate the club's sternest test of the season without one of City's most creative players. He should be available from the following Premier League fixture.

Jeremy Doku (fit — returned vs Sunderland): The Belgian winger returned from his calf injury as a second-half substitute in the 75th minute of the 5-3 win over Sunderland on 20 September — his first appearance since the Community Shield on 16 August. He played 15 minutes and contributed directly to the build-up for Haaland's 81st-minute winner. With the international break giving him further time to build match sharpness, Doku is expected to be available for the trip to Anfield on 11 October. Sources: mancity.com (team news — 20 Sep 2026); esteemedkompany.com ('Jeremy Doku finally returned from injury for Manchester City' — 23 Sep 2026); mancitysquare.com (Doku injury update).

Nico O'Reilly (back): The academy graduate suffered a back injury during the warm-up ahead of the Coventry City Premier League fixture on 5 September. Maresca confirmed the injury is not expected to be serious. O'Reilly missed the UCL opener at Porto (8 Sep), the Manchester derby at Old Trafford (13 Sep), the Carabao Cup win over Norwich (17 Sep) and the Sunderland fixture (20 Sep). He is expected to be in contention for a return during the international break period ahead of Liverpool. Sources: RotoWire ('Nico O'Reilly Injury: Back injury not expected to be serious'); sportsdunia.com (injury update, 5 Sep 2026); bet365 News (O'Reilly injury timeline).

Players on International Duty

A large contingent of City's multinational squad are representing their countries during the break. Erling Haaland is with Norway for their 2030 World Cup qualification campaign, while Gianluigi Donnarumma represents Italy, Josko Gvardiol and Mateo Kovacic are with Croatia, Ruben Dias is in the Portugal squad, and Rayan Ait-Nouri is involved with Algeria. Elliot Anderson and Marc Guehi are both part of the England setup.

With the break spanning several weeks, all players are expected to return to the City Football Academy no later than the week of 6 October, giving Maresca adequate preparation time ahead of Anfield.

Liverpool at Anfield — A First Test of City's Title Credentials

City have not yet dropped a league point this season. Liverpool — managed by Andoni Iraola — represent arguably the sternest test of the opening weeks. The Reds sit in the lower half of the early standings but are well capable of raising their level for a home fixture of this magnitude.

The match kicks off at 16:30 BST on Sunday 11 October at Anfield, live on Sky Sports. City will be without Foden, and Doku and O'Reilly remain doubts. On current form, Maresca's side — with Haaland, Cherki, Anderson and Fernández in brilliant touch — will arrive at Merseyside as favourites. Whether they can extend their perfect start to six wins from six remains to be seen.
