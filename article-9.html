Match Day — City at Anfield: Six-Pointer With Perfect Record on the Line

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 11 October 2026

Manchester City travel to Anfield this afternoon (kick-off 16:30 BST) with a perfect Premier League record: five wins from five, 15 points, and a goal difference of +8 — three clear at the summit. It is the biggest test yet of Enzo Maresca's unbeaten start. Foden is definitely out; O'Reilly is confirmed fit; Liverpool carry injury doubts heading into one of the season's first genuine six-pointers.

What Is at Stake

City can extend their perfect start to six Premier League wins and move six points clear of the chasing pack with a win. A City victory would also mean they have won four successive league meetings with Liverpool at Anfield — something no visiting side has managed in the modern era of the rivalry.

For Andoni Iraola's Liverpool, defeat would leave them 12 points off the top with only five games played — a gap that would make sustaining a title challenge extremely difficult. The Reds sit 7th on 9 points (W3 D0 L2), having lost their opening two away games to Crystal Palace and Everton.

City Team News — Confirmed Absences and Returns

Phil Foden (OUT — suspended & injured): Foden will play no part at Anfield. He is serving the third and final match of his three-game ban (red card against Manchester United, 13 September) and simultaneously recovering from appendicitis surgery confirmed during the international break. He was operated on in London; no firm return date has been issued but the PSG Champions League tie on Wednesday (14 October) remains a possibility, as his domestic ban does not apply to UEFA competition.

Nico O'Reilly (AVAILABLE — confirmed fit): O'Reilly has been named in the England squad for October's international fixtures — confirmation that he has fully recovered from the back injury sustained in the warm-up before the Coventry City match on 5 September. He missed four matches (Porto UCL, Manchester derby, Carabao Cup vs Norwich, Sunderland PL) during his absence. He is available for selection today. Sources: fourfourtwo.com (Oct 2026).

Jeremy Doku (available): Returned as a substitute against Sunderland (20 September) after his Community Shield calf injury and had the full international break to build match sharpness. He should be in contention at Anfield.

Liverpool Doubts — Isak and Gakpo

Liverpool's attacking options have been hit by the international break. Alexander Isak withdrew from the Sweden squad with a minor thigh problem and missed both of Sweden's fixtures. Cody Gakpo was substituted off for the Netherlands against Serbia with an ankle injury and confirmed absent for the rest of the break — both are doubts for today. Liverpool's broader injury list also includes Conor Bradley, Giovanni Leoni, Federico Chiesa and Vitezslav Jaros. Sources: Sky Sports (Liverpool injury reports — Oct 2026); 101GreatGoals (Oct 2026).

City's Form and Goalscorers

City have scored 13 goals and conceded five in their five Premier League games so far. Erling Haaland leads the scoring charts with five goals (seven in all competitions), including his record-breaking 81st-minute winner against Sunderland that made him the first player to score against all 25 Premier League teams he has faced. Rayan Cherki has contributed three PL goals and Enzo Fernández already has a league goal and an assist from his first four City appearances.

Off-Field Context

City arrive under significant off-field scrutiny following the independent commission's guilty verdict on all Premier League financial charges on 29 September. The club lodged a comprehensive appeal on 1 October, branding the decision "unsafe." Sanctions are yet to be determined and will not be finalised until after the appeal process concludes. The verdict has no immediate on-field impact. Sources: Al Jazeera (29 Sep 2026); mancity.com (1 Oct 2026).

Up Next — PSG at the Etihad (Wednesday)

Win or lose today, City return to action just three days later for UEFA Champions League Matchday 2 at the Etihad, when Paris Saint-Germain — the reigning European champions — visit Manchester. That fixture kicks off at 20:00 BST on Wednesday 14 October.
