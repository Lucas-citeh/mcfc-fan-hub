RB Leipzig 2-3 Manchester City: Haaland's Late Winner Keeps City Third in UCL

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 4 November 2026

Erling Haaland's 78th-minute header settled a pulsating Champions League Matchday 4 fixture as Manchester City twice came from behind to beat RB Leipzig 3-2 at the Red Bull Arena on Wednesday, 4 November 2026.

Three Leads, Three Levellers — City Edge a Thriller

It was a night of drama in Saxony. Castello Lukeba turned a City cross into his own net in the 37th minute to give Enzo Maresca's side the lead, but Ridle Baku capitalised on a lapse in City's defensive shape to restore parity for the home side just four minutes after the restart.

Enzo Fernández, who had been City's most dynamic midfielder in the first half, restored the lead in the 58th minute — driving forward from deep and curling a low effort inside the far post for his first Champions League goal in sky blue. The Argentine World Cup winner had earlier been involved in the build-up to the own goal, and his composure in front of goal showed why City made him their most expensive signing this summer.

Leipzig refused to be beaten, however. Antonio Nusa cut in from the left and found the bottom corner in the 61st minute to make it 2-2 and silence the City bench. For a few minutes it looked as though Leipzig might even go on to win, with Enzo Maresca reportedly unhappy with City's high line in the first ten minutes of the second half.

Haaland Has the Final Say

It was left to Erling Haaland to settle the tie, as it so often is. The Norwegian timed his run to perfection in the 78th minute, meeting a right-sided delivery with a clinical downward header that left Leipzig goalkeeper Peter Gulacsi with no chance. It was Haaland's 13th career goal against RB Leipzig — a remarkable record against a team he seems to relish facing — and his 14th in all competitions for Manchester City this season.

Maresca said afterwards he was "happy with the three points" while acknowledging there were moments when City were too open in transition. "Leipzig are a dangerous team. We knew that. We need to be more compact when we don't have the ball, but the character the players showed to come back twice was very good," he told reporters.

UCL Standings and What's Next

The result moved City to third in the Champions League league phase standings with three wins from four games (P4 W3 D0 L1). With Napoli visiting the Etihad in Matchday 5 on 24 November, City have a strong opportunity to consolidate their position in the top eight and avoid the knockout round play-offs.

Three days after the Leipzig win, City travelled to Nottingham Forest in the Premier League — where a 3-1 defeat extended their domestic wobble to three straight losses. The European campaign, however, remains in excellent health.
