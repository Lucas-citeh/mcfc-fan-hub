Opta Gives Liverpool 43% Chance as Anfield Showdown Approaches

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 6 October 2026

Opta's supercomputer has handed Liverpool a 43% chance of victory in Sunday's Premier League showdown at Anfield (kick-off 16:30 BST), with Manchester City rated at 30.3% and the draw at 26.7%. Despite City's perfect start — five wins from five and three points clear at the top — home advantage at one of the Premier League's most formidable grounds is a significant factor in Opta's model.

The Numbers

Opta's model reflecting Liverpool's home dominance in this fixture. The Reds have lost just once in their last 22 Premier League home matches against Manchester City, recording 14 wins and seven draws in that period. Anfield has proved a particularly difficult destination for visiting sides in the opening months of this campaign, underlining why the supercomputer gives the home side an edge despite City's superior league position.

City arrive as leaders on 15 points from five games — a goal difference of +8 — but will be operating under considerable off-field pressure in the wake of the independent commission's guilty verdict on 29 September and the subsequent appeal lodged by the club on 1 October.

Team News: Foden Definitely Out

Phil Foden (OUT — suspended & injured): Foden will play no part at Anfield — he is serving the third and final match of his three-game ban from the red card against Manchester United on 13 September, and is also recovering from appendicitis surgery confirmed during the international break. City confirmed he underwent the operation in London during the break; the NHS recommends a minimum two-week recovery after an appendectomy. No firm return date has been issued.

Nico O'Reilly (watch): The academy midfielder has been sidelined since suffering a back injury during the warm-up ahead of the Coventry City match on 5 September. Maresca confirmed the injury is not considered serious, and O'Reilly has been targeting a return ahead of this fixture. His involvement on Sunday is not confirmed but the signs are cautiously positive.

Jeremy Doku (fit): The Belgian winger returned from his Community Shield calf injury as a substitute against Sunderland on 20 September and has had the full international break to build fitness. He should be available for Anfield.

Liverpool's Form

Andoni Iraola's Liverpool sit 7th on 9 points from five games (W3 D0 L2) heading into this match. Defeats against Crystal Palace and Everton have left them six points off the pace, but Anfield form and the weight of the historical record against City give them reason for optimism. City's last win at Anfield in the Premier League was a rare result; recent history firmly favours the home side.

City's Outlook

Maresca's side will look to Erling Haaland — who has scored five Premier League goals in five games, including the record-breaking goal against all 25 teams he has faced — plus the form of Rayan Cherki, Enzo Fernández and Elliot Anderson to extend the unbeaten start. The trip to Anfield is the first real test of whether this City side can sustain a title challenge across a full and demanding season.

With Foden out and O'Reilly's fitness uncertain, Maresca may call on Mateo Kovacic to start alongside Anderson and Fernández in midfield, with Ayyoub Bouaddi a potential option from the bench. Iliman Ndiaye and Antoine Semenyo provide wide options.
