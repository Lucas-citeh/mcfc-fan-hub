Brighton Twice in Four Days: City's Biggest Rotation Test of the Season

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 24 October 2026

After today's trip to Villa Park, Manchester City return to the Etihad for a gruelling double-header against Brighton & Hove Albion: the Carabao Cup Round 4 on Wednesday 28 October (19:30 BST) and then a Premier League meeting on Saturday 31 October (15:00 GMT). Four days, same opponent, maximum stakes in both competitions.

The Cup First

City enter the Carabao Cup Round 4 as holders, having won the trophy in 2025/26 under Pep Guardiola. Enzo Maresca's side cruised through Round 3 with a 5-0 demolition of Norwich City on 17 September — a match that handed debut goals to academy prospect Floyd Samba (twice) and summer signing Allan Elias, and confirmed that Maresca is happy to ring the changes in cup football.

Brighton have made the Carabao Cup a priority of their own season, and the Seagulls will arrive at the Etihad having had an extra day's preparation. It will be no walkover. City's young and rotated side will need to be at their best.

Then the League

Seventy-two hours later, the Premier League demands City's full attention again. Brighton visit the Etihad for Matchweek 9 on 31 October — City's second home league match in five days. The fixture represents a chance to extend what may still be a perfect top-flight record, but it also highlights the challenge of managing a 25-man squad across four competitions.

The Injury Problem

City's rotation options are complicated by the absence of Antoine Semenyo, who faces up to eight weeks out after picking up a knee injury against Ipswich Town on 17 October. Confirmed by manager Enzo Maresca on 19 October, Semenyo's expected return is December 2026 — ruling him out of the entire Brighton double-header and much of November.

Maresca will look to Allan Elias, Jeremy Monga, and Jeremy Doku — who returned from a calf injury against Sunderland on 20 September — to fill the wide attacking positions across both Brighton ties. Iliman Ndiaye, signed from Everton in September, provides further cover and has impressed in his early appearances.

The Squad Depth Question

City's summer spending has given Maresca genuine options in every position. The midfield depth — Anderson, Fernández, Bouaddi, Kovacic, Foden and O'Reilly — means wholesale changes are possible without a significant drop in quality. Gerónimo Rulli will likely start in goal on Wednesday, as he did in the Norwich League Cup victory, while Vitor Reis, Rico Lewis and Matheus Nunes can rotate into the back four.

Floyd Samba and Allan Elias are expected to get further cup minutes. The 17-year-old Samba in particular has made a strong impression — his double on debut against Norwich, described as "mind-blowing" by the player himself, has made the case for regular involvement.

What to Expect

The Carabao Cup tie is effectively an audition for fringe players to stake a first-team claim. The Premier League match three days later will likely see a return to closer to Maresca's strongest available side, with Brighton's Premier League form making it a fixture City will want to take seriously.

For now, City fans can enjoy what has been an extraordinary start to the 2026/27 campaign under their new manager. Two Brighton wins in four days — cup and league — would round off the month in the kind of style that is beginning to feel routine at the Etihad.
