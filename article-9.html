Three Doubts Cloud City's Forest Bounce-Back as Nunes, O'Reilly and Kovacic Assessed

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 2 November 2026

Manchester City travel to Nottingham Forest on Saturday (7 November, 15:00 GMT) looking to arrest a run of back-to-back Premier League defeats — but Enzo Maresca will need to assess the fitness of three first-team players ahead of the trip to the City Ground.

Injury Concerns

Matheus Nunes is rated doubtful with physical discomfort, while Nico O'Reilly — who had recently returned from a back injury — is also listed as doubtful with a recurrence of that same concern. Mateo Kovacic is similarly uncertain after picking up a knock. Antoine Semenyo remains the only confirmed absentee, continuing his recovery from the knee injury he sustained in the 3-2 win over Ipswich Town on 17 October; Enzo Maresca has indicated an expected return in December 2026.

On the Forest side, Igor Jesus (calf) and Nicolò Savona (knee) are both unavailable for the home side.

The Bounce-Back Mission

City head to the City Ground having suffered consecutive Premier League defeats — 2-1 at Aston Villa on 24 October and 0-1 at home to Brighton on 31 October. Despite those setbacks, City remain top of the Premier League on 21 points (W7 D0 L2, GD +10), though Arsenal — the Community Shield winners who face them at the Emirates on 28 November — could draw level or leapfrog them depending on their own results this weekend.

Saturday's fixture carries a subplot of its own: Elliot Anderson returns to the City Ground for the first time since his record-breaking £116m move to Manchester City in July 2026. The box-to-box midfielder has settled quickly in sky blue, scoring his first City goal in the 5-1 Champions League win over AEK Athens on 20 October.

Wider Context

City's November schedule remains demanding: Fulham at home on 21 November, Napoli in the Champions League on 24 November, and then the top-of-the-table Premier League trip to Arsenal on 28 November. Getting points on the board at the City Ground is essential if City are to maintain their grip on first place through a congested fixture list.
