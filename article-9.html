Match Day Preview: City vs Ipswich Town — Back to League Business After UCL Setback

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 15 October 2026

Manchester City host Ipswich Town at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday 17 October (15:00 BST) in Premier League Matchday 7. Enzo Maresca's side will be desperate to return to winning ways after Wednesday's chastening 4-2 Champions League defeat to PSG — a result that ended their unbeaten run in Europe. The Premier League leaders have not lost in the league all season.

City's Standing

Despite Wednesday's UCL setback, City's Premier League position is commanding. Six wins from six games — including the 3-0 triumph at Anfield on 11 October — has them six points clear at the top with 18 points from six matches. A win on Saturday extends that lead and restores momentum ahead of a busy October.

Erling Haaland, with six Premier League goals already, leads the division's scoring charts. Rayan Cherki (four PL goals) and Antoine Semenyo (two) have also contributed. The squad's depth — with summer signings Enzo Fernández, Ayyoub Bouaddi, Allan Elias and Iliman Ndiaye all settling in — gives Maresca excellent options from bench and start.

Team News

Phil Foden's three-match domestic ban is now fully served (Norwich EFL Cup, Sunderland PL, Liverpool PL). However, the England international is still recovering from appendicitis surgery carried out during the international break, and his availability for Saturday will depend on his recovery progress. No firm return date has been given by the club.

Jeremy Doku (calf) has made two substitute appearances since returning against Sunderland on 20 September and should be available from the bench. Nico O'Reilly is fit after his back injury kept him out for several weeks earlier in the season.

About Ipswich Town

Ipswich Town were promoted to the Premier League for the 2026/27 season. They make their first Etihad visit under new Premier League conditions and will look to be organised and hard to break down. The gap in quality and resources between the sides is significant, and City will be expected to win comfortably at home.

Key Numbers

  • City Premier League record 2026/27: P6 W6 D0 L0 — 18 points, 1st place (+11 GD)
  • Haaland: 10 goals in all competitions (6 PL, 4 UCL including the PSG brace)
  • City have won their last 6 Premier League home games
  • Ipswich are making their first visit to the Etihad this season

Kick-off: Saturday 17 October 2026, 15:00 BST, Etihad Stadium.
