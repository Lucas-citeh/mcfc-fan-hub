Manchester City Found Guilty of All Premier League Financial Charges — Appeal Lodged

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 3 October 2026

Manchester City have been found guilty of all charges relating to serious breaches of Premier League financial rules, an independent commission announced on Monday 29 September 2026. The club lodged a comprehensive appeal on 1 October, branding the verdict "unsafe" and maintaining their innocence.

The Verdict

After several years of legal proceedings — the longest and most complex case in Premier League history — an independent commission ruled on 29 September that Manchester City had committed serious breaches of the league's financial rules between the 2009-10 and 2017-18 seasons.

The commission found that City had arranged what it described as "sham" commercial contracts with a number of partners as part of a "disguised funding scheme" that artificially inflated the club's revenues and reduced costs by more than £900 million over the nine-year period.

The Premier League released a statement confirming the verdict, while the club said they were "both disappointed and surprised" by the opinion.

Manchester City's Response

City moved swiftly to contest the decision. On 1 October, the club lodged a comprehensive appeal against the independent commission's opinion, stating it "contains clear material errors of law, principle and fact" and describing the ruling as "unsafe."

The club maintained their position of innocence, as they have throughout the process. Former manager Pep Guardiola — who departed the Etihad at the end of the 2025/26 season — publicly sent a message of support to the club.

What Happens Next

Sanctions — which could include a points deduction, fine, or other penalties — are to be decided in a separate hearing by the independent commission. Those deliberations cannot begin until the appeal process is resolved. Football finance analyst Kieran Maguire has indicated that, given the scale of the breaches found by the commission, any sanction is likely to be significant.

For the time being, the club's on-field status remains unchanged. Manchester City currently sit top of the Premier League on 15 points from five games under new head coach Enzo Maresca. All results from the current 2026/27 season were achieved after the period covered by the charges.

Timeline of the Case

  • February 2023: Premier League refer 115 charges of alleged financial rule breaches to an independent commission.
  • 2023–2026: Proceedings conducted over multiple years, involving extensive legal arguments from both sides.
  • 29 September 2026: Independent commission delivers guilty verdict on all charges.
  • 1 October 2026: Manchester City lodge formal appeal, contesting the verdict as "unsafe."
  • TBC: Appeal hearing date and sanctions hearing to be scheduled.
