Manchester City 5-1 AEK Athens: Foden Hat-Trick Fires City to UCL Bounce-Back

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 20 October 2026

Manchester City delivered a statement of intent in the UEFA Champions League on Tuesday evening, dismantling AEK Athens 5-1 at the Etihad Stadium in Matchday 3 of the league phase. The result was defined by Phil Foden, who claimed a stunning hat-trick — ending a goal drought stretching back nearly ten months — while Elliot Anderson opened and Rayan Cherki added a fifth to send City surging back to winning ways in Europe.

Anderson Sets the Tone Early

Enzo Maresca opted to keep faith with a strong lineup, starting Foden alongside Cherki and Semenyo behind Haaland in a fluid front four. The opening goal came in the 12th minute when Elliot Anderson, ever-present in City's engine room, drove forward from midfield and delivered a close-range finish after a quickly worked set piece. It was a goal that encapsulated Anderson's impact this season — direct, purposeful and clinical in the final third.

City continued to press, creating a series of half-chances before the interval without adding to the tally. Foden was lively throughout the first half, making runs in behind and inviting fouls, but the real fireworks were still to come.

Foden's Drought-Ending Hat-Trick

Phil Foden had not scored for City in any competition since December 2025 — a period that included a red card against Manchester United, a domestic ban, and a spell out through appendicitis surgery. There was nothing cautious about his return to the scoresheet. In the 34th minute he finished crisply inside the area to make it 2-0, and although AEK's Stavros Pilios pulled one back in the 52nd minute to briefly set nerves jangling, Foden responded immediately.

Rayan Cherki restored the two-goal cushion in the 57th minute with a typical left-footed effort that flew into the top corner. But the night truly belonged to Foden. He completed his hat-trick in the 65th and 76th minutes with two more composed finishes — the second a driven effort from the edge of the area that Sofascore rated as the pick of the bunch. The Etihad erupted as he raised the match ball aloft: Foden was back, and in the best possible fashion.

A Message Sent

The 5-1 scoreline was a pointed response to last week's 4-2 home defeat to PSG and underlined the quality Maresca's side retain even without a fully fit Haaland for extended spells. The Norwegian started and was involved in the build-up play, but with Foden on fire there was no need to overburden him — a sensible precaution with Aston Villa arriving at Villa Park in just four days.

City now sit at W2 D0 L1 in the Champions League league phase, level on points with the top eight but with a goal difference that has improved dramatically. The focus now turns to Saturday's Premier League visit to Villa Park, where Maresca will be hoping to extend City's perfect domestic record to eight wins from eight.
