City vs AEK Athens: UCL Bounce-Back Bid — Haaland and Semenyo Both Fit to Start

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 20 October 2026

Manchester City host AEK Athens at the Etihad Stadium tonight (20:00 BST) in UEFA Champions League League Phase Matchday 3. It is an opportunity to banish the memory of last week's 4-2 home defeat to Paris Saint-Germain, and pre-match reports confirm that both Erling Haaland and Antoine Semenyo are fit and available after their injury scares against Ipswich Town.

A Chance to Bounce Back

City go into tonight's fixture with a UCL record of one win and one defeat — the opening 2-0 victory away at Porto (8 September) followed by the sobering 4-2 reverse against defending champions PSG six days ago. In that match, Pep Guardiola — now in the opposing dugout — watched his PSG side race into a 3-0 half-time lead before Haaland pulled two back in the second half. City never really recovered.

Against AEK Athens, Enzo Maresca's side will expect to be comfortable. The Greek champions have struggled in their first two UCL outings and arrive at the Etihad without a league phase win to their name. Their key threats are frontman Barnabás Varga and attacking midfielder Razvan Marin, who have shared four league phase goals between them this season.

Team News: Haaland and Semenyo Available

The primary fitness concerns heading into this fixture centred on Haaland and Semenyo, both of whom left Saturday's 3-2 win over Ipswich Town with post-match knocks. Pre-match reporting confirms there are no fresh fitness concerns for either player ahead of tonight, and both are expected to feature. With Haaland on 12 goals in all competitions this season and Semenyo bringing energy and directness from wide positions, their availability is a major boost.

City played on Saturday and will travel to Villa Park for the Premier League on Friday — a punishing turnaround. Maresca is expected to manage minutes across the squad, with possible rotation in midfield and defensive positions. Ayyoub Bouaddi, who impressed in the Porto UCL win, is likely to keep his place alongside Enzo Fernández, while the likes of Rico Lewis, Gerónimo Rulli and Allan Elias may feature.

What Is at Stake in the UCL Table

Under the reformed UEFA Champions League format, all 36 clubs compete in a single league phase, with the top eight qualifying automatically for the round of 16. A win tonight would take City to six points from three games and keep them firmly within automatic qualification range. A defeat would leave them at three points with significant ground to make up — placing extra pressure on the remaining five league phase fixtures.

The wider picture is also relevant. City have conceded seven goals in their two UCL appearances so far (two against Porto, five against PSG). Donnarumma's clean sheet record in the competition is an area Maresca will want to address, and a controlled defensive performance against a limited AEK Athens side would serve as the ideal reset before the weekend trip to Villa Park.

Kickoff and Where to Watch

Manchester City vs AEK Athens kicks off at 20:00 BST (19:00 UTC) on Tuesday 20 October at the Etihad Stadium. The match is available live on TNT Sports in the UK. International broadcast listings are available via UEFA's official channel guide.
