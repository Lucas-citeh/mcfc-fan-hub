Four from Four: The Stats Behind City's Perfect Premier League Start

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 20 September 2026

Twelve points from twelve, eight goals scored, just two conceded, and a goal difference of +6 — as Manchester City prepare to host Sunderland at the Etihad on Sunday 20 September, Enzo Maresca's side have made the most promising start to a Premier League campaign in the club's history under the new regime.

The Numbers Don't Lie

Four wins from four. Bournemouth away 2-1 (late drama — Guehi 84', Gvardiol 90+2'); Crystal Palace away 4-1 (Cherki's first City brace, two from Haaland); Coventry City at home 1-0 (Haaland header, Donnarumma double save); Manchester United away 1-0 (Haaland again, VAR-confirmed, 10 men for 67 minutes). Whatever the circumstances, City have found a way to win. That is what champions do.

The goal record across all competitions reads even more impressively. In seven games — four PL, one Community Shield, one Champions League, one Carabao Cup — City have scored 17 goals in regulation time (not counting the Community Shield where Arsenal were dominant). Erling Haaland alone has contributed six across competitions: four in the Premier League and two in the Champions League opener at Porto.

Haaland: Still Unstoppable

Erling Haaland failed to score in the opening Premier League game — Bournemouth away, when Guehi and Gvardiol were the unlikely heroes — but the Norwegian has been relentless since. Two at Selhurst Park, one against Coventry, one at Old Trafford. He stands alone at the top of the Premier League scoring chart with four goals from four games. Add his double in the 0-2 win at Porto in Champions League Matchday 1, and Haaland's 2026/27 season is exactly on the trajectory his standards demand.

Cherki: The Early-Season Revelation

When Rayan Cherki signed from Lyon in summer 2025, the expectation was a patient development curve. Instead, the French playmaker has hit the ground running in his second City season. His brace at Crystal Palace — a dazzling footwork finish at 54' and a clinical right-footed strike from outside the box at 59' — signalled that Cherki is no longer a prospect but a genuine Premier League force. Add a goal against Norwich in the Carabao Cup and he already has three for the season across all competitions in just six appearances.

Anderson's Arrival, Bouaddi's Emergence

Elliot Anderson, the club-record £116m signing from Nottingham Forest, was City's best player at Old Trafford by some distance — recording the most touches of any player on the pitch despite City playing over an hour with ten men. His partnership with Enzo Fernández in the centre of the park offers a physical intensity and late-arriving dynamism that last season's midfield often lacked.

Ayyoub Bouaddi, at 18 years old, has already racked up four appearances. The Moroccan's Champions League debut at Porto was particularly eye-catching — composed in possession, aggressive in the press, and never overawed despite playing his first UCL match in a hostile atmosphere. The most expensive teenage signing in Premier League history is, so far, justifying the billing.

The International Break and What Comes Next

After today's Sunderland fixture, the Premier League pauses for the October international break. When club football resumes, City face their sternest test of the campaign so far: a trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on Sunday 11 October (16:30 BST). Phil Foden — suspended for today's game, the third and final match of his domestic ban from the Manchester derby red card — will be available again for the Liverpool fixture. His return to fitness and form will be watched closely in the coming weeks.

Twelve points from twelve. The new era is underway — and it has started perfectly.
