Derby Day Eve: O'Reilly Returns as Doku Ruled Out for Old Trafford

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 12 September 2026

Manchester City head to Old Trafford on Sunday with an injury boost: Nico O'Reilly is confirmed available for the 199th Manchester derby (16:30 BST, 13 September), while Jeremy Doku is ruled out with his ongoing calf complaint. Enzo Maresca's side go into the fixture top of the Premier League with nine points from three games.

O'Reilly Back, Doku Confirmed Out

The 20-year-old midfielder Nico O'Reilly is back in contention for Sunday's derby after missing both the Premier League win over Coventry City (5 September) and the 2-0 Champions League victory at FC Porto (8 September) with a back complaint. The academy product had dropped out of the Coventry matchday squad during the warmup and did not travel to Portugal, but has been passed fit ahead of the trip to Old Trafford.

Jeremy Doku remains sidelined, however. The Belgian winger has been absent since sustaining a calf injury during the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal on 16 August and has yet to feature in the Premier League or Champions League this season. He is confirmed unavailable for Sunday. Per Yahoo Sports / NBC Sports / ESPN (12 September 2026).

City's Predicted XI

Enzo Maresca is expected to line up in a 4-2-3-1 at Old Trafford. Gianluigi Donnarumma starts in goal behind a back four of Khusanov, Dias, Guehi and Gvardiol. Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernández are expected to form the double pivot in midfield, with Antoine Semenyo, Rayan Cherki and Phil Foden operating behind the striker. Erling Haaland leads the line.

Predicted Manchester City XI (4-2-3-1): Donnarumma; Khusanov, Dias (C), Guehi, Gvardiol; Anderson, Fernández; Semenyo, Cherki, Foden; Haaland.

Nico O'Reilly, Ayyoub Bouaddi, Mateo Kovacic and Iliman Ndiaye are all available and provide strong options from the bench. Haaland has scored five goals in all competitions this season; Cherki has two. The collective front four have been City's creative engine in an unbeaten start to the campaign.

United's Injury Situation

Manchester United host the derby without a number of key players. Matthijs de Ligt, Amad Diallo, Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte are all unavailable through injury. Luke Shaw, who missed United's midweek Champions League win through cramp, is expected to return at left-back, with Diogo Dalot at right-back. Bruno Fernandes captains the home side. Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans provide the midfield foundation.

Predicted Manchester United XI (4-2-3-1): Lammens; Dalot, Maguire, Martínez, Shaw; Mainoo, Tielemans; Mbeumo, Fernandes, Dorgu; Cunha.

Context and Stakes

City enter as clear favourites. Top of the Premier League with three wins from three, they travel to a United side sitting eleventh with four points after one win, one draw and one defeat. The 199th Manchester derby is Enzo Maresca's biggest fixture yet as City head coach, and a win would extend their perfect record to 12 points from four games — a powerful early-season statement.

The summer reconstruction of City's midfield — Rodri, Bernardo Silva, Reijnders and Nico Gonzalez all departed — has been absorbed impressively. Anderson's energy, Fernández's composure and Bouaddi's maturity from the bench give Maresca flexibility and quality across every stage of a game. The spine of the team — Donnarumma, Dias, Guehi, Anderson, Fernández, Haaland — looks formidably settled for a side only three months into a new managerial era.
