Carabao Cup R3: Holders City Face Norwich as Maresca Eyes Rotation

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 15 September 2026

Manchester City welcome Norwich City to the Etihad Stadium on Thursday 17 September (19:30 BST) in the Carabao Cup third round, as Enzo Maresca's holders begin their defence of the trophy they claimed as part of last season's domestic cup double.

The Opposition

Norwich City return to Premier League football in 2026/27 after winning the Championship last season, and their Carabao Cup campaign now takes them to the Etihad for the toughest possible draw at this stage. They arrive aware that City are expected to rotate significantly — providing a genuine opportunity for an upset that would be celebrated loudly at Carrow Road.

Team News

Phil Foden will miss Thursday's tie. The England midfielder received a straight red card in the 23rd minute of Sunday's 1-0 Manchester derby win at Old Trafford — a kick on United captain Bruno Fernandes, confirmed by VAR — and was handed a three-match domestic suspension. He will also miss the Premier League visit of Sunderland on Sunday 20 September and City's trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on 11 October.

Jeremy Doku also remains unavailable. The Belgian winger has been troubled by a calf complaint throughout the opening weeks of the season. Maresca indicated ahead of the derby that Doku "needs some more days", and his return date is uncertain — though is expected within the coming weeks rather than imminently. Sources: Sports Mole (injury tracker — 14 Sep 2026); mancity.com ('City v Norwich: Kick-off time and TV information').

Rotation Expected

With four Premier League victories already banked and the Sunderland home game following on Sunday, Maresca is expected to use the League Cup to hand minutes to members of the squad who have featured less prominently so far. Ayyoub Bouaddi, Jeremy Monga, Allan Elias and Nico O'Reilly are candidates for starts, as are players who have come on as substitutes in the opening Premier League run. The cup provides a competitive platform for newer faces to press their case.

City's squad depth was rebuilt significantly during the summer as Maresca oversaw the departure of four senior midfielders — Rodri, Bernardo Silva, Tijjani Reijnders and Nico González — and replaced them with Enzo Fernández, Elliot Anderson, Bouaddi and Kovacic taking on enlarged roles. Thursday is an early examination of whether that rebuilt group can perform cohesively across multiple competitions.

The Bigger Picture

City go into the tie top of the Premier League on 12 points — four wins from four — offering Maresca the luxury of rotating freely without anxiety about league position. A victory would put the holders into the Carabao Cup last 16.

The match will be shown live on Sky Sports+. Kick-off at the Etihad is 19:30 BST on Thursday 17 September 2026.
