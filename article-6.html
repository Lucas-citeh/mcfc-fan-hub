Can Perfect City Make It Five? Sunderland Visit the Etihad

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 19 September 2026

Manchester City host Sunderland at the Etihad Stadium on Sunday 20 September (14:00 BST, Sky Sports) in Premier League Matchday 5, looking to extend their perfect start under Enzo Maresca to five wins from five.

City's Momentum

Enzo Maresca's side go into this fixture on a high. After winning their first four Premier League games — against Bournemouth, Crystal Palace, Coventry and Manchester United — City demolished Norwich 5-0 in the Carabao Cup third round on Thursday night, with academy debutant Floyd Samba scoring twice and Allan Elias marking his first start with a goal. The result extended City's unbeaten run across all competitions to seven games since the season opened.

Erling Haaland leads the Premier League scoring chart with four goals, and City sit first in the table on 12 points — two ahead of Arsenal. Rayan Cherki has been a creative menace throughout the opening weeks, scoring a brace at Selhurst Park in August and adding a goal against Norwich in the cup.

Team News

Phil Foden remains suspended. The England midfielder is serving the second game of his three-match domestic ban after his red card against Manchester United on 13 September — he will also miss the trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on 11 October.

Jeremy Doku is a doubt. The Belgian winger has missed all four of City's opening Premier League games with a calf complaint, though Maresca has indicated he has not ruled the 24-year-old out of contention for the Sunderland match. A return to the squad — whether from the bench — remains possible. Sources: Sportskeeda (injury preview, 20 Sep 2026); Football Whispers (preview, 20 Sep 2026).

The Opposition

Sunderland arrive in Manchester 14th in the Premier League table with four points from their opening four games — one win, one draw, two defeats. Their most recent result was a 2-0 home loss to Arsenal on Matchday 4, goals from Bruno Guimarães and a Bukayo Saka penalty sending the Black Cats to defeat at the Stadium of Light.

Sunderland are without midfielder Mundle — a long-term absentee with a knee injury likely sidelining him until early 2027 — and forward Mandava, who is suspended. Their goal has been to stay organised on the road and threaten on the counter, but the Etihad is an especially daunting venue. Sources: Sportskeeda; Football Whispers (preview — 20 Sep 2026).

What to Expect

After using a 4-1-4-1 system against Coventry and a 4-3-3 at Old Trafford, Maresca may opt for a similar shape at home — with Anderson, Fernández and a recovered Kovacic as midfield options. Haaland leads the attack; Ndiaye, Semenyo and possibly Doku compete for wide roles. Sunday's game is a test of City's ability to assert dominance at home against a well-organised lower-half side — the sort of game where Maresca will want to avoid a sluggish display heading into the international break.

Live on Sky Sports. Kick-off 14:00 BST, Sunday 20 September 2026.
