Porto 0-2 City: Haaland Brace Opens UCL Campaign in Style

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 8 September 2026

Manchester City opened their 2026/27 Champions League campaign with a commanding 2-0 win at FC Porto on Tuesday evening, with Erling Haaland scoring twice at Estádio do Dragão. The Norwegian headed home from Enzo Fernández's cross in the 47th minute, then sealed the points with a composed right-footed finish from Rayan Ait-Nouri's cutback deep in stoppage time. It was Enzo Maresca's first European win as City head coach.

A Dominant Debut in Europe

City arrived in Portugal carrying a perfect Premier League record — nine points from nine — and the performance in Porto showed the depth and quality at Maresca's disposal. Despite an intimidating atmosphere at the Estádio do Dragão, City were composed throughout, controlling long stretches of the match and limiting Porto to zero shots on target before Haaland's decisive second in injury time.

The opening half saw Haaland wasteful on three occasions, with Porto goalkeeper Diogo Costa twice denying the Norwegian in the area and Rayan Cherki also seeing an effort saved on the rebound. City's patience, though, was ultimately rewarded.

Haaland Opens the Scoring

The deadlock was broken two minutes into the second half. Enzo Fernández played a precise cross from the right channel, and Haaland attacked the ball with typical authority, nodding emphatically past Diogo Costa to make it 0-1. It was a familiar goal for City fans — the service from midfield, the clinical finish from the striker — and a sign that the new-look side under Maresca carries the same attacking threat from set pieces and delivery that Pep Guardiola's teams relied upon.

Stoppage-Time Seal

After City took their foot off the gas in the second half, Porto grew in confidence and created some half-chances. But Maresca's side remained defensively disciplined and punished Porto on the break when substitute Rayan Ait-Nouri drove into the box down the left and squared for Haaland, who struck first-time with his right foot to finish into the bottom corner in the 90+2nd minute. City were the first team to win at Estádio do Dragão in 2026.

Bouaddi Stars on UCL Debut

If Haaland was the headline act, 18-year-old Ayyoub Bouaddi deserved special mention for a mature performance in his first Champions League appearance. The Moroccan, signed for £86m from Lille in August, showed the composure and positional intelligence that made him one of the world's most coveted teenagers. Maresca had flagged before the match that he wanted Bouaddi and Iliman Ndiaye to get Champions League experience: Bouaddi repaid that faith emphatically. "Patience was key," Bouaddi said post-match via mancity.com. "We knew the goal would come."

City's Starting XI and Substitutions

Manchester City (4-2-3-1): Donnarumma; Nunes, Dias (C), Guehi, Gvardiol; Fernández, Bouaddi; Semenyo, Cherki, Foden; Haaland.

Substitutes used: Ndiaye (62', for Semenyo); Ait-Nouri (76', for Gvardiol); Kovacic (76', for Bouaddi).

Noteworthy: Marc Guehi started rather than resting, and Enzo Fernández was preferred to Elliot Anderson in the double pivot. Nico O'Reilly (back) and Jeremy Doku (calf) did not travel.

Looking Ahead

City now face what Maresca called "the biggest game of the early season" — the Manchester derby at Old Trafford on Sunday 13 September (16:30 BST). O'Reilly is expected to be available; Doku's fitness remains uncertain. City sit first in the Premier League with nine points from three games and top of their UCL group at the first opportunity. Under Maresca, the early signs are emphatically positive.
