Rumours Summer 2026

  • Elliot Anderson
    From Nottingham Forest · NEW BID INCOMING (14–15 June): Fabrizio Romano confirmed City are "preparing a new official bid to be sent soon to Nottingham Forest" and are "confident to get the deal done" after leading the race since March. Man United remain keen but City are firmly ahead and optimistic (Romano, 14 June). The Hard Tackle also reported City "confident of sealing" the deal on 14 June — a significant shift from earlier reports suggesting City might walk away. City's second bid (£106m guaranteed + £15m add-ons, ~£121m total) was rejected on 10 June; Forest owner Marinakis insists on a guaranteed flat fee above £120m with no add-on structures. Anderson has agreed personal terms in principle (five-year deal, per Schira) and England manager Tuchel has cleared a World Cup medical. Fabrizio Romano names Sandro Tonali (Newcastle) as City's Plan B if talks collapse.
    Per Fabrizio Romano on X (City preparing new official bid, confident to get deal done, 14 June 2026); The Hard Tackle (City confident of sealing Anderson deal, 14 June 2026); BBC Sport Saturday gossip (City prefer Anderson to Wharton, 13 June 2026); Sky Sports Transfer Centre (second bid rejected, 10 June 2026); CaughtOffside / Vanguard (£106m + £15m rejected; Forest flat-fee demand, 10 June 2026); ESPN (British record bid, June 2026); The Athletic — David Ornstein (opening bid rejected, 3 June 2026); Nicolo Schira (personal terms in principle, five-year deal); Fabrizio Romano (Tonali Plan B, June 2026)
  • Josko Gvardiol
    To Real Madrid (potential OUT) · TALKS HALTED (11 June): Gvardiol has pressed pause on contract renewal negotiations with City, per Diario AS and Florian Plettenberg (Sky Sports DE) — the decisive catalyst being Jose Mourinho's confirmed appointment as Real Madrid head coach (three-year deal to June 2029). Mourinho has made Gvardiol a personal priority signing. Real Madrid had a concrete offer on the table (Plettenberg, 10 June). City have upgraded their contract proposal to 2031 — extended from an initial 2030 offer — with improved salary terms. Bayern Munich are 100% out of the running. City retain internal belief they can keep him; Gvardiol faces a straight choice between Etihad renewal or the Bernabeu. (Update 13 June): CityXtra reports that Real Madrid and Barcelona are monitoring the situation but applying no immediate deadline pressure — both are content to wait for Gvardiol's own decision. City retain strong internal belief that their 2031 contract offer will ultimately be accepted, with a resolution expected once Croatia's World Cup campaign concludes.
    Per Florian Plettenberg / Sky Sports DE (Real Madrid concrete offer on table, 10 June; Gvardiol halts renewal talks, 11 June 2026); Diario AS (Gvardiol presses pause on renewal, 11 June 2026); Sky Sports (Mourinho confirmed as Real Madrid head coach, targets Gvardiol, June 2026); CityXtra / Yahoo Sports (City's improved contract to 2031, 11–12 June 2026); CityXtra (City internal retention belief, 12 June 2026); GoonerDaily (City extension + club option, 10 June 2026); CityXtra (Real Madrid & Barcelona stance amid renewal talks progress, 13 June 2026)
  • Tijjani Reijnders
    To Atletico Madrid (OUT) · Sky Sports reports Manchester City are considering Reijnders' future under incoming head coach Enzo Maresca, with Atletico Madrid having opened talks with City over the Dutch midfielder. Reijnders only joined from AC Milan in summer 2025 but fell down the pecking order after a strong start — starting just 27 of a possible 38 PL matches. Atletico are understood to be preparing a formal offer; City are not actively pushing a sale but have not ruled one out given the midfield competition Maresca's rebuild will bring. Fee likely to be in the €60–70m range.
    Per Sky Sports News (City weighing Reijnders future, May–June 2026); CaughtOffside — Atletico open talks, prepare offer (4 June 2026); Football España (Atletico open talks with City, 29 May 2026)
  • Rodri
    Contract concern (potential exit) · Entering the final year of his deal (expires summer 2027); Romano reported a "significant gap" between City's offer and Rodri's demands, though subsequent reports suggest the two sides are not far from agreement. Rodri has now confirmed the issue will wait: "If there hadn't been a World Cup, things might be different now." But more recent reports (May–June 2026) suggest City are nearing a renewal — Rodri told TNT Sports he is "very calm" about his future and City sources say the club want him to "stay, stay, stay"; Real Madrid have not yet taken official steps regarding Rodri (CityXtra). Jose Mourinho's confirmed appointment as Real Madrid head coach remains a potential complicating factor if renewal stalls.
    Per Fabrizio Romano (significant gap in talks, June 2026); ESPN / Yardbarker (Rodri to wait until after World Cup, direct quote, June 2026); OneFootball / CityXtra (City nearing Rodri renewal, late May–June 2026); TNT Sports (Rodri 'very calm', direct quote, June 2026); CityXtra (Real Madrid not taken official steps on Rodri, June 2026)
