Match Preview

Maresca's City Must Rediscover UCL Form Against Napoli

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 22 November 2026

Manchester City host Napoli in Champions League League Phase Matchday 5 at the Etihad on Tuesday 24 November (20:00 GMT). With four Premier League games without a win, Enzo Maresca will be desperate for his side to restore confidence in Europe — and Haaland's return to form is the clearest reason for optimism.

City's UCL Campaign So Far

Despite the turbulence in the Premier League — four games without a win and a worrying defensive fragility on the counter — City have been considerably more reliable in Europe. Their Champions League record heading into Matchday 5 reads: W3 D0 L1, nine points from four games. The sequence: a 2-0 win at Porto, a 2-4 loss at home to PSG, a 5-1 demolition of AEK Athens and a nervy but deserved 3-2 comeback win at RB Leipzig.

That Leipzig win was particularly significant. Coming from a goal down twice and finding a winner through Haaland's 78th-minute header, it showcased the mentality City have shown in Europe this season — an ability to absorb adversity and deliver when it matters. Maresca will hope to channel that spirit against Napoli.

Haaland: Europe's Favourite Stage

Erling Haaland has 14 goals across all competitions this season — nine in the Premier League and five in the Champions League. The Norwegian has consistently been City's most decisive player in Europe, and home UCL nights at the Etihad remain his preferred environment. He came on as a substitute in the AEK Athens rout, contributing without scoring, but his two-goal performance in both Porto and PSG — even in defeat against the latter — underlined his perpetual threat. A win on Tuesday, against an Etihad side that has struggled since the international break, begins with Haaland.

Key Absences and Selection Questions

Antoine Semenyo (knee) remains out and is estimated to return in December. That means Iliman Ndiaye and Jeremy Doku are likely to provide the wide attacking threat alongside Haaland, with Rayan Cherki offering a creative option off the bench or from the start. Phil Foden showed his best form against Porto and in the hat-trick performance against AEK Athens, and a European outing could be the ideal moment for him to rediscover his best.

In midfield, Maresca faces a selection decision between the experience of Mateo Kovacic and the high-energy pressing of Ayyoub Bouaddi alongside Elliot Anderson. Anderson's late-season form in the Premier League — culminating in his equaliser against Fulham on Saturday — gives him a strong case to retain his place. Rayan Ait-Nouri, absent in recent weeks, may be assessed ahead of kick-off; Nico O'Reilly returned from his back issue to start the Fulham draw, adding an option at left back.

Looking Ahead: Arsenal on Saturday

Maresca and City will have one eye on the Arsenal fixture at the Emirates this coming Saturday (28 November, 15:00 GMT). Arsenal lead the Premier League and beat City 3-0 in the Community Shield in August; Saturday's clash is already being billed as the most significant Premier League result of the season for City. Getting a result against Napoli on Tuesday — and the momentum that comes with it — may well set the tone.

Key Facts

  • Fixture: Manchester City vs Napoli — UEFA Champions League Matchday 5
  • Venue: Etihad Stadium, Manchester
  • Kick-off: Tuesday 24 November 2026 · 20:00 GMT
  • City UCL record: P4 W3 D0 L1, 9 pts
  • City PL form: Winless in last 4 (W7 D1 L3 overall, 22 pts, 3rd)
