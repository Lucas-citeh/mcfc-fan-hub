Rulli Official, Haaland Back: City's Community Shield Squad Takes Shape

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 13 August 2026

Wednesday 12 August was a day of quiet but meaningful progress for Enzo Maresca's Manchester City. Gerónimo Rulli was confirmed as the club's fifth summer signing. Squad numbers for four players — Elliot Anderson, Marc Guehi, Gianluigi Donnarumma and Rulli himself — were officially assigned for 2026/27. And mancity.com's training gallery documented the return of six World Cup stars to the Etihad Campus, headlined by Erling Haaland. With Sunday's Community Shield against Arsenal in Cardiff now three days away, the squad is taking its final shape.

Rulli Officially Confirmed — No. 28

Manchester City and Olympique de Marseille announced the official signing of Gerónimo Rulli on 12 August 2026. The 34-year-old Argentine goalkeeper joins on a two-year deal running to June 2028, with an option to extend by a further year. The fee is reported in the region of €3.8 million — straightforward business for a club seeking experienced, decorated cover behind Gianluigi Donnarumma.

Rulli will wear No. 28 at the Etihad, as confirmed by the club's squad number announcement. His arrival fills the void left by James Trafford's £40 million departure to Leeds United earlier this month. Where Trafford was a development-track goalkeeper who never quite made the first team his own, Rulli arrives as a fully formed No. 2 — the sort of calm, senior presence who can contribute to the training environment and step in without disruption should Donnarumma be unavailable.

This is technically Rulli's second stint as a Manchester City player. He originally signed from Deportivo Maldonado in July 2016, but spent the entire 2016/17 season on loan at Real Sociedad and never made a single senior appearance in sky blue. In the decade since, he has built a remarkable career: a winner of the 2021 UEFA Europa League with Villarreal, the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, and a consistent presence at the top level through spells at Villarreal and Marseille. Enzo Maresca worked alongside Rulli during his time on the coaching staff in Italy and knows the goalkeeper's temperament well. Sources: mancity.com ("City seal signing of Gerónimo Rulli" — 12 Aug 2026); Yahoo Sports; ESPN; Get French Football News.

Squad Numbers: Four Players Assigned

Alongside the Rulli confirmation, mancity.com published the official squad numbers for four players ahead of the 2026/27 campaign. The assignments reflect the shifts in the squad over the course of a busy summer:

  • Elliot Anderson — No. 5. The club-record signing from Nottingham Forest inherits the shirt previously worn by John Stones, who departed to Inter Milan on a free transfer in July. The No. 5 is a significant number at the Etihad, associated for years with one of City's most decorated defenders. Anderson, who signed a five-year deal after his £116 million move, will aim to build his own legacy in it.
  • Marc Guehi — No. 6. The January 2026 signing from Crystal Palace moves from No. 15 — worn during his first half-season at the club — to No. 6 for the new campaign. A reward of sorts for a player who settled quickly into Maresca's defensive structure.
  • Gianluigi Donnarumma — No. 1. The Italian shot-stopper, who wore No. 25 throughout the 2025/26 season, takes the goalkeeper's traditional first-choice number now that Trafford's departure has freed it up. Donnarumma wore No. 1 for Italy and during his final season at PSG; the assignment reflects his uncontested status as City's first-choice keeper.
  • Gerónimo Rulli — No. 28. As noted, Rulli's number was confirmed simultaneously with his official signing announcement.

Sources: mancity.com ("Squad numbers confirmed for Anderson, Guéhi, Donnarumma and Rulli" — 12 Aug 2026).

The Boys Are Back: Six Stars Return

On the same day, mancity.com's training gallery — titled 'The boys are back in Manchester!' — confirmed the return of six players who had been on extended post-World Cup breaks. Erling Haaland, Jeremy Doku, Elliot Anderson, Marc Guehi, Nico O'Reilly and Rayan Cherki all returned to the Etihad Campus to resume pre-season preparations.

The significance of Haaland's return cannot be overstated. The Norwegian striker was absent from all three of City's Asia tour matches — the 1-1 draw with Inter Milan (which City lost on penalties in Hong Kong), the 3-1 win over K League All-Stars in Seoul, and the 2-1 defeat to Atlético Madrid — and without him the attack lacked a focal point. He arrives with three days to prepare for a competitive fixture against Premier League champions Arsenal, which is a tight turnaround, but Haaland's physical conditioning is rarely in question.

Doku, O'Reilly, Guehi, Anderson and Cherki also strengthen Maresca's options significantly. Combined with the squad that completed the Asia tour — Donnarumma, Rico Lewis, Khusanov, Gvardiol, Ait-Nouri, Matheus Nunes, Kovacic, Reijnders, Foden, Savinho, Semenyo and Marmoush — Enzo Maresca now has a near full-strength group from which to pick his first competitive team.

One notable absence remains. Rodri was not pictured in the gallery and continues to be linked with an imminent move to FC Barcelona, following the club-to-club fee agreement of €50 million reported on 9 August. No official departure announcement from mancity.com or an official signing announcement from FC Barcelona has been received as of 13 August 2026. Sources: mancity.com ("Gallery: The boys are back in Manchester!" — 12 Aug 2026); FootballPlace ("Six Man City stars return before Arsenal as Rodri remains absent" — 12 Aug 2026).

Community Shield: Three Days in Cardiff

The 2026 FA Community Shield takes place at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August, kicking off at 15:00 BST. The match is live on TNT Sports and HBO Max in the UK. Arsenal arrive as Premier League champions, having won the 2025/26 title by three points on the final day of the season. Manchester City arrive as FA Cup holders, with Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute back-heel winner at Wembley in May sealing a 1-0 victory over Chelsea.

Arsenal are themselves missing key players due to injury — William Saliba and Jurrïen Timber are both unavailable — making this a more equitable contest than the league table gap might suggest. For Maresca, the Community Shield is more than a season-opener; it is the first opportunity to demonstrate his football on a competitive stage, and to show how City function without Rodri as the fulcrum of the midfield. Sunday cannot come soon enough.
