Gvardiol Signs New Deal: City Defender Commits to 2031 as Real Madrid Saga Ends

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 16 June 2026

Josko Gvardiol will remain a Manchester City player for the long term. Fabrizio Romano confirmed on 15 June 2026 that the Croatian defender has signed a new contract at the Etihad Stadium running until June 2031 — describing it as a "done deal" with the move to Spain "off". Jack Gaughan of the Daily Mail simultaneously confirmed the renewal, bringing to a close one of the summer's most tense subplots.

Romano: 'Done Deal — Move to Spain Off'

Fabrizio Romano's confirmation was characteristically unequivocal: Gvardiol has agreed a new Manchester City contract, the deal is done, and Real Madrid will not be signing the Croatian defender this summer. The update resolved a situation that had been in flux for several weeks, with Gvardiol having paused contract renewal talks on 11 June following the confirmed appointment of Jose Mourinho as Real Madrid head coach.

At the time, the narrative shifted sharply. Florian Plettenberg (Sky Sports DE) reported on 10 June that Real Madrid had a concrete offer on the table, and Diario AS confirmed that Mourinho had made Gvardiol a personal priority. The pause in talks appeared to signal genuine interest in the Bernabeu move. Instead, it appears to have been a moment of reflection that ultimately ended in renewed commitment to City.

Daily Mail City correspondent Jack Gaughan confirmed the substance of Romano's report independently — a significant marker of a deal that is genuinely done rather than speculative.

The Contract: Until June 2031

The new deal commits Gvardiol to the Etihad until June 2031 — an extension from his previous contract that was set to expire in 2028. City upgraded their offer from an initial 2030 proposal to the longer 2031 term, with improved salary terms, as the negotiations intensified through May and June. The Croatian's decision to sign represents a five-year commitment from summer 2026, and makes him among the longest-contracted players in the first team.

For sporting director Hugo Viana and new head coach Enzo Maresca, securing Gvardiol was a critical summer objective. At 22, the defender has already established himself as one of the best left-sided central defenders in world football — capable of playing centre-back or left-back with equal authority. He was central to City's FA Cup-winning run in 2025/26 and started 31 times across all competitions, contributing three goals.

Why Gvardiol Stayed

Several factors appear to have been decisive. First, City's willingness to commit to a long-term, well-remunerated deal demonstrates unambiguously that Gvardiol is considered a cornerstone of the Maresca project. Second, the player's own connection to the club — and the prospect of a new era under a coach with a clear footballing identity — appears to have outweighed the allure of the Bernabeu.

Real Madrid's failure to make a more decisive move also contributed. Reports from CityXtra (13 June) had indicated that both Real Madrid and Barcelona were monitoring the situation but applying no immediate deadline pressure — a stance that may have worked against them. With City prepared to upgrade the contract and Maresca's rebuilding project offering genuine excitement, the moment for Gvardiol to leave never fully arrived.

Multiple reports from mid-June confirmed that Real Madrid have since pursued alternative defensive targets — removing the urgency from the Spanish club's side of the equation and making Gvardiol's renewal the natural conclusion.

What It Means for City

In a summer that has involved the departures of Pep Guardiola, Bernardo Silva, and John Stones, retaining Gvardiol sends a clear signal about the direction of the rebuild. Alongside the confirmed appointment of Enzo Maresca as head coach, the new deals handed to Phil Foden (May 2026, until 2030), Savinho (June 2026, until 2031), and now Gvardiol, Maresca inherits a spine of young, contracted talent that forms a credible platform for a new era.

At 22, Gvardiol may yet be the best centre-back in the world before this contract expires. Locking him in on terms that run to 2031 is, quite simply, outstanding business from Viana and City's hierarchy.
