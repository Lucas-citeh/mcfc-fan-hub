2026/27 Fixtures Released: City Begin Maresca Era on 22 August — Community Shield Against Arsenal First

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 19 June 2026

It is here. The Premier League published the full 2026/27 fixture list at 10:00 BST on Friday 19 June 2026, marking the official start of the planning cycle for what will be Enzo Maresca's first full season as Manchester City head coach. The Blues begin their competitive campaign with the FA Community Shield against Arsenal on Sunday 16 August before the Premier League season proper gets under way on Saturday 22 August 2026.

Community Shield: City vs Arsenal in Cardiff

As FA Cup holders — following Semenyo's 72nd-minute winner against Chelsea at Wembley in May — Manchester City face Premier League champions Arsenal in the season's traditional curtain-raiser. The game takes place at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August. The venue was confirmed after Wembley became unavailable due to concert commitments on 15–16 August.

The fixture gives Maresca a competitive match before the Premier League begins — valuable preparation time in what promises to be a transitional opening to the season, with international players returning from the 2026 FIFA World Cup in waves throughout July and early August.

Premier League Season Starts 22 August

The 2026/27 Premier League campaign begins on Saturday 22 August 2026 — one week later than the traditional August start. The delay reflects the Premier League's commitment to player welfare following the 2026 World Cup, which runs into mid-July and involves a large portion of City's squad. The season runs through to the final day on Sunday 30 May 2027, covering 33 weekends and five midweek match rounds.

World Cup Returns: A Complex Opening

For Maresca and his coaching staff, the opening weeks of 2026/27 present a significant squad management challenge. City's senior players at the World Cup include Erling Haaland (Norway), Phil Foden (England), Josko Gvardiol (Croatia), Rayan Ait-Nouri (Algeria), Rodri and Nico Gonzalez (Spain), Omar Marmoush (Egypt), Jeremy Doku (Belgium), Antoine Semenyo (Ghana), and Gianluigi Donnarumma (Italy). Players returning from deeper tournament runs will have shorter preparation windows before the season begins.

The extra week before the Premier League start — and the Community Shield as a warm-up fixture — partially mitigates this, but the opening months are likely to require careful rotation and squad depth management.

Three New Clubs Join the Premier League

The 2026/27 season sees three newly promoted clubs enter the top flight: Coventry City (Championship title winners), Ipswich Town (runners-up), and Hull City (play-off final winners). All three add new fixture weekends to City's calendar and offer the prospect of trips to grounds that the club's supporters haven't visited in years.

A New Era Under Maresca

Friday's fixture list marks the first real structural landmark of the post-Guardiola era at the Etihad. Pep Guardiola departed after 10 years, Bernardo Silva and John Stones left on free transfers, and Manuel Akanji permanently joined Inter Milan. Maresca takes the reins of a squad in transition but one with genuine quality retained — Josko Gvardiol (new deal until 2031), Phil Foden (signed until 2030), and Erling Haaland remain among the most potent attacking forces in European football.

The summer transfer window remains active. Manchester City's pursuit of Nottingham Forest midfielder Elliot Anderson is ongoing, and Savinho's departure to Tottenham — with a basic agreement reported to be in place — is expected to be formally confirmed in the coming days. The framework for 2026/27 is now set; the question is who will be in it when the season begins.
