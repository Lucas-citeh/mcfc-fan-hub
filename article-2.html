City Close In On Elliot Anderson as Maresca Targets Midfield Overhaul

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 26 May 2026

Manchester City have entered advanced discussions with Elliot Anderson's agents as incoming manager Enzo Maresca moves quickly to stamp his authority on the squad ahead of the 2026/27 season, Fabrizio Romano has confirmed.

Anderson, 22, has been one of the Premier League's most dynamic midfielders since his move from Newcastle United to Nottingham Forest in 2024. The England international has combined relentless pressing, carrying ability, and a reliable goal threat in a way that fits precisely the profile Maresca is understood to want in the centre of the park — a modern, box-to-box operator who can contribute at both ends.

What Romano Has Confirmed

Romano reported in May 2026 that Manchester City are the club pushing hardest for Anderson, with advanced discussions already under way between City and the player's representatives. Romano had first confirmed City's interest and contact with Anderson's camp in March 2026, with the situation accelerating as the season drew to a close.

Crucially, it is understood that Anderson himself is open to the move — the prospect of working under Maresca and competing for the biggest prizes is described as appealing to the midfielder. No personal terms have been officially confirmed, and the club-to-club phase of negotiations is yet to begin in earnest.

The Asking Price

Nottingham Forest are not expected to make this straightforward. Anderson, who has three years remaining on his contract at the City Ground, is valued by Forest at in excess of £100 million — a fee that would make him one of the most expensive midfielders in Premier League history. City are prepared to make a significant financial commitment, according to Romano, but a deal will require substantial negotiation.

Manchester United have also been credited with interest in Anderson, and Romano noted in April that both clubs were in "active pursuit." City, however, are understood to be the frontrunners, and Maresca's personal desire to bring the player to the Etihad is viewed as a decisive factor.

What It Means for the Squad

The midfield is the area where City face the most significant rebuilding work. The departures of Bernardo Silva and John Stones as free agents this summer, combined with the need for new energy as the Guardiola era closes, mean Maresca is operating quickly. Rodri, Phil Foden, Tijjani Reijnders and Mateo Kovacic form a strong core, but adding Anderson's athleticism and forward runs would provide a different dimension to City's engine room.

Anderson also suits the high-tempo, positionally fluid system Maresca is associated with from his time at Leicester and Chelsea, where his teams pressed aggressively and relied on midfielders who could cover vast amounts of ground.

Livramento Also in the Picture

Anderson is not the only significant target on City's summer list. Romano previously confirmed Tino Livramento of Newcastle United as the club's primary right-back target — the England international has been described as City's "dream" option for that position, though Newcastle are determined to keep him and the valuation is understood to be in excess of £70 million.

The summer window is not yet open, and no deals are close to being finalised. But the speed with which City's new football operation — led by sporting director Hugo Viana — has moved to identify and engage with targets suggests the club is determined not to be caught flat-footed in the transition from the Guardiola decade.

Sources: Fabrizio Romano (March–May 2026); Sky Sports transfer blog (May 2026); Football365 (May 2026).
