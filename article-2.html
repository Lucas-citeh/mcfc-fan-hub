Maresca in Agreement to Succeed Guardiola as City Boss

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 19 May 2026

Pep Guardiola will leave Manchester City at the end of the 2025/26 season, and Enzo Maresca has reached an agreement in principle to succeed him as manager at the Etihad Stadium, according to David Ornstein of The Athletic.

Guardiola, who joined City in the summer of 2016, has guided the club to six Premier League titles, the Champions League, and a host of domestic trophies during a historic decade in east Manchester. He is set to take charge of his final two matches — tonight at Bournemouth and at home to Aston Villa on Sunday — before bringing his tenure to a close.

Maresca's Agreement

Maresca, 46, is reported to have agreed a deal in principle with City on a two-year contract, with the option of a third year. No formal announcement is expected before the end of the season. The club are understood to have been working towards the appointment for several weeks.

The Italian is no stranger to the Etihad. He served as a first-team assistant under Guardiola during the 2022-23 season — the campaign in which City won the treble — and previously managed City's Elite Development Squad. He went on to take the Leicester City job, winning the Championship title, before moving to Chelsea where he led the club to a top-six finish in the Premier League.

Ten Years of Guardiola

The scale of what Guardiola has achieved at City is difficult to overstate. Six Premier League titles, including a record-breaking 100-point campaign in 2017-18. The 2022-23 treble. Seven League Cups. Two FA Cups, with the second claimed just three days ago at Wembley against Chelsea. He departs having won 15 major trophies in Manchester, transforming the club into one of the most dominant forces in European football history.

His final season has been a fitting send-off in many respects: the domestic double is secured, and City remain alive in the Premier League title race heading into the final two games.

What Comes Next

Maresca will inherit a squad that is itself in transition. Club captain Bernardo Silva and ten-year stalwart John Stones are both departing as free agents. Loans for Jack Grealish, Manuel Akanji, and Vitor Reis all expire this summer. Against that backdrop, the new manager and sporting director Hugo Viana will need to plan a significant rebuild window.

For now, the focus at the club is squarely on these final two matches — and the remote but still-possible chance of hauling in Arsenal at the top of the Premier League table.
