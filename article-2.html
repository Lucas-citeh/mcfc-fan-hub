Savinho Medical Imminent as City Accelerate Winger Search

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 17 July 2026

Manchester City and Tottenham Hotspur have reached a club-to-club agreement for the transfer of Savinho, with the fee reported at approximately £60m including add-ons. A medical is expected to take place next week, after which the deal is expected to be officially announced. Neither club has confirmed the transfer as of 17 July 2026 — but the exit is now a formality, and City's sporting director Hugo Viana is wasting no time beginning the search for a replacement.

The Deal: What We Know

Read Man City reported on 16 July that the two clubs have agreed terms, with the fee understood to include approximately £10m in add-ons on top of a base fee. Personal terms between Savinho and Tottenham were confirmed by Fabrizio Romano as agreed weeks ago — a five-year contract reportedly worth around £6m per season including bonuses, running until 2031.

CaughtOffside had reported in June that a "basic agreement" had been reached at approximately £60m, with City having given the green light to allow Savinho to leave the club. Romano described Savinho as having "said yes" to the move on 8 July and called the transfer a "strong possibility" as recently as 13 July, stating that "Tottenham have not stopped" in their pursuit of the Brazilian winger.

Spurs have already completed the £100m signing of Sandro Tonali from Newcastle United. Savinho would represent their second marquee acquisition of the summer under new head coach Roberto De Zerbi — a clear signal of Tottenham's intent to compete at the highest level in 2026/27.

Savinho's City Career

Savinho joined Manchester City from Troyes in summer 2024 after a standout loan at Girona during which he became one of La Liga's most exciting wide players. In two seasons at the Etihad he accumulated 48 appearances in all competitions, registering 10 Premier League goals and 14 assists across his debut campaign. His pace, directness and skill in one-on-one situations made him a crowd favourite — but under Enzo Maresca, the decision was made early that the Brazilian was surplus to requirements as the new manager sought to reshape the squad to his preferred profile.

Savinho is understood to be enthusiastic about the move to North London and the opportunity to play in Roberto De Zerbi's expansive, high-tempo system — a style expected to suit his strengths as a wide dribbler.

City's Search for a Replacement

With the Savinho exit edging toward completion, Viana's recruitment operation is understood to be actively identifying wide-attacking options. Sam Lee of The Athletic reported this week that Viana's preferred profile is a direct winger capable of both dribbling and creating chances through passing — a description pointing toward a ball-carrier with playmaking output rather than a purely wide forward.

Among the names that have surfaced in French football circles is Ibrahim Mbaye, the 18-year-old Paris Saint-Germain winger who made a significant impression at the 2026 World Cup when he came off the bench to score for Senegal against France — becoming the youngest African player ever to score at a World Cup at 18 years and 143 days old. Foot Mercato reported on 15 July that City are monitoring the teenager, with multiple English Premier League clubs also expressing interest.

PSG are open to a sale this summer. Fabrizio Romano has reported that the French club set their asking price at €30m when Dortmund opened initial discussions, while Goal reported PSG's internal valuation approaching €50m given the volume of interest that has since emerged. Tottenham, Aston Villa, RB Leipzig and Borussia Dortmund are all tracking the situation, meaning any deal for Mbaye is likely to require City to move quickly.

Marmoush: Staying Put?

In a separate development that could affect City's attacking recruitment plans, CityXtra reported on 17 July that Manchester City are now "planning to keep faith" with Omar Marmoush for the 2026/27 season and have dropped plans to search for his replacement. Earlier reports had suggested Atletico Madrid were the frontrunner for the Egyptian forward at around £51m (€60m), with Tottenham also having submitted a formal bid. It appears the club's position has shifted. No official statement has been issued and the situation remains subject to change before the window closes.

The Window in Context

City's Asia Tour opens in Hong Kong on 1 August against Inter Milan, with further fixtures against the K League All-Stars (5 August) and Atlético Madrid (9 August) in Seoul before the season gets properly underway. The Premier League opener against Bournemouth at the Etihad is on 23 August.

That timeline gives Viana roughly five weeks to complete a winger signing if City are to have their preferred option ready for the competitive season. The next fortnight will be critical — beginning with Savinho's expected medical.
