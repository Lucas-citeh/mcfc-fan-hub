John Stones Completes Free Transfer to Inter Milan — 'Hungry to Win Again'

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 30 July 2026

John Stones has officially joined Serie A champions Inter Milan on a two-year free transfer, completing his move on Wednesday 30 July 2026. After a decade and six Premier League titles at the Etihad, the 32-year-old England international said he is "excited" and "hungry to win again" as he begins a new chapter in Italy — and he "didn't hesitate" in seizing the Inter opportunity when it arose.

End of an Era at the Etihad

Stones arrived at Manchester City from Everton in the summer of 2016 for £47.5 million and became one of the most decorated defenders in the club's history. Under Pep Guardiola, he developed from a raw but talented centre-back into a technically exceptional, positionally intelligent player who redefined what it meant to defend in a high line. In 2020/21 — his finest individual season — he was named in the Premier League Team of the Year as City romped to the title by 12 points.

His haul over a decade at the Etihad reads as follows: six Premier League titles, one Champions League, one FA Cup and one League Cup — plus two FA Community Shields. He made over 280 appearances for the club and was a key figure in the 2022/23 Treble, playing 38 times across all competitions. His ability to carry the ball from defence and his comfort under pressure made him one of the most distinctive centre-backs of the Guardiola era.

Why Inter?

Stones's contract at the Etihad expired on 30 June 2026 at the end of the 2025/26 season. With both Arsenal and Chelsea reported to have shown interest, the defender chose to move abroad — "I didn't hesitate," he told reporters upon arriving in Milan. Inter represent the natural destination: Serie A's dominant force under Christian Chivu, backed by Zhang family funding and buoyed by their domestic title win in 2025/26 (which triggered Manuel Akanji's mandatory €15 million purchase from City). Stones joins Akanji at the San Siro, reuniting with a former City teammate in the Inter backline.

"I'm excited to be here," Stones said on arrival. "This is a massive club with a great history, great fans and a squad that's capable of winning the biggest prizes in European football. I'm hungry to win again, and I believe Inter can do that." The two-year deal runs until summer 2028.

Stones and the Maresca Era

Stones featured in Enzo Maresca's final game in charge — the 1-2 home defeat to Aston Villa on 24 May 2026, Guardiola's farewell match — where he was substituted at the 77th minute as part of a triple change. He appeared 11 times across all competitions during the 2025/26 season, injury limiting his involvement in the final months of the campaign.

Maresca, who inherited the squad ahead of the 2026/27 season, has made clear he was happy for Stones to find a new challenge given his contract situation, and City opted not to offer a renewal. The club released a warm farewell statement thanking him for his contribution to one of the most successful decades in Premier League history.

What It Means for City

With Stones and Manuel Akanji (who joined Inter via the mandatory purchase clause triggered in May) both departing, City's centre-back group entering 2026/27 now consists of Ruben Dias, Marc Guehi, Josko Gvardiol, Abdukodir Khusanov, and Vitor Reis. Dias signed a new four-year deal through to 2030 in July — as confirmed by ESPN — and is expected to captain Maresca's side from the back. With Marc Guehi's arrival from Crystal Palace in January 2026, City's central defensive options remain strong despite the departures.

The Stones sale — technically a free transfer, generating no immediate fee — is partially offset by City's ability to redirect those wage costs into their ambitious summer recruitment programme, which includes Elliot Anderson (£116m from Nottingham Forest) and Jeremy Monga (£10m from Leicester City) as confirmed additions.
