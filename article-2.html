Rodri Agrees Personal Terms With Real Madrid as City Exit Looms

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 4 August 2026

Rodri has agreed personal terms with Real Madrid, per multiple credible sources including Yahoo Sports, CityXtra and OneFootball — moving the Spain captain and World Cup winner a decisive step closer to leaving Manchester City this summer. The player's contract with City expires in summer 2027, entering its final year, and the Ballon d'Or holder has made clear that a move to the Bernabéu is his preferred destination. A fee gap between the two clubs remains the only formal obstacle.

How We Got Here

The saga began building momentum after Rodri played the full 120 minutes in Spain's 2026 World Cup Final victory over Argentina at MetLife Stadium on 19 July. Days later, on 28 July, City confirmed he had undergone back surgery in Madrid — a procedure that will keep him sidelined for approximately four to six weeks and rule him out of the Community Shield and the opening Premier League fixture. The back issue did not slow transfer speculation; if anything, it drew attention to the fact that his contract at the Etihad is now in its final year.

Real Madrid made their first formal approach to Manchester City for Rodri on approximately 30 July, per Managing Madrid — a contact that signalled Madrid's intent had moved from interest to active pursuit. In the days that followed, Paris Saint-Germain and Barcelona were also linked with the midfielder, but per Tribuna.com and other reports, Rodri declined to engage with either club and has held out specifically for Real Madrid. That position has not changed.

Where Negotiations Stand

Personal terms between Rodri and Real Madrid are now agreed in principle, per Yahoo Sports, CityXtra and OneFootball. The remaining obstacle is a club-to-club fee agreement. Manchester City are asking for in the region of €80 million for a player entering the final year of his contract, reflecting both his status as arguably the world's best midfielder and City's reluctance to sell to a direct Champions League rival. Real Madrid, aware of the contract situation, are offering closer to €60–65 million.

Per Sky Sports and Forbes, City communicated a revised asking price to Real Madrid on 2 August — a sign that the gap is narrowing, even if it has not yet closed. FootballTransfers, citing Fabrizio Romano, reported that the "deal is on." Yahoo Sports described the transfer as "on track." Neither Manchester City nor Real Madrid have made any official announcement, and no deal is confirmed as of 4 August 2026.

What It Means for City

The potential departure of Rodri would represent a seismic moment for the club. Since joining from Atlético Madrid in 2019, he has been City's heartbeat — the defensive midfielder around whom six Premier League title-winning sides were built. His 2023/24 Ballon d'Or was a statement of undeniable individual quality. In the 2023/24 season, his only injury-interrupted campaign at City, his absence was directly correlated with a collapse in results. When Rodri doesn't play, City historically struggle.

New head coach Enzo Maresca is aware that planning for the 2026/27 season must account for the possibility of losing his most influential midfielder before the window closes. The club have been proactive: Elliot Anderson was signed from Nottingham Forest for a club-record £116 million in July, and City are closing in on a deal for Ayyoub Bouaddi — the Lille midfielder who has already agreed a five-year deal with City in principle, per CityXtra — as what appears to be a long-term Rodri successor.

The Rodri Legacy

Rodri has spent seven seasons at the Etihad since arriving in August 2019. In that time, he has won six Premier League titles, one Champions League, one FA Cup, one League Cup, two FA Community Shields, the UEFA Nations League (2021) and the 2026 FIFA World Cup with Spain — for whom he also claimed the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player. He made 304 appearances for City across all competitions. His legacy at the Etihad, whatever happens this summer, is secure.

The coming days and weeks will determine whether City accept a compromise fee and allow him to leave, or hold firm and keep him for the final year of his contract. At the time of writing, the situation remains unresolved — but the direction of travel is unmistakable.
