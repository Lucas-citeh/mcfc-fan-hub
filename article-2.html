Haaland and Semenyo Shortlisted for PL Player of the Season

By MCFC Fan Hub 14 May 2026

Manchester City duo Erling Haaland and Antoine Semenyo have both been named on the eight-man shortlist for the 2025/26 Premier League Player of the Season award.

The recognition caps a remarkable individual campaign for both forwards as City close in on Arsenal at the top of the table with two matches remaining.

Haaland's Golden Boot Charge

Haaland heads into the final week of the season with 26 Premier League goals, four clear of his nearest rival in the race for a third Premier League Golden Boot. The Norwegian has scored 37 goals across all competitions and reached his 100th Premier League goal in record time back in December.

"To be on this list with players of that quality is special, but the only thing that matters this week is winning football matches." - Erling Haaland

Semenyo's Stunning Debut Season at City

Antoine Semenyo's nomination is the more eye-catching of the two. The Ghana international has scored 16 Premier League goals across his split season with Bournemouth and Manchester City, with nine of those coming since his January move to the Etihad.

His first-time finish from a Phil Foden flick opened the scoring in Tuesday's 3-0 win over Crystal Palace, the latest in a string of decisive performances since his arrival.

The Shortlist

The pair are up against six other Premier League stars on the eight-man shortlist, with the winner to be revealed at the end of the season. City fans can vote via the official Premier League channels.
