City Sign Mathys Detourbet from Troyes in €25m CFG Transfer — 19-Year-Old Headed to Monaco on Loan

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 28 June 2026

Manchester City have officially signed Mathys Detourbet from City Football Group sister club ESTAC Troyes for a fee of €25 million including add-ons, the clubs confirmed on 27 June 2026. The 19-year-old French left winger, born 29 April 2007, becomes the first Troyes academy product to complete an internal CFG transfer to the Premier League club. He will spend the 2026/27 season on loan at AS Monaco to gather Ligue 1 experience before integrating into Enzo Maresca's first-team plans at the Etihad.

A CFG Pipeline Transfer

Troyes and Manchester City share ownership under the City Football Group umbrella, making this transfer a structured internal move rather than a conventional open-market deal. Troyes won promotion back to Ligue 1 for 2026/27, with Detourbet playing a central role in that Ligue 2 title campaign. Initial reports of a transfer agreement surfaced in mid-May 2026; Troyes and City jointly confirmed the deal on 27 June.

The fee — understood to be €25 million guaranteed, with performance-related add-ons potentially taking the total higher — reflects the value CFG places on one of the best young players to emerge from their French academy system. Detourbet is the first player to make this specific internal route from Troyes to Manchester City directly, a milestone that underlines the growing maturity of CFG's talent pipeline.

Who Is Mathys Detourbet?

Born in April 2007, Detourbet joined the Troyes academy at the age of eight and progressed through every age group before breaking into the first team. A direct, pacy left winger with sharp close control and an eye for goal, he scored three times as Troyes won Ligue 2 in 2025/26 and won promotion back to the top flight. His combination of technical quality, athleticism, and positional intelligence has attracted interest from clubs across France and beyond.

His profile sits firmly within the mould of attacking players Enzo Maresca has favoured throughout his coaching career — direct, press-resistant, capable of carrying the ball at pace in tight spaces and arriving late into scoring positions. At 19, he has the raw material to develop into that type at the highest level.

Monaco Loan: The Right Next Step

Rather than integrating Detourbet immediately into Premier League competition, Manchester City and the player's representatives have agreed that a season-long loan to AS Monaco is the ideal next stage of his development. Monaco will provide Ligue 1 football and, potentially, European competition — the kind of high-level experience that will accelerate his growth without the risk of being used sparingly in City's first-team shadow during a transitional season under a new manager.

The arrangement follows a pattern well established within CFG for promising teenagers: develop at a partner club, gain elite competitive exposure, then integrate when the timing is right. With Detourbet, that timeline points toward a potential arrival in Manchester for 2027/28 — still only 20 years old and with a full season of top-flight experience behind him.

The Bigger Picture for Maresca's Rebuild

The Detourbet signing is consistent with the direction Enzo Maresca has set for City's summer. This is a window built around long-term quality and profile rather than short-term fixes. With Pep Guardiola gone, and Bernardo Silva, John Stones and Manuel Akanji all following, the squad is being reimagined from the ground up. Investing in a 19-year-old of Detourbet's quality — even with a loan immediately following — signals an intention to build a squad that will compete at the highest level for years, not just one transfer window.

With Elliot Anderson's £116m deal also agreed and in the final stages of completion, City's summer is beginning to take clear shape. The Detourbet signing adds the longer-term dimension — a prospect who may yet prove to be one of the most important pieces of Maresca's new era.
