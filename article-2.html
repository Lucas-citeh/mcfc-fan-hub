City and PUMA Unveil 2026/27 Home Kit: Not Your Typical City

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 20 May 2026

Manchester City and PUMA have unveiled the club's new home kit for the 2026/27 season, launching under the campaign theme 'Not Your Typical City'. The shirt marks the beginning of a new chapter at the Etihad — and it arrives with an unmistakable design statement.

The kit was officially announced on Wednesday 20 May via mancity.com, going on sale immediately through the club's official store and online shop. It goes on show for the first time at the Etihad on Sunday, when City host Aston Villa in the final Premier League fixture of the 2025/26 season — and Pep Guardiola's farewell match as manager.

The Design

The centrepiece of the new shirt is a full sky-blue gradient that runs from a deeper tone at the shoulders and progressively lightens toward the hem. The effect gives the shirt a modern, dynamic quality that sets it apart from the flat-colour shirts of recent seasons. White is used on the collar and sleeve cuffs, grounding the gradient in a classic City palette.

Details carry weight here. The back collar is printed with the word "CITY" in bold lettering, while the inside of the collar bears the campaign phrase "Not Your Typical City" — a nod to the philosophy behind the launch. The crest and sponsor details are rendered in a metallic monochrome finish, adding a premium feel to the overall look.

Performance and Sustainability

PUMA's ULTRAWEAVE fabric features in the Authentic jersey — an ultra-lightweight performance material incorporating dryCELL moisture management and ThermoAdapt temperature regulation technology. The Replica jersey, aimed at supporters, is produced using RE:FIBRE technology: a fabric made from 95% recycled polyester sourced entirely from textile waste, aligning the kit with the club's sustainability commitments.

A New Era, A New Look

The 2026/27 home kit arrives at an inflection point for the club. Pep Guardiola departs after ten years and 15 major trophies. Enzo Maresca is expected to take over for the new season. Phil Foden, who signed a new four-year contract this week, will wear this shirt as the figurehead of what comes next. Erling Haaland, the fastest player in Premier League history to reach 100 goals, remains the focal point up front.

The campaign theme — 'Not Your Typical City' — is a deliberate challenge to static, traditional kit design and an assertion that this club continues to evolve. For supporters, the gradient home shirt is both a piece of kit and a statement of intent heading into a new chapter.

The 2026/27 home kit is available now at mancity.com/shop and Manchester City Official Stores.

Source: Manchester City FC (mancity.com), 20 May 2026.
