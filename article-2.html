End of an Era: Bernardo Silva and John Stones Confirm City Exits

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 16 May 2026

Manchester City have officially confirmed that club captain Bernardo Silva and ten-year servant John Stones will both depart the Etihad Stadium as free agents this summer, bringing a combined 19 years of loyal service to a close.

The announcements, made separately on the club's official website, mark the end of a transformative era at the Etihad. Both players are expected to remain with the squad through the remainder of the 2025/26 season — Bernardo captaining the side at the FA Cup Final today at Wembley — before departing in June.

Bernardo Silva: Nine Years, 451 Appearances

Bernardo Silva arrived from AS Monaco in 2017 and has since become one of Manchester City's greatest ever players. Over nine seasons he racked up 451 appearances, contributed to 19 major trophies, and was selected by Pep Guardiola more times than any other player in the Catalan's coaching career.

The Portuguese midfielder confirmed his decision to leave earlier this year, with Guardiola paying a heartfelt tribute: "He deserves the best. What he has given us is impossible to explain in words."

Bernardo is expected to be available to European clubs and beyond when his contract expires in June, with several top-flight sides — including FC Barcelona — reported to be monitoring his situation closely.

John Stones: A Decade at the Etihad

John Stones joined City from Everton in 2016 for £47.5 million and has spent ten years establishing himself as one of English football's finest defenders. The 31-year-old has won six Premier League titles, three FA Cups, and the Champions League during his time in Manchester.

The club confirmed the news on mancity.com, stating: "The Club will take the time to deliver a fitting tribute and farewell to Stones at the end of the season." Fabrizio Romano separately confirmed the departure, noting Stones is "ready for a new chapter" after making history at City.

Injuries have limited Stones to 22 appearances this season, but his contribution to the club's decade of dominance is unquestioned.

What Comes Next

The departures of two such decorated players signal the beginning of a significant rebuild this summer. With City still in contention for a league and cup double heading into the final weeks of the season, attention will turn to the transfer window once the 2025/26 campaign concludes.

Sporting director Hugo Viana will oversee the rebuild, and the club will be in the market to reinforce several areas of the pitch. For now, however, the focus remains on giving Bernardo and Stones the send-off they deserve over the final three matches of the season.
