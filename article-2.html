Community Shield Day: Rodri Asks City to Skip Training as Barcelona Push for Monday Deal

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 16 August 2026

It is Community Shield day, and it is also the day the Rodri saga reached its most urgent point yet. Multiple outlets — including Yahoo Sports, Goal.com and Barca Universal — report that the 2024 Ballon d'Or winner has asked Manchester City for special permission to absent himself from training as Barcelona race to complete his long-anticipated transfer before Monday. City face Arsenal at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff at 15:00 BST without the midfielder who has defined their football for six years. A club-to-club agreement has been in the works for weeks; what remains is the final paperwork, Rodri's signature on a Barcelona deal, and an official announcement that has so far eluded the summer's most drawn-out saga. A contradictory "radical shift" report from Goal.com adds an extra layer of uncertainty.

Rodri Asks to Skip Training

The clearest indication yet that Rodri's time at the Etihad Stadium is nearing its end emerged on Community Shield morning. Per Yahoo Sports, Goal.com and Yardbarker, the Spanish midfielder has requested special permission from Manchester City to skip training as he awaits the finalisation of his move to FC Barcelona. His post-World Cup holiday period ended some days ago, meaning his continued absence from Carrington is no longer schedule-related — it is directional. The request is understood to reflect how close the deal has come, with Barcelona pushing hard to have everything signed before Monday 17 August.

Barca Universal and Yahoo Sports both report that Barcelona are targeting Monday completion, with sources close to the player indicating that personal terms — a four-year contract reported at approximately €30 million per year gross — have long been agreed. The outstanding issue has been the club-to-club fee. City have consistently demanded a figure above €60 million; reports in recent days have cited a Barcelona offer of approximately €70 million including add-ons, a rise from the figures of €50–60 million that City rejected through July and into August. Barcelona accelerated their ability to pay after confirming the sale of Ferran Torres to PSG (per FootballTransfers).

A Contradictory Picture: The 'Radical Shift'

A separate Goal.com piece published under the headline "A radical shift: Rodri delivers a blow to Barcelona for the sake of City" added a significant complication to the narrative. According to that report, Rodri has privately communicated to Manchester City that if Barcelona cannot meet the club's fee demands, he is prepared to stay and see out the final year of his contract at the Etihad — rather than agitate for a move or engineer a confrontation with a club he deeply respects. Multiple sources within City's structure were cited as describing the relationship as built on "mutual respect," with Rodri understanding that any exit must happen on terms acceptable to his employers.

The two stories are not necessarily incompatible. Rodri may simultaneously prefer to leave this summer, be willing to stay if the financial gap cannot be bridged, and have requested a training absence so that — in the event Barcelona do meet City's demands at any hour — he can travel without delay. What is beyond dispute is that Enzo Maresca will take his squad to Cardiff this afternoon without the player who was the fulcrum of City's midfield under Pep Guardiola. No official departure announcement has been issued by mancity.com or FC Barcelona as of 16 August 2026.

Community Shield: City's First Competitive Test Without Rodri

The Community Shield kicks off at 15:00 BST at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff, the first time the fixture has been held in Wales since 2006. Arsenal arrive as Premier League champions; Manchester City arrive as FA Cup holders, Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute back-heel at Wembley in May sealing a 1-0 victory over Chelsea.

Without Rodri — and also without Reijnders, who is awaiting finalisation of his €61 million exit to Al Qadsiah following Fabrizio Romano's "here we go" confirmation on 13 August — Maresca's midfield options are centred on Mateo Kovacic, Elliot Anderson and either Phil Foden or Rayan Cherki. The £116 million Anderson is the headline arrival, a box-to-box midfielder whose progressive carry numbers and pressing intensity made him the most expensive signing in City's history. Haaland, Doku, Guehi, O'Reilly and Cherki all returned from extended World Cup breaks on 12 August, giving Maresca a near full-strength group for his competitive debut at the club. Arsenal are themselves missing William Saliba and Jurrïen Timber through injury.

How Maresca structures his side without his two senior central midfielders will define the tactical story of this Cardiff afternoon — and provide the first real evidence of what post-Guardiola, post-Rodri Manchester City looks like when the stakes are real.
