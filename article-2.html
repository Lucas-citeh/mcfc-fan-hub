City Land Mishel Nduka: Arsenal's 16-Year-Old Academy Star Chooses Etihad Future

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 21 July 2026

Manchester City have agreed a deal to sign Mishel Nduka — the 16-year-old attacking midfielder who rejected a competitive long-term offer from Arsenal to choose the Etihad instead. David Ornstein of The Athletic broke the story on 21 July 2026: Nduka will undergo a medical before joining City on scholarship terms, with a professional contract to follow once he becomes eligible to sign one.

Who Is Mishel Nduka?

Nduka is a 16-year-old England under-16 international described by those who have watched him closely as technically refined in tight spaces, comfortable under pressure, and a creative passer with the ability to dictate play from central midfield positions. He is versatile enough to contribute in both halves of the pitch — pressing with intensity when out of possession and linking play with precision when in it.

He arrived at Arsenal from Charlton Athletic in September 2025, spending just one season in North London before making the decision to move north. In that short period, he made approximately ten appearances for Arsenal's under-18s, featured twice in Premier League 2, earned a UEFA Youth League outing, and collected seven caps for England's under-16 side — a rapid progression for a player of his age.

Why Nduka Chose City

Arsenal, by all accounts, made a serious effort to keep him. The Gunners offered Nduka a highly competitive long-term deal — one that would have locked him in at London Colney for several years and represented a significant investment in a player they clearly regarded as a priority academy asset. He turned it down.

The pull of Manchester City was, in part, the pull of Enzo Maresca's project. The new head coach built a reputation at Chelsea for developing and deploying technically gifted midfielders, and City's academy pathway — which has produced players such as Rico Lewis and Nico O'Reilly in recent seasons — carries genuine weight with young players assessing their options. The Etihad's infrastructure, the City Football Academy at Carrington, and the depth of the club's coaching staff all form part of an offer that is difficult to refuse.

Nduka is also understood to be focused on the opportunity to sign a professional contract at the highest level once he is of age. Joining a club of City's stature on scholarship terms — rather than committing to a long-term deal elsewhere — gives him the chance to prove himself in an elite environment before taking that next formal step in his career.

A Scholarship Signing — But One to Watch

It is worth being clear about the nature of this deal. Nduka joins City on scholarship terms — standard procedure for a player of his age who cannot yet sign a professional contract. He will be integrated into City's academy structure, with a professional contract to follow when he becomes eligible. He is not a first-team signing and will not appear in the first-team squad for the 2026/27 season.

That said, the manner in which this signing was conducted — Ornstein reporting it as confirmed, Arsenal having offered him a lucrative long-term deal, and City prevailing — signals the regard in which Nduka is held by those within the game. Scholarship signings from rival Premier League clubs are not routine occurrences. City do not pursue them unless they are convinced of the player's potential to reach the first team.

For City supporters, this is a name to file away. The academy pipeline has been a source of genuine first-team contributions in recent seasons, and if Nduka develops along the trajectory that City clearly believe he can, the Etihad faithful will be hearing a great deal more about him.
