Rulli Completes Medical, Rodri Bound for Barcelona — City's Decisive Week

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 10 August 2026

Monday 10 August is shaping up as the most consequential day yet in Manchester City's summer. Gerónimo Rulli has completed his medical at the Etihad Stadium, with an official club announcement expected shortly. Rodri is due to arrive in Barcelona on Wednesday 12 August to finalise his €50 million transfer. And looming over all of it is Sunday's FA Community Shield against Arsenal in Cardiff — Enzo Maresca's first competitive match in charge, now just six days away.

Rulli's Medical Done — Official Announcement Imminent

The Argentine goalkeeper completed a medical at Manchester City's Etihad Campus on Monday morning, according to CaughtOffside, who had reported on Friday that the procedure would take place today. Multiple outlets including beIN Sports — whose headline read simply "Rulli Joins Manchester City" — and Yahoo Sports had already reported an agreement in principle between City and Olympique de Marseille earlier last week.

Rulli, 34, joins on a two-year contract running to June 2028, with an option to extend by a further year. The fee is reported to be in the region of £1.7m–€3.5m — a straightforward piece of business for a goalkeeper arriving to serve as Gianluigi Donnarumma's backup. Sources: Yahoo Sports; beIN Sports; CaughtOffside.

The deal is a curious one in that Rulli technically joined Manchester City once before — from Deportivo Maldonado in July 2016 — only to spend the entire 2016/17 season on loan at Real Sociedad without making a single senior appearance. He eventually signed permanently for Sociedad, going on to win the 2021 Europa League with Villarreal and the 2022 World Cup with Argentina before joining Marseille in 2024. At the Etihad this time around, his experience will be an asset in the squad rather than a reason for regret.

An official announcement from mancity.com is expected once the formalities are signed. Until that lands, Rulli remains in City's rumours section — the club's official confirmation will complete his move from target to signed player.

Rodri: Barcelona on Wednesday

The more seismic departure closes its final chapter this week. According to beIN Sports and Goal.com (citing Radio Catalunya 1), Rodri is expected to travel to Barcelona on Wednesday 12 August to sign his four-year deal with FC Barcelona. A fee of €50 million was agreed between the clubs on 9 August, per Yahoo Sports — a compromise figure between City's initial €80 million valuation and what Real Madrid, who had been the frontrunner until last week, had been willing to offer.

Rodri remains technically a Manchester City player until the paperwork is countersigned and the transfer window formalities are complete. City published a "2026/27 injury update" for him on mancity.com, indicating he continues to be listed within the club's player group for now. His back surgery on 28 July — carried out in Madrid to address a lumbar issue he managed through the World Cup — means he would not have been available until mid-September in any case. The opening months of the season were always going to be played without him; the question is now whether that absence becomes permanent.

Once confirmed officially by mancity.com or FC Barcelona, Rodri will be moved to the Outs panel in our Transfer Hub and removed from the squad listing. Watch this space for Wednesday.

Bouaddi: Into the Final Stretch

Manchester City's pursuit of Ayyoub Bouaddi has entered what multiple outlets now describe as its final stages. Yahoo Sports reported on Monday that City have agreed five-year contract terms with the 18-year-old Moroccan midfielder and that Lille negotiations are close to conclusion. Goal.com described the situation as a "huge step," with City "closing in on completing the historic deal."

The major outstanding issue remains the loan-back arrangement. Lille want to retain Bouaddi for the 2026/27 season; City had initially resisted but are now reportedly weighing whether to accept a season-long loan as the price of securing the player. A fee of approximately €90 million has been reported (City's position), with Lille's ask hovering nearer €100 million. The situation remains live — no official announcement from mancity.com or Lille has been made — but the volume of positive reporting from credible outlets over the past 48 hours marks a significant shift from the "new blow" narrative that Sports Mole had reported on 8 August. Sources: Yahoo Sports (10 Aug); Goal.com (10 Aug); Fabrizio Romano (July–Aug 2026).

Community Shield: Six Days in Cardiff

While the transfer business continues to unfold, the first competitive fixture of the Maresca era is now almost upon City. The 2026 FA Community Shield takes place at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August, kicking off at 15:00 BST — the first time the traditional curtain-raiser has been staged at the Welsh venue since 2006, with Wembley unavailable due to concerts on 15–16 August.

Arsenal arrive as Premier League champions, having pipped City by three points in a title race that went to the final day. City arrive as FA Cup holders, with Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute winner at Wembley sealing a 1-0 victory over Chelsea in May. The match is live on TNT Sports and HBO Max in the UK.

Both sides will be missing post-World Cup players through fatigue and staggered returns, making this more of a statement of direction than a full test of each manager's plans. For Maresca, the first competitive look at his starting XI — and how he lines up without Rodri — begins here. City's pre-season record (1-1 pens vs Inter, 3-1 vs K League All-Stars, 1-2 vs Atlético Madrid) suggests a team still finding its footing. The Arsenal contest will be a very different examination.
