Savinho Wants Out: Romano Confirms Tottenham Talks Reactivated at £70m

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 29 May 2026

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that Tottenham Hotspur have reactivated talks to sign Savinho from Manchester City this summer, with the 22-year-old Brazilian winger open to leaving the Etihad in search of regular first-team football. Manchester City are prepared to sanction a sale if their valuation of around £70 million is met.

Romano revealed on X (formerly Twitter) on 26 May 2026 that Spurs had renewed their push for Savinho after a previous move collapsed last summer — at that time Pep Guardiola refused to sanction the departure. With Guardiola now gone, the situation has changed significantly, and City's new hierarchy under incoming manager Enzo Maresca and sporting director Hugo Viana are not standing in the player's way.

A Season of Frustration

Savinho managed just 14 starts across all competitions in 2025/26, restricted by the competition for wide positions that arrived with the signings of Tijjani Reijnders, Rayan Cherki, and the January arrival of Antoine Semenyo. The Brazilian's electric dribbling ability and direct running were on display when selected, but his overall contribution — three Premier League goals, five across all competitions — did not reflect his potential.

According to Romano, Savinho was ultimately omitted from Brazil's squad for the 2026 World Cup, which begins next month, with the selectors citing a lack of regular club minutes as a key factor. That decision, sources say, hardened his resolve to secure a move where he will be a first-choice starter.

Spurs Come Back In

Tottenham are rebuilding under head coach Roberto De Zerbi following their relegation play-off drama this season, and De Zerbi is understood to be a major admirer of Savinho's profile. Spurs are willing to meet City's asking price of around £70 million, according to Romano — a figure City consider fair given Savinho's age, ability, and remaining contract length.

Newcastle United are also monitoring the situation, Romano noted, providing City with potential negotiating leverage, though Spurs are understood to be the frontrunners at this stage.

City's Response: A Winger Hunt Begins

Should Savinho depart, Manchester City have already begun exploring replacements. Romano confirmed on 28 May that City are 'exploring the possibility' of bringing in a new wide attacker and that a sale would accelerate those plans. Among the names in City's early thinking are Sporting CP's Francisco Trincao, Bournemouth's Eli Junior Kroupi — who scored against City at the Vitality in May — and RB Leipzig's Yan Diomande.

None of those moves are described as advanced at this stage, and no formal bids have been submitted. But the winger market is one Maresca's staff is actively working in this summer, and with £70 million potentially arriving from the Savinho sale, the finances are there to reinvest.

What It Means for Maresca

The Savinho situation crystallises the scale of the squad reconstruction task facing Enzo Maresca as he prepares for his first season in charge. Bernardo Silva and John Stones have already departed as free agents; Savinho could follow. Adding Elliot Anderson and a right-back are the priority additions — but replacing a player of Savinho's dynamism on the left wing will also be critical to how City set up in 2026/27.

For City supporters who have appreciated Savinho's bright flashes over two seasons, this is a bittersweet development. The talent was never in doubt. The minutes, ultimately, never came in sufficient numbers to keep him.

Sources: Fabrizio Romano on X, 26 May 2026 (Tottenham talks reactivated); Fabrizio Romano, 28 May 2026 (City exploring winger replacement).
