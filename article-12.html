Manchester City 0-1 Brighton: Ayari's Early Strike Stuns Etihad as City Lose Back-to-Back

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 31 October 2026

Brighton achieved something no side had managed before on Saturday afternoon — a Premier League win at the Etihad Stadium. Yasin Ayari's 11th-minute tap-in, aided by a deflection off Maxim De Cuyper's shin, was all the Seagulls needed. Bart Verbruggen's seven saves did the rest, leaving Manchester City with back-to-back Premier League defeats for the first time under Enzo Maresca.

An Unfortunate Goal Opens the Scoring

The decisive moment arrived barely before City had found their rhythm. A low Brighton cross into the box was missed by Matheus Nunes, the ball cannoning off Maxim De Cuyper's shin and landing perfectly for Yasin Ayari, who tapped in from six yards with City's defence momentarily static. It was the kind of goal that can leave a side feeling hard done by — yet, as the afternoon wore on, its significance only grew.

Enzo Maresca had made ten changes from Wednesday's Carabao Cup penalty shootout win over the same opposition, with Ayyoub Bouaddi the only player to retain his place. Erling Haaland and Iliman Ndiaye led the attack, with Jérémy Doku and Enzo Fernández supporting behind, while a midfield pairing of Bouaddi and Elliot Anderson was tasked with controlling proceedings.

Verbruggen's Heroics Repel City's Siege

City responded by dominating possession and creating chance after chance, but Brighton goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen was in inspired form. The Dutch international made seven saves across the 90 minutes — denying Haaland on multiple occasions and frustrating Doku and Fernández — to produce a display that drew comparisons with the finest individual goalkeeping performances seen at the Etihad in recent years.

Rayan Cherki was introduced around the 55th minute for Bouaddi in an attempt to inject more creativity, and Phil Foden replaced Ndiaye just before the 80th minute as City pushed desperately for a leveller. But Brighton's defensive organisation, marshalled calmly by Lewis Dunk, held firm. The visitors barely threatened after their opener yet departed with all three points.

City's Title Lead Under Pressure

The defeat leaves City on 21 points from nine Premier League games — seven wins and two losses — with Arsenal able to go three points clear if they avoid defeat at Liverpool on Sunday. It is a significant shift in the title picture following two successive defeats, and Maresca was candid in his reaction: "It feels very similar to the Villa loss," the Italian said. "We deserved more from the game but we couldn't score."

On a brighter note, Antoine Semenyo — sidelined since a knee injury against Ipswich on 17 October — remains on course to return in December, and the club's Champions League form has been impressive. City will look to regroup when they travel to Nottingham Forest next Saturday.
