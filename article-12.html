Aston Villa 2-1 Manchester City: Jackson Brace Ends City's Perfect Start

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 24 October 2026

Manchester City's perfect Premier League start came to an end at Villa Park on Saturday, as Aston Villa won 2-1 to inflict Enzo Maresca's first domestic defeat. Nicolas Jackson scored twice — in the sixth and 60th minutes — while Erling Haaland's header briefly levelled the contest after the break, but Zion Suzuki's heroics and Villa's clinical edge proved decisive.

Jackson Strikes Early to Set the Tone

Villa wasted little time making their intentions clear. In just the sixth minute, Nicolas Jackson latched on to a John McGinn through ball and slotted coolly past Gianluigi Donnarumma to put the hosts in front. It was the kind of sharp counter-attacking opener that City had rarely conceded this season — a signal that Villa had come prepared.

City struggled to find their rhythm in the first half against a well-organised Villa side. Rayan Cherki and Iliman Ndiaye were lively down the flanks but without the injured Antoine Semenyo — sidelined with a knee injury — City's forward lines lacked their usual fluency. Haaland, who recovered to start, worked hard but was largely isolated in the first period.

Haaland Levels — Briefly

Enzo Maresca's side came out with greater urgency after the break. In the 52nd minute, Erling Haaland powered home a header from Ndiaye's cross to make it 1-1 and briefly restore parity. It was Haaland's ninth Premier League goal of the season — and it seemed to shift the momentum decisively in City's favour.

Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, however, was in inspired form. The Japanese international produced a string of saves to deny City a second goal, most notably tipping a fierce Josko Gvardiol effort on to the crossbar. Suzuki earned widespread acclaim as Villa's standout performer, his confidence growing with every stop.

Jackson Seals It on the Hour

City's vulnerability on the counter proved costly once more. In the 60th minute, Jackson collected a pass from Ibrahim Mbaye and finished with composure for his second of the afternoon, restoring Villa's lead and sealing a 2-1 victory. Phil Foden and Rico Lewis were introduced from the bench but City couldn't manufacture the equaliser, as Villa held firm for a fourth successive home win over the champions.

The result means City now sit on 21 points from eight Premier League games — seven wins and one defeat — their unbeaten start ended by a Villa side that has made Villa Park a fortress against Maresca's predecessors and successors alike. The focus now turns to a demanding double-header against Brighton: the Carabao Cup on Wednesday, then the Premier League on Saturday.

Goals Summary

  • 6' Jackson (AVL) — clinical finish from McGinn's through ball
  • 52' Haaland (MCI) — powerful header from Ndiaye's cross
  • 60' Jackson (AVL) — composed second finish, assisted by Mbaye

Manchester City XI

Donnarumma; Khusanov, Dias (C), Guehi, Gvardiol; Anderson, Fernández; Ndiaye, Cherki, Doku; Haaland. Subs used: Foden, Lewis.
