Guardiola Names Rodri in Squad as City Face Chelsea at Wembley Tomorrow

By MCFC Fan Hub 15 May 2026

Manchester City travel to Wembley on Saturday for their fourth consecutive FA Cup Final, where they face Chelsea in a showdown that could deliver a historic domestic double — with Pep Guardiola confirming that Rodri has been included in the squad after recovering from a calf injury.

Kick-off is at 15:00 on Saturday 16 May, live on BBC One and TNT Sports. City, who secured the EFL Cup earlier this season, can become only the sixth club in English football history to complete the domestic double if they overcome a resurgent Chelsea side at the national stadium.

Rodri's Return

The return of the 2024 Ballon d'Or winner could prove decisive. Rodri has been absent since picking up a calf complaint before City's 3-0 win at Brentford in early May, missing four consecutive matchday squads. Guardiola confirmed on Friday that the Spaniard has come through training without issue and is available for selection.

"Rodri is available and we are happy about that. He is a very important player for us and to have him back for a final at Wembley is fantastic. Now we must be ready — this is the chance to win a trophy and nothing else matters tomorrow." — Pep Guardiola

Road to the Final

City's path to Wembley has been emphatic. An Erling Haaland hat-trick, supplemented by an Antoine Semenyo finish, produced a stunning 4-0 quarter-final demolition of Liverpool at the Etihad. In the semi-finals, goals from Jeremy Doku and Nico O'Reilly sealed a dramatic late comeback over Southampton to book City's place at Wembley.

Chelsea, meanwhile, edged past Leeds United 1-0 in their semi-final, with Enzo Fernández netting the only goal of the game to send the Blues through to the final.

Seeking Cup Redemption

City make history just by taking to the Wembley pitch — the first club ever to appear in four consecutive FA Cup Finals. They lifted the trophy in 2023 against Manchester United, before suffering back-to-back final defeats to United in 2024 and Crystal Palace in 2025. Guardiola's men are desperate to reclaim the cup and erase those painful recent memories.

After Tuesday's authoritative 3-0 Premier League win over Crystal Palace — in which Semenyo, Savinho, and Marmoush all netted — Guardiola is expected to revert to his strongest lineup, with Haaland, Doku, and Rodri all returning to the starting eleven.

City's Record Against Chelsea

The head-to-head record offers significant encouragement. Manchester City are unbeaten in their last 13 meetings with Chelsea across all competitions, a run stretching back to a 1-0 defeat in the 2021 Champions League final. City beat Chelsea 3-0 at Stamford Bridge in the Premier League in March, and The Opta supercomputer makes Guardiola's side clear favourites, winning in 57.6% of pre-match simulations.

What City Need

For Guardiola, Saturday's final arrives at a critical juncture. City sit two points behind Premier League leaders Arsenal with two league games remaining — away at Bournemouth on 19 May and at home to Aston Villa on 24 May. A Wembley triumph would galvanise the squad and pile enormous pressure on Arsenal heading into the final weekend of the season.

Haaland is expected to lead the line — 26 Premier League goals and 37 across all competitions — with Bernardo Silva captaining the side from midfield. Marc Guehi, dominant since his January arrival from Crystal Palace, is set to anchor the defence alongside Ruben Dias, with Abdukodir Khusanov also confirmed fit.

One thing is certain: City have the quality, the experience, and the motivation to lift the FA Cup for the second time in three years. Wembley awaits.
