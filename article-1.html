Savinho to Spurs: Basic Agreement Reached as £60m Fee All But Sealed — Personal Terms Already Signed

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 10 July 2026

The Savinho saga has entered its final chapter. Caught Offside report that Manchester City and Tottenham Hotspur have reached a basic agreement for the Brazilian winger's transfer, with a fee of approximately £60m all but sealed. Fabrizio Romano had already confirmed Savinho has agreed personal terms with Spurs on a contract until 2031, and that the player has "said yes" to the move. No official announcement has been made by either club as of 10 July 2026.

From "Progressing" to "Basic Agreement"

The language around the Savinho deal has shifted materially in recent days. As recently as 8 July, Romano described negotiations as "progressing well" with the deal remaining "on." By the end of the week, Caught Offside were reporting a basic agreement in place between the two clubs — the critical step that separates ongoing talks from a transfer that is substantively done pending formalities.

GiveMeSport, citing their Tottenham sources, confirmed that personal terms between Savinho and Spurs are agreed: a five-year contract running to June 2031. Caught Offside added in an earlier report that City had given the "green light" for the winger to leave, ending weeks of speculation about whether Enzo Maresca's incoming regime would sanction the sale.

ESPN reported that Tottenham are "in talks with Man City" for a deal in the region of £60m. Sky Sports confirmed that City and Spurs are in "active negotiations." The convergence of detail across multiple outlets — fee, personal terms, green light, "basic agreement" — leaves little doubt about the direction of travel.

City's Profit on the Deal

Manchester City purchased Savinho from CFG sister club Troyes in July 2024 for approximately £30.8m. A sale at £60m would represent a near-double return on that investment inside two years — reflecting the appreciation in the 20-year-old's market value following two seasons in the Premier League, even if his form in 2025/26 was inconsistent under Pep Guardiola's final campaign.

For City's sporting director Hugo Viana, who inherited a squad review from the moment Guardiola's departure was confirmed, the Savinho sale provides significant reinvestment capital. The club has already committed £116m to Elliot Anderson; further reinforcement is expected before the season opener against Bournemouth on 23 August.

What Savinho Brings to Tottenham

At 20, Savinho is among the most exciting raw wingers in the Premier League. His direct dribbling, quick feet in tight spaces, and ability to commit defenders made him one of City's most dynamic attacking outlets despite the team's overall turbulence in 2025/26. Spurs are acquiring a player whose ceiling remains high even if City's new head coach, Enzo Maresca, decided he did not fit the system being built at the Etihad.

Savinho previously signalled interest in a Tottenham move last summer before City elected to keep him. The fact that personal terms were agreed quickly this window — and that Spurs sources were described as "optimistic" throughout negotiations — suggests his desire to make the move was consistent and genuine.

Pending Formalities

A "basic agreement" between clubs is not the same as a completed transfer. The remaining steps typically involve finalising add-ons, medical examinations, work permit paperwork, and the formal announcement from both clubs. These can be completed within days when there is genuine will on all sides — which all reporting suggests is the case here.

For City fans, the departure of another Guardiola-era forward is bittersweet. Savinho arrived with enormous promise and showed glimpses of what he can do. But with Enzo Maresca drawing up a new attacking blueprint and significant funds available from outgoings — Bernardo Silva (free to Real Madrid), John Stones (free), Savinho, and potentially further sales — the summer rebuild is in motion.

An official announcement is expected imminently. When it comes, it will confirm one of the most advanced transfer stories of the summer window without a formal confirmation date.
