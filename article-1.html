City's Asia Tour Confirmed: Inter Milan, K League All-Stars and Atlético in Three Pre-Season Tests

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 4 July 2026

Manchester City have officially confirmed their full 2026 pre-season schedule, which takes in three matches across Asia before the competitive season gets under way. Enzo Maresca's side will face Inter Milan in Hong Kong on Saturday 1 August, K League All-Stars in Seoul on Wednesday 5 August, and Atlético Madrid in Seoul on Sunday 9 August — all confirmed on mancity.com.

Fixture 1: Inter Milan — Sat 1 Aug, Kai Tak Stadium, Hong Kong (12:30 BST)

City's first pre-season match of the Maresca era takes place at the brand-new Kai Tak Stadium in Hong Kong as part of the Hong Kong Football Festival. The fixture, which kicks off at 12:30 BST on Saturday 1 August, sees City take on Serie A title holders Inter Milan — whose €15m mandatory purchase of Manuel Akanji was activated by Inter in May, making the reunion between club and former defender an added subplot.

This will be Enzo Maresca's first competitive appearance as Manchester City head coach, a significant milestone after being appointed on a three-year deal on 26 May 2026. The Kai Tak Stadium is one of the most modern football venues in Asia; the match forms part of a wider Hong Kong Football Festival designed to grow the game across the region.

Fixture 2: K League All-Stars — Wed 5 Aug, Seoul World Cup Stadium (13:00 BST)

Four days later, City travel to Seoul for the first of two matches as part of the Coupang Play Series. The K League All-Stars fixture on Wednesday 5 August — kickoff 13:00 BST — pits City against an assembled best-of-the-best from South Korea's top flight. City have a significant following in East Asia, and the Seoul double-header represents a major commercial and sporting investment in the region under City Football Group's direction.

Fixture 3: Atlético Madrid — Sun 9 Aug, Seoul World Cup Stadium (13:00 BST)

The Seoul leg concludes with a match against Atlético Madrid on Sunday 9 August (13:00 BST). Diego Simeone's side have also been linked with City midfielder Tijjani Reijnders this summer, which could add a layer of intrigue to the fixture. It will serve as City's final run-out before the Community Shield on 16 August.

The Bigger Picture: Who Will Be Available?

The Asia tour timetable has a significant question mark hanging over it: how many of City's World Cup players will be back? As of 4 July, both Erling Haaland (Norway, vs Brazil on 5 July) and Elliot Anderson (England, vs Mexico on 6 July) remain active in the tournament. Even if both countries are eliminated this weekend, standard rest periods for players reaching the Round of 16 typically run to 2–3 weeks — meaning neither is guaranteed to feature in Hong Kong on 1 August.

Other City internationals — including Rodri (Spain), Phil Foden (England), Jeremy Doku (Belgium), and Omar Marmoush (Egypt) — have already been eliminated and should be available from the start of preparations. Maresca will have a good portion of his squad available from the beginning of the Asia tour even if Haaland and Anderson need extra recovery time.

City will also travel without Savinho, whose exit to Tottenham is widely expected to be completed before pre-season begins, should the deal cross the line. And Mathys Detourbet — the summer signing from Troyes — heads to Monaco on loan, so he will not feature.

Then: Community Shield and Premier League Opener

After the Asian double-header, the competitive season begins with the FA Community Shield against Arsenal at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August, before the Premier League kicks off at home to Bournemouth on 23 August (14:00 BST). Maresca will have just one week between the final pre-season fixture (Atlético, 9 Aug) and the Community Shield — a tight turnaround that puts a premium on the squad returning from the World Cup as soon as possible.

All three Asia tour matches will be available to watch live on City+ (the club's official streaming service).
