Romano: Savinho to Spurs 'Deal is Absolutely On' — City Hunt Winger Replacement as Rodri Undergoes Surgery

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 28 July 2026

Manchester City face a dramatic few days in the transfer window. Fabrizio Romano reported on 27 July 2026 that the Savinho deal to Tottenham Hotspur is "absolutely on" at approximately £60–65m — reversing earlier reports the move had collapsed. Hours later, Rodri underwent his scheduled back surgery in Madrid as his uncertain future at the Etihad entered a new chapter.

Savinho to Tottenham: Romano Says 'Absolutely On'

The Savinho saga took a decisive turn on Sunday 27 July when Fabrizio Romano — widely regarded as the most reliable transfer insider in European football — updated followers that the deal between Tottenham Hotspur and Manchester City is "absolutely on". City and Spurs have reached an agreement in principle on a fee of approximately £60–65m, and Savinho has agreed personal terms with the north London club.

This represents a significant reversal of the picture as it appeared in mid-July. Around 10–15 July, multiple outlets — including Goal.com and SI.com — had reported that the sale collapsed after City refused to sanction Savinho's departure without a replacement winger secured. That reporting appeared to indicate the Brazilian was staying. Romano's intervention on 27 July makes clear the situation has evolved: the deal is live and, importantly, the hold-up is not about Savinho or Spurs — it is about Manchester City identifying and signing their own winger replacement before giving the green light.

Savinho holds a contract at the Etihad until 2031. City signed him permanently from Girona in the summer of 2024, and he has been one of the squad's most direct and exciting players on the right flank. The expected fee of £60–65m would represent a meaningful profit on the original outlay.

What City Need Before the Announcement

The hold-up, per Romano and supporting reports from Spurs Web, is City's insistence that a winger replacement be identified before they sanction the sale. This is both a footballing and a strategic calculation: losing Savinho without an immediate replacement would leave Enzo Maresca with Jeremy Doku, Jeremy Monga and Rayan Cherki covering wide positions — a significantly thinner bench than Guardiola had in his final seasons.

Whether the pending Ayyoub Bouaddi signing (medical reportedly imminent per David Ornstein) counts as the replacement, or whether City seek a more direct wide attacker, remains unclear. Malo Gusto from Chelsea is also in discussions as a right-back, though the fee gap there is significant. The picture is expected to clear within the next few days.

Tottenham have been patient throughout. The deal is understood to be waiting on City's timetable rather than any unresolved issue on the Spurs side.

Rodri Undergoes Back Surgery in Madrid

As the Savinho story broke, Rodri underwent his scheduled back surgery in Madrid on Monday 28 July — the date confirmed by head coach Enzo Maresca at his press conference on 24 July. The procedure addresses a lumbar issue Rodri had managed throughout Spain's World Cup campaign — a campaign that ended with him lifting the trophy and winning the Golden Ball as the tournament's outstanding player.

Spanish reports characterise the surgery as an outpatient procedure, with an estimated recovery of approximately four to six weeks. If that timeline holds, Rodri could return to full training at Carrington in September — after the Community Shield (16 August), the Premier League opener at home to Bournemouth (23 August), and Crystal Palace away (29 August).

However, the surgery does not simplify the Real Madrid situation — it may have complicated it. Sky Sports reported on 27 July that Rodri is "pushing for a Real Madrid move" despite the surgery, with sources suggesting he has privately signalled his desire to return to Spain. Real Madrid, for their part, are said to have "hatched a plan" to complete a blockbuster transfer, per Sports Illustrated. Maresca has publicly stated he is planning with Rodri for next season and that no official approach has been received — but the trajectory of the story is increasingly hard to dismiss as pure noise.

City's Squad Entering a Critical Week

In the space of a few days, Manchester City have gone from uncertainty over whether Rodri would stay to uncertainty over whether Savinho would stay — while simultaneously pursuing Bouaddi from Lille, managing the Trafford-to-Leeds situation (a deal reported at around £45m, expected soon), and having travelled to Hong Kong for the Asia Tour.

Enzo Maresca faces Inter Milan on Saturday 1 August in his first game as City head coach. He will do so without Rodri, Haaland, Elliot Anderson, Foden, Guehi, O'Reilly, Cherki, and Trafford — all on mandatory post-World Cup breaks. Kovacic, Gvardiol, Reijnders, Ait-Nouri, Semenyo and Khusanov are expected to form the spine of the touring group. Sources suggest a "stronger-than-expected" squad has travelled.

By the time the Community Shield arrives in Cardiff on 16 August, the Savinho and Rodri situations may well both have resolved — in whichever direction the window takes them.
