City Submit Bid for Marseille's Rulli as Trafford's Leeds Move Edges Closer

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 5 August 2026

Manchester City have submitted an official bid to Olympique de Marseille for Argentine goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli, with personal terms on a two-year contract already agreed, per ESPN, Yahoo Sports and TeamTalk. The move is directly linked to James Trafford's expected departure to Leeds United — a club-record £40m deal that is itself expected to be confirmed imminently.

Rulli Returns — Sort Of

The 34-year-old Rulli has an unusual prior connection to City: he was signed from Deportivo Maldonado in 2016 after impressive loan form at Real Sociedad, only to remain on further loans and never make a senior appearance for the club before his permanent departure. A decade later, Manchester City are targeting him again — this time as experienced backup to Gianluigi Donnarumma under Enzo Maresca.

Rulli has spent the past two seasons at Olympique de Marseille, cementing himself as one of Ligue 1's more reliable goalkeepers. He carries eight Argentina caps, Champions League experience and over a decade at Europe's top level. Maresca, who prizes experienced squad depth, is understood to view Rulli as the pragmatic, low-risk option to fill the void that Trafford's departure will create.

The Fee

City's opening bid stands at approximately €2 million, with Marseille seeking closer to €3.5 million. The gap is small, and given that Rulli's personal terms are already locked in — a two-year deal at the Etihad — only a club-to-club agreement remains. An announcement is expected once the Trafford situation is formally resolved. No official statement from either Manchester City or Olympique de Marseille has been made as of 5 August 2026.

Trafford's Departure: Where Things Stand

The backdrop to City's Rulli pursuit is Trafford's increasingly imminent exit. Fabrizio Romano issued his "here we go" confirmation for the Trafford-to-Leeds deal in late July, with the package worth approximately £40 million initial plus £5 million in add-ons — a club record for Leeds United. The delay since then has been on Leeds' side: the club needed to move on current first-choice goalkeeper Lucas Perri before completing the announcement.

According to Read Man City (4 August 2026), Leeds have now cleared the way — Perri is understood to be undergoing a medical at Torino, which should open the door to Trafford's formal confirmation at Elland Road. Trafford has given his personal approval for the move and has been ready to sign for some time. His FIFA-mandated three-week post-World Cup break is understood to end this Friday, 8 August — removing the final regulatory barrier to the deal being processed. No official mancity.com announcement has been made as of 5 August 2026.

The 2026/27 Goalkeeping Picture

If both moves complete as expected, City's goalkeeping unit for the Maresca era will be: Gianluigi Donnarumma as undisputed number one; Gerónimo Rulli as experienced backup; and Marcus Bettinelli continuing as third choice and dressing-room presence. Pierce Charles, the £10 million summer signing from Sheffield Wednesday, is on a season-long loan at QPR and represents the long-term development option in the position.

It is a considered, low-cost approach to the second-choice role — pragmatic rather than ambitious. With Donnarumma commanding the number one spot, heavy investment elsewhere in the position was never necessary, and Rulli's depth of experience makes him a meaningful upgrade on an untested backup option.
