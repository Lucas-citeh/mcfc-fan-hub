Foden Signs New Deal: Phil Commits to City Until 2030

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 20 May 2026

Phil Foden will remain a Manchester City player until at least the summer of 2030. The England midfielder has agreed a new four-year contract at the Etihad Stadium, with an option to extend for a further 12 months, according to David Ornstein of The Athletic.

The deal puts an end to any speculation over the 25-year-old's future. Foden's previous contract was due to expire in 2027, meaning this new agreement — negotiated through his agent Rafaela Pimenta, who also represents Erling Haaland — represents a significant long-term commitment from both player and club.

Continuity Through a New Era

The timing of the signing is significant. With Pep Guardiola set to depart after Sunday's final-day fixture against Aston Villa, and Enzo Maresca confirmed as his successor in principle, City's hierarchy have moved quickly to tie down their most homegrown talent through the transition period.

Foden has been one of the standout performers of what has been an emotionally mixed 2025/26 season — scoring seven Premier League goals and contributing nine assists, with ten goals across all competitions. His backheel assist for Semenyo's winner against Crystal Palace on 13 May was a moment of typical brilliance.

A Blue Since Birth

Born in Stockport, Foden joined the City academy at the age of eight and made his first-team debut under Guardiola in 2017. He was a central figure in the 2022-23 treble-winning campaign and won the PFA Players' Player of the Year award in the following season. Staying at City through the new era under Maresca feels not just commercially logical, but personally fitting for a player who has never wanted to play anywhere else.

What It Means for the Squad

The Foden extension is welcome news for an Etihad rebuild that will also need to account for the departures of Bernardo Silva and John Stones, who both leave as free agents this summer. It sends a clear message: whatever changes are coming, Foden is the heartbeat of what Manchester City will be next.

City face Aston Villa at the Etihad on Sunday 24 May in their final Premier League fixture of the season — Guardiola's farewell and the start of what comes next.
