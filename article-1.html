Blue Moon in the Streets: City Celebrate Double and Bid Farewell to Pep in Parade

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 25 May 2026

Manchester City took to the streets of their home city on Monday 25 May to celebrate the most emotionally loaded season in recent memory — an FA Cup and EFL Cup double, the Women's Super League title, and a farewell to the greatest manager the club has ever had.

Tens of thousands of supporters lined the route as the open-top bus parade set off from Manchester's Northern Quarter at 5pm, winding through Ancoats before finishing at the Etihad Stadium. The men's, women's, and academy squads all joined the procession, making it one of the most expansive celebrations in the club's history.

A Season to Remember

For the men's first team, the trophies speak for themselves. City lifted the EFL Cup in February and capped the campaign with a 1-0 FA Cup Final victory over Chelsea at Wembley on 16 May — Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute back-heel the moment that sealed a domestic double. In the Premier League, they accumulated 78 points and finished second, four points behind champions Arsenal.

The women's team had even more cause for celebration. City were crowned Women's Super League champions for the first time in years, with the two squads sharing the same parade for a fitting joint tribute. The academy added their own chapter, winning the FA Youth Cup for a fifth time while also reaching the Premier League 2 play-off semi-finals and the Under-18s national final.

Goodbye, Pep

For most of the crowd, though, the afternoon was about one man. Pep Guardiola — who confirmed on 22 May that he would step down as manager after ten years, 429 matches, and 20 major trophies — was on the bus to receive an outpouring of gratitude that is impossible to put into words. The newly expanded North Stand at the Etihad, which now bears his name, loomed in the background as the parade concluded.

The farewell extended to Bernardo Silva and John Stones, both of whom depart as free agents this summer after ten years of extraordinary service. Between them they won six Premier League titles, the Champions League, and multiple domestic cups. Stones played all 90 minutes of the Villa finale before a guard-of-honour substitution; Bernardo captained the side throughout his time on the pitch before the same treatment at the 59th minute.

After Party at Co-op Live

The celebrations continued into the evening at Co-op Live — Manchester's newest indoor arena — where the club held 'The After Party', an event for fans and families to mark the close of the season together. The arena had extended its last entry time for the occasion.

The parade was free to attend and required no ticket. Hundreds of thousands are expected to have turned out across the route — a city united in blue, looking back on a decade of glory and forward to whatever Enzo Maresca has planned next.
