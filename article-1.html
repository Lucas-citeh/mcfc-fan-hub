City Beat Palace 3-0 to Keep Title Hopes Alive

By MCFC Fan Hub 13 May 2026

Manchester City moved within two points of Premier League leaders Arsenal with a commanding 3-0 victory over Crystal Palace at the Etihad Stadium.

Antoine Semenyo, Savinho, and Omar Marmoush all found the net as City delivered a dominant performance to keep the pressure on Arsenal heading into the final weeks of the season.

The victory follows an impressive 3-0 win over Brentford earlier in May, where Jeremy Doku opened the scoring with a curling effort before Erling Haaland doubled the lead with a brilliant backheel finish, and Marmoush completed the scoring.

Starting Lineup

Pep Guardiola made some notable changes for the Palace clash. Donnarumma started in goal behind a back four of Khusanov, Ait-Nouri, Gvardiol, and Guehi. Bernardo Silva captained the side alongside Matheus Nunes in midfield, with Semenyo, Savinho, Foden, and Marmoush providing the attacking threat.

"We can only focus on winning our games. What happens with Arsenal is not in our control. We must be ready for everything." - Pep Guardiola

Title Race

With Arsenal sitting on 79 points and City on 74, every remaining match is crucial. City face Chelsea in the FA Cup Final on Friday before crucial league games against Bournemouth and Aston Villa.

New signing Marc Guehi has formed an impressive partnership at the back since his January arrival from Crystal Palace, while January 2025 additions Omar Marmoush and Abdukodir Khusanov continue to prove their worth in their second half-season at the club.
