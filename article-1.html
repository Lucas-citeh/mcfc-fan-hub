Semenyo Back-Heel Wins City the FA Cup at Wembley

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 16 May 2026

Manchester City are FA Cup winners again. A 72nd-minute moment of brilliance from Antoine Semenyo settled a tense Wembley final 1-0 against Chelsea and completed the domestic double for Pep Guardiola's side.

City had to be patient. Chelsea pressed high in the first half and the game was tight at the break, but the introduction of Rayan Cherki for Omar Marmoush at half-time shifted the balance. Once Mateo Kovacic replaced Rodri just past the hour, City began to control possession through midfield.

The Goal

The breakthrough came in the 72nd minute and it was a thing of beauty. Erling Haaland traded passes with Bernardo Silva on the right before clipping a first-time cutback across the face of goal. With his back to goal, Semenyo improvised with a delicate back-heel that wrong-footed Robert Sanchez and rolled into the far corner. A finish worthy of a final.

Domestic Double

With the EFL Cup already in the trophy cabinet from earlier in the season, the FA Cup win means City have completed the domestic double. Only a handful of clubs in English football history have managed both cups in the same season.

It also rounds off Bernardo Silva's Manchester City career. The captain, who has officially confirmed his summer departure as a free agent, lifted the trophy at Wembley after 451 appearances for the club. John Stones, who has also announced his exit, watched on from the bench.

Eyes on the Title

The celebrations will be tempered by the Premier League run-in. City sit two points behind Arsenal with two games left — away at Bournemouth on Tuesday and at home to Aston Villa on the final day. The Wembley win provides momentum at the perfect moment, but the title is not in City's hands.

For tonight, though, it's about lifting another trophy. Bring on the bus parade.
