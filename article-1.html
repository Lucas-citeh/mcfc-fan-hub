City's 2026/27 Away Kit Launched: Black, Gold and the Worker Bee Unveiled at Hong Kong's Victoria Harbour

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 31 July 2026

Manchester City and PUMA gave the 2026/27 away kit its global launch on Friday 31 July — and they did it in style. More than 1,500 City fans gathered at a unique floating pitch on Hong Kong's Victoria Harbour to see the new shirt unveiled, with Phil Foden, Abdukodir Khusanov and Ryan McAidoo stepping out to model the new design ahead of Saturday's pre-season opener against Inter Milan at Kai Tak Stadium.

The Design: Black, Gold and the Worker Bee

The new away shirt combines a black base with striking gold accents across the collar, underarm stripes and sleeve cuffs. The standout feature is an all-over embossed pattern of worker bees — one of Manchester's most recognisable cultural symbols — woven into the fabric in a subtle raised texture. The pale gold tone echoes City's beloved 1989/90 third kit, giving the design a contemporary look with a nod to the club's heritage.

PUMA have described the kit as "a tribute to the city of Manchester." The worker bee motif, which first appeared on Manchester's coat of arms in the 19th century as a symbol of industry and hard work, has never been more prominent on a City shirt than this. Proceeds from special bee-print add-ons sold at the Stadium Store launch event were donated to the We Love MCR Charity.

The Hong Kong Launch Event

The unveiling took place at a bespoke floating pitch installed on Victoria Harbour — a striking backdrop that gave the launch an atmosphere unlike any kit reveal City have staged before. Phil Foden, who signed a new four-year deal until 2030 in May, was among the key figures on stage, alongside centre-back Abdukodir Khusanov and teenage winger Ryan McAidoo. The players were greeted by an enthusiastic crowd gathered around the harbour's edge, with Hong Kong's skyline behind them.

The event preceded the club's first competitive engagement of the Asia Tour. City face Inter Milan on Saturday 1 August at the newly opened Kai Tak Stadium — Enzo Maresca's first game as Manchester City head coach. It is a fitting setting: the same city that hosted the kit reveal will also witness Maresca's managerial debut.

Pre-Season Tour Context

The Hong Kong Football Festival runs from 1–5 August at Kai Tak Stadium, with Manchester City, Inter Milan, Chelsea and Juventus all participating. After facing Inter on Saturday, City continue their Asia Tour with a trip to Seoul — first against the K League All-Stars on Wednesday 5 August, then against Atlético Madrid on Sunday 9 August — before returning to prepare for the Community Shield against Arsenal in Cardiff on 16 August.

Maresca's touring squad includes Kovacic, Gvardiol, Reijnders, Ait-Nouri, Semenyo and Khusanov. Vitor Reis, who has returned from his successful 31-appearance La Liga loan at Girona, is integrating into Maresca's pre-season group. Players with World Cup commitments — including Haaland, Elliot Anderson, Foden, Guehi and O'Reilly — are joining the squad on a staggered schedule.

On Sale Now

The 2026/27 away kit is available to purchase globally from Friday 31 July, from mancity.com/shop and Manchester City's Stadium Store, Arndale Centre and Trafford Centre outlets. It joins the 2026/27 sky blue home kit as part of City's new-era PUMA range for the Enzo Maresca season.
