Reijnders Heading to Saudi Arabia as Bouaddi Move Nears and Maresca Reveals Rodri Update

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 15 August 2026

Less than 24 hours before Enzo Maresca's first competitive game as Manchester City head coach, the summer transfer window is still rewriting itself. Tijjani Reijnders is set to leave for Al Qadsiah in the Saudi Pro League for €61 million — confirmed by Fabrizio Romano with his trademark "here we go" on 13 August and corroborated by ESPN. The Dutchman's departure frees a midfield slot that City intend to fill with Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille, a deal that all indications suggest is inching toward completion. Meanwhile, Enzo Maresca addressed Rodri's status at a press conference on 14 August — confirming that the Ballon d'Or winner is present at the CFA in rehabilitation — while the pursuit of Enzo Fernandez from Chelsea collapsed after City missed a 5pm deadline.

Reijnders: 'Here We Go' for Saudi Arabia

The Tijjani Reijnders saga concluded with a swiftness that surprised most observers. Through July and into the first week of August, Manchester City had rebuffed interest from Newcastle and other clubs, with Maresca's staff understood to consider the Dutchman part of their 2026/27 plans. By 13 August, the picture had changed entirely.

Fabrizio Romano posted his signature confirmation: Reijnders says 'yes' to Al Qadsiah, Manchester City have agreed to receive €61 million — a fee representing a £14–15 million profit on what City paid AC Milan for him in June 2025. The clubs were exchanging documents ahead of formal signatures. Romano noted that Reijnders had not reported for training as he was preparing to fly to Saudi Arabia. ESPN followed with its own report confirming "Reijnders exits Manchester City for £52M Saudi move," while beIN Sports also reported the move was heading towards completion on 14 August.

Reijnders made 47 appearances in his debut City campaign, scoring seven goals. He was a reliable contributor but never fully nailed down a starting berth in Guardiola's final season, with Rodri ahead of him in the holding role and a crowded midfield. Maresca's arrival, combined with the £116m signing of Elliot Anderson from Nottingham Forest, effectively made Reijnders surplus. The Al Qadsiah offer — the Saudi club is managed by Brendan Rodgers — gave both club and player a clean exit at maximum value.

No official announcement has been received from mancity.com as of 15 August 2026. Per the pattern of this summer window, formal club confirmation is expected to follow imminently. Sources: Fabrizio Romano (13 Aug 2026); ESPN ("Sources: Reijnders exits Manchester City for £52M Saudi move" — 14 Aug 2026); beIN Sports ("Reijnders heading towards Man City exit" — 14 Aug 2026).

Bouaddi: Reijnders Exit Paves the Way

The significance of Reijnders' departure extends beyond the fee received. Goal.com published a piece headlined "Paving the way for Bouaddi: City agree to their player's move to Al-Qadsiah" — drawing a direct line between the Reijnders exit and City's pursuit of Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille.

Bouaddi, 18, has had personal terms agreed with City for weeks — a contract running to 2031 per multiple reports. The outstanding issue has been the fee: Lille's asking price is in the region of €100 million, while City's best offer has been approximately €85–90 million plus add-ons. A further sticking point has been whether Bouaddi returns to Lille on loan for 2026/27 or joins Manchester immediately. Bouaddi himself prefers the latter. Fabrizio Romano has described City as "on the verge" of completing the deal and Goal.com declared it "imminent" on 11 August.

With a midfield slot now opening and the directional logic of the Reijnders exit pointing clearly at the Moroccan international, the expectation across all credible transfer sources is that the Bouaddi deal will complete before the summer window closes. No official mancity.com or Lille announcement has been received as of 15 August 2026.

Maresca on Rodri: 'I Gave Him a Big Hug'

Perhaps the most intriguing development of Friday came at Enzo Maresca's pre-match press conference on 14 August. Asked about Rodri's status, Maresca offered an answer that surprised those expecting final confirmation of the long-rumoured Barcelona transfer: the Ballon d'Or winner is at the Etihad Campus.

"I've just seen Rodri now in the building. I gave him a big hug. He looks good. So, we'll see what happens," Maresca said, per Yahoo Sports and mancity.com. The City head coach confirmed that Rodri is in rehabilitation following the back surgery he underwent in Madrid on 28 July, and that his own focus is fully on Sunday's match against Arsenal.

What the quote does not resolve — and what Maresca deliberately declined to resolve — is whether Rodri will ultimately depart for FC Barcelona. A club-to-club fee agreement has been widely reported since 9 August at €50–60 million, with Rodri expected to sign a four-year deal. Multiple outlets continue to describe the deal as being in its "final stages." But Maresca's description of Rodri as present, healthy and in the building illustrates the ambiguity that has defined this saga all summer. No official departure announcement from mancity.com or FC Barcelona has been received as of 15 August 2026. Sources: mancity.com ("Maresca fitness update on McAidoo, Haaland and Rodri" — 14 Aug 2026); Yahoo Sports ("Maresca gives Man City fitness update on Haaland and Rodri" — 14 Aug 2026).

Enzo Fernandez Pursuit Collapses

One thread that can be fully put to rest is Manchester City's reported interest in Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernandez. Chelsea had set a firm deadline — 5pm on 14 August — for City to submit a formal bid of £120 million or abandon their pursuit. City did not meet the deadline. Per Sky Sports, Fernandez will remain at Stamford Bridge for the 2026/27 season. The pursuit is over.

The Fernandez saga was notable more for its optics than for any near-miss. City reportedly had personal terms agreed with the Argentina international, but Chelsea's valuation was non-negotiable and City declined to match it. With Bouaddi expected to arrive as the long-term Rodri successor and Anderson already in the door, the failed pursuit appears to be a case of City exploring the market rather than a serious attempt at completion. Sources: Sky Sports ("Enzo Fernandez transfer news: Man City miss Chelsea's deadline to bid for midfielder" — 14 Aug 2026).

Community Shield: 24 Hours to Go

Against all this transfer noise, Enzo Maresca is preparing for the most important day of his City career so far: the 2026 FA Community Shield against Arsenal at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Sunday 16 August, kicking off at 15:00 BST.

The squad available to Maresca is near full-strength. Haaland, who returned from his post-World Cup break on 12 August, is expected to be in contention even if his limited pre-season minutes raise questions about whether he starts. Doku, Guehi, Anderson, O'Reilly and Cherki all returned on the same day. The Asia tour players — Donnarumma, Lewis, Khusanov, Gvardiol, Ait-Nouri, Matheus Nunes, Kovacic, Foden, Savinho, Semenyo and Marmoush — have had additional training time. The central question is how Maresca arrays his midfield without Rodri. Anderson, Kovacic and either Foden or Cherki appear to be the most likely combination in the engine room.

Arsenal arrive missing Saliba and Timber. City arrive as FA Cup holders. Cardiff awaits.
