The Pep Guardiola Stand: City Honour Departing Manager with Stadium Renaming and Statue

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 22 May 2026

Manchester City have announced that the newly-expanded North Stand at the Etihad Stadium will be renamed 'The Pep Guardiola Stand'. A statue of the departing manager will also be commissioned, to be placed on the approach to the stand — a permanent tribute to a coach who transformed the club and the English game.

The decision was announced by club owner Sheikh Mansour in recognition of Guardiola's ten historic years as Manchester City manager. The expanded North Stand, which adds over 7,000 seats and brings the Etihad's total capacity to more than 61,000, will open for the first time at Sunday's season finale against Aston Villa — Guardiola's last game in charge.

A Physical Legacy Built Into the Etihad

The renaming places Guardiola among a small group of football figures honoured with a stand named in their memory during their own lifetime. At City, it represents the highest institutional tribute the club can bestow — a permanent, physical marker of what his decade has meant not just to the football club but to the city of Manchester itself.

The statue commission will take time to complete and will stand on the exterior approach to The Pep Guardiola Stand, joining the existing statues of club legends around the Etihad. No further details of the sculptor or timeline have been released, but the club confirmed it will be unveiled at a ceremony in the coming seasons.

Capacity Rises to Over 61,000

The expanded stand also marks a major infrastructure milestone. Adding more than 7,000 seats brings the Etihad's capacity to over 61,000 — one of the largest dedicated club football venues in England — and is expected to significantly boost matchday revenue for the seasons ahead under incoming manager Enzo Maresca. The construction had been under way throughout the 2025/26 campaign; fans had watched scaffolding slowly give way to the emerging new upper tier throughout the season.

Ten Years, Fifteen Trophies

Guardiola has managed 428 matches for Manchester City, winning 305 of them. In that time he has delivered six Premier League titles, the 2022-23 Champions League, seven League Cups and two FA Cups — including last Saturday's 1-0 victory over Chelsea at Wembley, secured by Antoine Semenyo's 72nd-minute back-heel. The domestic double this season, his third as City manager, means he departs without a barren run to his name in a decade of management.

The stand naming and statue commission ensure his legacy is literally built into the fabric of the club. On Sunday, for the first time, 61,000 supporters will fill that stand to say goodbye.
