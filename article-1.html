Romano Confirms: Savinho 'Wants Tottenham' as Spurs Close In on £50m Deal

By Blue Moonies Fan Hub 6 June 2026

Fabrizio Romano has explicitly confirmed that Savinho "wants Tottenham" ahead of the Premier League transfer window opening on 15 June, with the feeling reported as entirely mutual from Tottenham's side. Spurs are closing in on a deal worth around £50 million — a reduction on Manchester City's earlier £60 million asking price — as both clubs look to finalise terms before the 2026 World Cup takes over the calendar.

Romano's update from 5–6 June represents the clearest statement yet about the 22-year-old Brazilian's personal preference. While the transfer had been gathering pace since formal talks opened in early June, the explicit reporting that Savinho "wants" the Tottenham move removes any meaningful ambiguity about where negotiations are heading. The sources for this update are Romano directly, with the sentiment described as mutual between player and club.

Romano: 'Wants Tottenham' — The Significance

In Romano's transfer reporting lexicon, the phrase "wants" — applied to a player's desire for a specific destination — sits just below "here we go" as a signal of how close a deal is. Romano had already reported the deal as "on" when formal negotiations opened between the clubs, and that Roberto De Zerbi had given his full personal approval to the signing before club-level talks had even begun. The escalation to Savinho "wanting" the move means both the sporting and personal terms are substantially aligned. All that remains is a formal fee agreement between City and Spurs — and that is well advanced.

Romano has also described the feeling from Tottenham's side as mutual: De Zerbi wants Savinho specifically, the sporting department are aligned, and the player has communicated his preference. In transfer negotiations, that triangulation of player desire, club approval, and managerial backing is what turns a rumour into a deal.

The Fee: £50m

Multiple outlets now report the fee being discussed at approximately £50 million, which is below Manchester City's original valuation of around £60 million but represents where both clubs can land. City's softening on their asking price reflects a practical assessment: Savinho, for all his talent, was not a guaranteed starter in 2025/26 as wide positions became increasingly competitive, his contract has time left but he has communicated his desire to leave, and a clean sale enables City to reinvest in the profile that fits Maresca's system.

For Tottenham, £50 million would still represent a significant outlay — potentially breaking or equalling the club's transfer record — and a demonstration that De Zerbi's backing carries genuine financial weight with the board. The willingness to commit at that level for a wide player who, by City's standards, underperformed his billing tells you something about how specifically De Zerbi identified him as the right fit for his system.

Savinho was acquired by Manchester City from Girona — a sister club in the City Football Group — in the summer of 2024 for approximately €70 million. A sale at £50 million after two seasons represents a financial loss, but one City are willing to absorb to free the player and fund their own recruitment.

The World Cup Complication — and Opportunity

Savinho is expected to travel to North America with Brazil for the 2026 World Cup, which opens on 11 June — just five days away. The window for either club to push through a formal announcement before the tournament is almost closed. What is achievable in the coming days is alignment in principle, which both parties appear to have reached. The formal paperwork and announcement are expected to follow once the window officially opens on 15 June, and potentially after Brazil's run in the tournament comes to an end.

Interestingly, the World Cup may accelerate rather than delay the deal. If Savinho performs strongly for Brazil in front of a global audience, his market value will only increase — creating pressure on Tottenham to secure the agreement quickly. Romano's framing of the move as a matter of "when" rather than "if" suggests both clubs have accepted that logic and are moving accordingly.

City's Winger Search Intensifies

Savinho's anticipated exit leaves a meaningful gap in Manchester City's wide attacking positions. Jeremy Doku and Antoine Semenyo are the primary remaining options — Semenyo having produced a breakout second half of the season with 17 Premier League goals from January onwards, and Doku remaining capable of brilliance but inconsistent with it. For Maresca's system, a direct, technical winger on the right side — Savinho's natural position — is an important piece.

City's primary replacement target remains Yan Diomande, the 19-year-old RB Leipzig winger who won the Bundesliga Rookie of the Year award in 2025/26 with 13 goals and 10 assists. Leipzig's asking price is reported at €100–120 million, which is steep. Liverpool are also in contention and pushing hard for a pre-World Cup agreement. City's ability to move for Diomande is, in part, contingent on receiving the Savinho funds — making the speed of the Spurs deal consequential for their own summer planning.

What Comes Next

The transfer window opens on 15 June. Savinho departs for Brazil duty from around 11 June. In practical terms, City and Spurs are expected to move swiftly in the first days after the window opens to formalise what is already substantially agreed. Romano's track record when reporting a player "wants" a specific club is that an announcement typically follows within days to a few weeks.

For City supporters, the Savinho departure is bittersweet: a player with unquestionable technical gifts who, in the end, never quite found the consistency to be the game-changer that his price tag and profile promised. Under De Zerbi, freed from competition for places and given the platform his game demands, the best version of Savinho — creative, direct, decisive — may yet be seen. Just not in a sky blue shirt.

Sources: Fabrizio Romano (Savinho "wants Tottenham," feeling mutual, 5–6 June 2026); Fabrizio Romano (deal "on," formal talks, 2–4 June 2026); Caught Off Side (formal talks confirmed, 3 June 2026); The Hard Tackle / TEAMtalk (Spurs nearing £50m deal, 6 June 2026).
